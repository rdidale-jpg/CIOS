## 1. Lloyds financial anatomy

**Commercial question:** how does this particular Group turn customer funding, lending, transactional services and asset/insurance activity into sustainable returns?

**Principal story:** a coherent funding → earning activity → income → costs/risk → return composition. Show the distinctions between the Retail, Commercial Banking and IP&I mechanisms, with dated legal/management context; do not build another financial dashboard or imply capability-level allocations.

**Available information:** existing eligible Gold Group stock/result statements and bounded deposit comparison retain their authority. Recovered research adds Retail depreciation/pricing mechanisms, the matched segment arithmetic and IP&I's separation of dividend, deferred-profit stock and release. RIE2 reports segment figures with period/basis; LEI provides 13 reproducible research reconciliations. These held candidates are a content inventory, not permission to render new figures.

**Legitimate relationships:** deposits fund a banking business at an explanatory level; matched income/cost movements can explain a period-specific result. Reporting segments are not legal subsidiaries. IP&I deferred profit and risk adjustment are not cash income; an upstream dividend is not an extra external customer sale. Do not draw quantified flow arrows between capabilities without actual allocation evidence.

**Commercial significance:** differentiate asset/funding/productivity/return levers and management's existing remedies, rather than generic “cost pressure”. Retail other-income/depreciation movement must never be labelled leasing profit or margin. Positive Group outcomes and existing mitigation remain visible alongside a possible issue.

**Material gaps:** [Source/Financial dependencies](Dependencies.md), current segment-to-management mapping, net unit economics, legal/workspace identity, attributable capability costs and any genuinely open customer decision. No global completeness statement.

**Recommended optional depth:** matched period/basis bridge, prior-year comparison, statutory/APM explanation, source/withdrawal status in authorised inspection, and existing qualified Leadership/Material Pressure navigation only where already supported. No invented numerical chart while financial authority remains unresolved.

## 2. Lloyds operating model and AI transformation

**Commercial question:** how do Lloyds' business capabilities, people, process, data and technology support customer outcomes, and what is genuinely changing?

**Principal story:** specific business value streams with enabling platform and control dependencies. Anchor Transaction Banking in CIB, keep BCB/SME distinct, show Scottish Widows protection/claims experimentation in its proper context, and distinguish workplace/engineering AI from Envoy's internal agent platform.

**Available information:** dated role/remit observations; Transaction Banking/CIB and appointment statements; reported Envoy/Google collaboration; Microsoft/GitHub scope; historical Sprout.ai trial; separate FCA testing/Sandbox events; existing Group AI reported contribution and expectation. Current public facts retain their own eligibility. Candidate research and the isolated proof are not silently promoted.

**Legitimate relationships:** direct reported platform collaboration, disclosed role remit and explicitly conceptual capability dependencies can be visualised with different assertion status. Supplier branding is not a verified legal contracting counterparty. Trial, designed control, rollout announcement and measured live outcome are separate states. Do not imply the entire cloud/app estate from one collaboration.

**Commercial significance:** the distinction between platform availability, adoption, process impact and net economic outcome is immediately useful. An existing capability is counter-evidence to a generic new-tool pitch; the meaningful later investigation is incremental, customer-confirmed value and any remaining decision.

**Material gaps:** original-source lifecycle bindings for recovered replay, full workload/service ownership, adoption denominators, realised quality/cost outcomes, cost of change/run/controls, contractual scope and delegated decision rights. No estimated gaps filled with fictional relationships.

**Recommended optional depth:** dated programme/role chronology, capability/process evidence, distinctions between gross/expected/net benefits, supplier-scope limitations and source inspection under existing authority. Investigation questions stay in authorised commercial-development work, not on the ordinary executive canvas.
