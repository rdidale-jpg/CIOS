# Three commercial investigation chains

These are private research/investigation candidates. They are not executive-canvas questions, asserted buying intent, qualified opportunities or Pipeline records. Each preserves a disconfirming route.

## 1. AI industrialisation: from announced platform to incremental net operating value

**Established in retained records:** RIE1 `LBG-ENVOY26-F1`, RIE2 `R2-OBS-ENVOY-DATE` and the existing Factory Envoy statements attribute an internal agent platform and Google Cloud collaboration to the Group. RIE2 `R2-OBS-AI-VALUE` distinguishes prior-year company-reported contribution from a later expectation. Tier `F11A/F11B/F22` separate Microsoft workplace scope and supplier-reported GitHub use. `F26C` is a dated Group COO roster observation; `F09` is a dated Group CRO role announcement, not delegated buying authority.

**Analytical chain:** stated productivity/AI priority → agent lifecycle and service/engineering capability → process integration, data access, controls and operational cost → platform and existing supplier dependencies → operating accountability. Google/Microsoft are reported collaborators in specific scopes; there is no supported claim about an executed incremental contract, exclusivity or displacement.

**Commercial hypothesis:** incremental value could depend more on measured process redesign, deployment discipline and control/run cost than additional licences. This is conditional, not an assertion of a Lloyds weakness or a net savings figure.

**Counter-evidence:** an existing platform, disclosed designed controls, current tool adoption and reported contribution indicate an established capability. The bank may already have the required delivery capacity and measurement. Gross and net methodology may already reconcile internally.

**Next practical action:** seek a permissioned discussion with the relevant operating/AI value-measurement role, not a presumed named buyer. Offer a narrowly scoped diagnostic of one agreed workflow: baseline, changed task/quality outcomes, platform/change/control cost, attribution and duplication of reported benefits. First establish whether any decision remains open and whether an external diagnostic is useful. Stop the commercial thesis if measurement is already adequate or no external scope exists.

**Source/authority:** research candidates; [dependencies](Dependencies.md) apply. The isolated AI proof demonstrates services, not a real customer validation or public-source admission.

## 2. Transaction Banking: commercial growth through a defined operating remit

**Established in retained records:** Factory `commercial_unit/condon` and Tier `F06A/F06B` place Transaction Banking within CIB and attribute a November appointment and remit covering cash, trade, working capital and payments. Tier `F05A/F05B` concern BCB/SME responsibilities and must not be collapsed into that CIB mandate.

**Analytical chain:** commercial customer / working-capital proposition → cash, trade and payment value streams → onboarding, execution, servicing and liquidity/risk capabilities → integration and data dependencies to be verified → announced Transaction Banking leadership remit.

**Commercial hypothesis:** an account discussion could test which customer journey or operating handoff, if any, remains a strategic improvement priority. No actual friction, technology gap or customer demand has been established merely by the appointment.

**Counter-evidence:** appointment may implement a settled plan; platforms, suppliers and internal delivery may already be selected. CIB organisation is not a freshly published addressable procurement opportunity and is not the same perimeter as historical Commercial Banking segment accounts.

**Next practical action:** verify effective remit and current priority through an authorised business contact; ask for one customer journey and the existing outcome metric, present ownership and decision status. Explore a customer-agreed diagnostic only after confirming an unresolved question and permission. Do not lead with a speculative platform replacement or assume budgets. If decisions are closed, retain account intelligence without creating an opportunity.

**Source/authority:** dated attributed replay and research, not delegated buyer authority. The divisional proof keeps the held financial record outside staging.

## 3. Retail asset economics: protect the distinction between growth and return

**Established in retained records:** RIE2 `R2-OBS-RETAIL-LEASE/PRICE/CREDIT-EXPLAIN`, W3 `LBG-3` and LEI reconciliations connect used-car prices, fleet growth, higher-value vehicles and existing mitigation with reported Retail economics. RIE2 financial inputs are period- and APM-specific; the public financial restriction remains unchanged.

**Analytical chain:** asset-based customer proposition → origination/pricing → fleet/asset servicing and remarketing → residual-value exposure and depreciation → matched income/cost/return explanation. Connected Finance's later management grouping does not retrospectively allocate H1 segment accounts to a brand or capability.

**Commercial hypothesis:** the right investigation is whether any incremental asset-return or process improvement remains beyond disclosed mitigation. It is not “Retail is unprofitable”, an inferred systems failure or a quantified sales opportunity.

**Counter-evidence:** existing mitigation, business growth and issuer-described strong/stable credit performance; depreciation timing and fleet mix can explain movements without a supplier problem.

**Next practical action:** after the existing source-use question is resolved for any proposed numerical materials, test a matched-period economic bridge with an authorised asset/product finance contact. Separate volume, mix, residual values, funding, depreciation and credit; confirm what management has already decided. Stop if public evidence cannot support the bridge or there is no customer-confirmed open issue.
