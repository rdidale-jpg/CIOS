# Predeclared challenge protocol

The frozen roster contains five enterprises in each of six sectors. It combines incumbent operators with newer challengers and includes UK and international publishers. None of the six enterprises used in the earlier Factory newsroom and register proofs appear here. Selection used sector, business model variety and availability of a plausible first-party newsroom landing page. No answer articles, events, numbers or outcomes were supplied. The first enterprise in each sector forms the development cohort. The remaining 24 form the held-out coverage cohort. Failures stay in the denominator and the roster is unchanged after execution.

Run the same existing body extraction and assurance path in both cohorts. Permit one official index and up to four linked originals on the exact HTTPS hostname and configured article path for each enterprise. Deny redirects and query-only discovery. Read at most 2 MiB of accepted HTML per response, with the existing 12-second network timeout, six concurrent enterprises and no paid search. A 90-day recency window applies to dated candidate publications. Trials cover commercial strategy/change, technology/AI, relationships, transactions and competitive change/risk. A matched theme is a topical indicator, with scope and meaning still subject to review.

Score source and candidate coverage against all selected enterprises. Measure HTTP and parsing gaps separately, then attributable and withheld outcomes. Authentic readiness requires independently established newsroom labels across sectors and permissions for independently sourced corroboration and eligible projection. This challenge does not assign ground truth from its own result set. No unsupported-verification accuracy or statistical inference follows from a zero verified denominator. Publication and Pipeline mutation remain outside the experiment.
