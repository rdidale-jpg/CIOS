# Evaluation criteria before execution

The controlled assurance suite must produce zero verified outcomes on unsupported, conflicting, stale, syndicated-only, out-of-scope or withdrawn controls. Every deliberately eligible control must pass. Every denied publication case must remain denied. Every withdrawal control must remove eligibility. Repeat execution must preserve the same decision. These are safety checks on a designed set of cases. Their proportions do not estimate field accuracy.

The held-out structured extraction check uses Tesco and National Grid. Compare company number, exact legal name and registry status against the original public register label for each company. Require precision and recall of 6/6 for those six specific values. The companies and structured fields differ from the four previously exercised newsroom enterprises. The original register supplies transcription ground truth for its own fields. A separate, independently reviewed reference corpus is still required to measure newsroom body extraction, event matching, commercial relationship resolution and corroboration in the field.

A production readiness claim requires that external corpus and a separate owner decision on thresholds and operational authority. No confidence interval is reported for the hand-selected controls or two register records.
