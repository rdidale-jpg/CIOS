## Evidence-led research cycle

### 1. Bound the question and entities

Identify industry/geography, enterprise/legal entity, business capability, reporting period and commercial question. Separate group disclosures from regulated subsidiaries. Keep an enterprise's stated objective, reported actual performance, analyst interpretation and unresolved hypothesis as different claim types. An industry-level rule or supplier designation is not an enterprise-specific contract.

### 2. Reconnoitre the industry

Use official statistics, central-bank/regulator publications, legislation and authoritative industry disclosures to populate PESTLE and Five Forces. Record mechanisms rather than adjective-only ratings: driver → affected activity/capability → enterprise exposure → measurable outcome → evidence gap. A conceptual value-chain edge is not a verified operating or contractual relationship. Environmental and competitive concentration assessments stay incomplete until appropriate primary evidence is available.

### 3. Research the enterprise in depth

Prioritise latest annual/interim/results publications and dated strategy disclosures. Then investigate regulatory disclosures, official technology announcements, supplier announcements corroborated by the enterprise, programme milestones and actual procurement notices where available. For each metric preserve unit, denominator, reporting period and legal-entity scope. Record benefit estimates separately from realised net benefit, programme investment and external supplier spend. Never turn an earnings figure or AI benefit aspiration into an addressable contract value.

### 4. Acquire and review sources

`python tools/collect_flora_primary_sources.py` audits the current overlay without network access or writes. Once official-publisher access is available, explicitly use `--collect --output <new-private-scratch-directory>` to download bounded originals/text and record retrieval URL/time and content hashes. Only approved HTTPS publisher hosts and redirects are followed. Collection never updates claims, canonical evidence or pipeline data. Downloaded material is **unreviewed**; a landing page is not proof of a number in a linked report. Failed retrieval is recorded as failure.

A researcher must identify the actual document, publication date, reporting period, exact supporting passage, legal entity and source independence. Preserve the original and extracted-text hashes. A supplier's announcement is not independent confirmation of its customer's commercial demand. Review extraction quality, especially PDF tables and units. Add a reviewed source snapshot to the repository's source-snapshot boundary only when publication rights and relevance permit; do not commit private scratch downloads. The model supports `primary_retrieved` sources only with dated HTTPS retrieval provenance, a snapshot and matching text digest. A claim becomes `source_supported` only with an exact matching primary quotation. This remains candidate intelligence rather than canonical acceptance.

### 5. Preserve facts while updating

`compare_revision(previous, proposed)` produces a read-only proposal retaining the complete prior assessment. Changing an established statement, quotation, entity scope, unit or period under the same ID is rejected. Append a new stable claim ID; explicitly supersede only same-scope/period corrections. A new reporting period adds history rather than erasing the previous period. Corroboration may add support while retaining earlier source references. Review conflicts explicitly. Use the existing governed evidence/memory acceptance path for any later canonical promotion; this overlay does not bypass it.

### 6. Label knowledge relationships

Edges carry subject, predicate, object, supporting claim IDs and assertion kind. Current edges are conceptual, interpretations, repository-reported relationships or unknowns; **none is verified**. A verified future edge requires primary-supported evidence explicitly matching that subject/predicate/object. Industry CTP designation cannot establish a Lloyds AWS/Oracle relationship. Reported Google/Microsoft usage does not prove contract scope, incumbent exclusivity, renewal date or buying access.

### 7. Develop and rank candidates

Each candidate links a specific enterprise problem, capability, driver, programme signal, supporting claims and unanswered customer questions. Ranking orders **research effort**, not probability of winning: explicit analyst research tier, primary-supported claim coverage, enterprise-specific reported claim coverage, stable ID tie-break. The UI exposes the basis. Citation count is not independent corroboration or confidence calibration. Revisit tiers after evidence review; do not infer precision from the ordinal position.

No customer demand is verified in the current assessment. Customer confirmation should establish the actual pain/outcome, accountable buyer, decision process, funding, timing, incumbent/scope constraints and an agreed next step. Existing Pipeline qualification rules remain authoritative; no background capture, stage advancement or qualification occurs.
