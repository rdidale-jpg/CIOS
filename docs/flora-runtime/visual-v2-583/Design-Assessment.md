# Visual design assessment

## Direction

The owner liked the prior illustrations and rejected their surrounding density. This proof keeps the architectural, nationally recognisable editorial craft, but removes persistent Home content modules altogether. One compact product header sits immediately beside FLORA. A short country heading and correctly scoped GVA measure give context; the rest is the commercial world. No bottom navigation, card wall, “More from FLORA”, repeated evidence prompts or competing headline CTAs.

The economy is a coherent twenty-district landscape based on the full domestic statistical taxonomy. It deliberately includes businesses, public services, households and productive infrastructure beyond the three researched sectors. Colour/solid-green action treatment identifies the only supported deeper experiences. Inert sectors remain beautiful parts of the same country instead of a backlog covered in warning badges.

Country total plus five section benchmarks make financial scale visible without twenty permanent number tiles. Shortened section labels remain reconciled to their full official definition. The artwork is conceptual: aesthetic area, building counts and landmark placement do not encode GVA ranking or actual geography. Illustrated landmarks/people/logos are not enterprise identities, counterparties or research evidence. The aircraft's stylised livery is decorative, not a customer/supplier assertion.

PESTLE is a second thematic world with a quiet central water/globe space. Six native, labelled points frame it. A selection reveals one small intelligence note **over that image**, without a second text page or scrolling down to a panel. The country relevance and potential industry question remain distinct from global themes and verified enterprise causation. The source mechanism stays discreet in the header.

## Design tradeoffs and manual acceptance

- Full economy versus legibility: twenty short labels are necessary to represent the taxonomy, but only three green actions and five benchmark figures are present. The full comparison moves to one optional disclosure.
- iPad portrait preserves the whole landscape's geometry and readable labels, with calm space beneath its natural aspect ratio. Desktop/landscape iPad use more of the viewport. Owner should explicitly assess that portrait balance.
- Phone Home folds one artwork into two vertical halves rather than adding a separate grid/list below it. This costs scroll length, but keeps all twenty sectors legible and image-native. The proof does not pretend the panorama fits a 320px thumbnail.
- Phone PESTLE uses a portrait companion, with intentional cover framing at 320px and a central note. All six controls remain unobscured. There are no drag/pan gestures required to discover labels.
- Six factor selections demonstrate the interaction model, not comprehensive fresh PESTLE research. Social is specifically existing procurement social-value intelligence; country law/source cutoffs and limitations are preserved. Economic adds only the cited ONS macro observation, without creating a canonical observation.
- Country context is explicit UK-only. A fake selector or second unresearched national scene would expand this two-screen proof unnecessarily; internationalisation slots/boundaries are documented instead.
- Mandatory safety/storage/auth alerts still render through existing owners. Their absence in a healthy local fixture is not a promise to suppress them in production.

Automated checks exercise responsive layout, native keyboard/touch/no-JS interactions, accessibility-tree names, source disclosure, focus, signed-in/out paths and unchanged canonical Home pixels. They do not certify physical iPad/Safari, VoiceOver/NVDA, high zoom, colour perception, real-world slow networks or production performance. Those are owner/manual checks before acceptance. The illustrations are not approved master assets until the owner accepts them.

**Decision sought: accept or refine these two compositions and their interaction/financial-scope contracts.** No wider screen restyling, canonical Home replacement or release is included. #581's Industry/Enterprise editorial work remains separate.
