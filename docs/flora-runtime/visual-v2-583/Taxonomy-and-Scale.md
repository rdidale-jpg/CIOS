# UK taxonomy, statistics and scene audit

## Chosen compatible basis

The [ONS Blue Book 2026 industrial analysis](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/grossdomesticproductgdp/compendium/unitedkingdomnationalaccountsthebluebook/2026/theindustrialanalysis/pdf), released **30 October 2026**, states UK 2024 total GVA of **£2,623bn** at current basic prices. Its supply-use-balanced series ends in 2024; later annual estimates are less fully balanced. Display: **£2.62tn**, ordinary rounding of that published figure, not an EST, GDP at market prices, revenue or an addressable sales market.

The latest compatible [ONS September 2026 monthly GDP bulletin](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/grossdomesticproductgdp/bulletins/gdpmonthlyestimateuk/september2026/pdf), released **12 November 2026**, supplies **2024 SIC 2007 weights** in its GVA2 appendix (PDF zero-based page 27). We divide each weight by ten to display a percentage. We do **not** use its chained-volume indices as nominal market values, multiply rounded shares into falsely precise sector pounds, normalise rounding away or mix years. The twenty rounded section weights sum to **1,002 per 1,000 = 100.2%**. B–E and G–T are aggregates, excluded from the sum to avoid double counting.

The [ONS classification catalogue](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/ukstandardindustrialclassificationofeconomicactivities) now also lists SIC 2026. This proof deliberately uses **SIC 2007 to match these national-accounts data**, not a mixture of old letters and new classification. [SIC 2007 explanatory notes](https://www.ons.gov.uk/file?uri=%2Fmethodology%2Fclassificationsandstandards%2Fukstandardindustrialclassificationofeconomicactivities%2Fuksic2007%2Fuksic2007web.pdf) define the sections. U covers extraterritorial organisations/bodies and is outside the published domestic A–T GVA denominator. That explicit exclusion is not a claim of zero international activity.

These are activity-based industries across public and private ownership, not institutional sectors: public administration does not absorb separately classified health or education. T does not include all housing or all social care: agency-provided care belongs to its appropriate industry. L includes **imputed owner-occupier rents**: its GVA share is not a property-company revenue opportunity. Tourism, digital/AI, clean technology, defence and life sciences span existing sections and are **not added again as extra additive GVA sectors**. Media/publishing/broadcasting sit within the appropriate J definitions; arts/recreation are R. The Technology view spans multiple activity types and is not statistically equated with J.

The default image shows only **five section shares**: H/J/K for context around the three researched destinations, plus L/G as the two largest section benchmarks in this extract. This is an editorial selection, not an assertion that these are the only material sectors. All twenty figures remain in the optional table and screen-reader text. No unsupported Aviation, Banking or “Technology industry” share is fabricated.
