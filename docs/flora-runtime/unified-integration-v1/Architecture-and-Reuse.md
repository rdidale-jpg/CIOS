## Evidence and security boundaries

Public research reads repository-owned approved evidence only. Existing finite public routes/query policies are unchanged. Public pages cannot enumerate workspace Pipeline records. No source or geographic/seller selector constructs a verified actor, membership, enterprise authority or ACL. Session expiry/revocation, workspace/resource matching, CSRF, qualification and protected transactions remain under existing owners.

Exact Lloyds Bank plc register identity is distinct from formal instrument applicability. UK binding-rule/regulator-register verification remains unresolved. France retains national text and consolidation counterevidence, with exact entity/size/phase-in/transposition Unknown. No French subsidiary, duty, vulnerability, supplier contract, procurement date, budget or customer buying intent is invented. Seller relevance remains reversible URL context, not permission or customer demand.

MacroContextReference remains the #566 deterministic **design/test-only** adapter. Integrating the stacks does not authorise changing immutable saved records or adding a Macro persistence channel. Existing capture freezes its original research assessment and compatible commercial review. Browser Back retains Macro context while crossing established enterprise routes; that Macro context is not represented as saved Pipeline provenance.

## Remaining debt and next decisions

- The authorised Pipeline retains its existing workspace/Canvas utility header; the shared primary navigation is present, but minor shell styling differences remain. No access controls were changed to cosmetically unify that header.
- Older report/node routes coexist with visual-first views; explicit dossiers intentionally retain specialist detail and can produce long appendices. Legacy HTML inside a closed dossier can contain older headings; default visible summaries remain separated.
- Phone industry exploration uses the existing equivalent text navigation where the large illustrated system is hidden. This is a functional fallback, not evidence of equal desktop/phone visual richness. Human evaluation is required before claiming visual acceptance.
- Native browser Back preserves cross-layer Macro selection. A persistent cross-layer breadcrumb/Macro capture reference is not implemented; any future extension must use existing route/capture owners and immutable semantics.
- Existing 57 inherited failures and ten unavailable retained-artifact skips remain product/diagnostic limitations, not newly accepted regressions. Physical iPad/Safari, screen-reader evaluation and human comprehension remain outstanding.
- #563's geography/jurisdiction separation and evidence-governed context are compatible with this build. Its wider entities, Research Missions, agent orchestration, legal applicability and expansion roadmap remain proposals or future governed work, not automatic schema ratification.
- Future AI-agent research should submit candidate observations through existing Researcher/Mission/source review and canonical evidence owners, preserving provenance, supersession and explicit acceptance. It must not self-qualify Pipeline opportunities or manufacture applicability.
- International expansion can reuse industry/capability models with separate evidenced market/entity/instrument context. The French research blockers must be closed before asserting enterprise duties or commercial effects; no all-sector/all-country expansion is included here.

Suitable for draft owner visual/commercial evaluation after actual local and hosted acceptance. Merge, deployment, production qualification and customer outreach remain separately authorised decisions.
