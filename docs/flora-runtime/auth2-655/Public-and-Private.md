## Nationwide decision

The unchanged existing Source record `RUD1-NATIONWIDE-FY26` permits isolated public
financial facts and independently authored factual analysis under the existing
minimal-facts method, not report-expression redistribution or a general issuer
licence. Its `flora_research_views` audience is also used for existing public FCA
facts and is not an explicit private/tenant label. No applicable restriction on
this finite same-purpose slice was recorded. AUTH-2 reassesses product access;
UX-R1's prior private Financial-run decision is not itself a publisher prohibition.

`public_nationwide_projection()` reads only the current existing Source bundle,
retains `routine_factual_use`, exact source/measurement bindings, source digests,
legal scope, dates and typed Financial reconciliation. It never reads a run path,
run file, workspace, ACL, user, provider, result cache or run availability. It does
not manufacture a temporary private run. It reuses the existing pure Financial
calculation and editorial composer. Private views still use their original IAM.

The eight values are actual Nationwide Building Society **consolidated Group**,
1 April 2025–31 March 2026, GBP millions with original precision/basis:

- Underlying income 6,378 − operating costs 4,021 − credit impairments 331 =
  underlying profit before tax 2,026.
- 2,026 + net income adjustment 1 − acquisition-related expense adjustment 138 − member rewards 399 =
  statutory profit before tax 1,490.
- £127m integration costs are already included in operating costs; not an extra
  deduction. Virgin Money full-year versus prior six-month contribution prevents
  an unqualified like-for-like growth claim.

Reported endpoints remain Reported; arithmetic bridges remain Calculated. Society
complaints cover 1 October 2025–31 March 2026 and a different perimeter/cohort.
They are not mixed into Group earnings. No buying need, margin, demand or sales
opportunity is inferred.

Compact attribution remains in the legal notice. Full original provenance stays
with Source and existing private Research/Owner inspection; no new public source
inspector or financial export is added. This is not a fresh legal opinion or
fresh terms inspection. Continued rights/reliability review, source withdrawal,
owner-only/private restrictions, contradiction, supersession and stale-source
flags fail closed and remove dependent public figures. Unrelated permitted facts
remain available. No source ledger, permission, issuer allowlist, original research
identity, canonical record or Proposed ADR-027 is changed.

No anonymous auth/provider network is needed. With a stale signed-in cookie,
identity failure falls back to permitted public facts, never to private authority.
