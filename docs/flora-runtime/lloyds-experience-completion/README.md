## Commercial usefulness

Three useful, unranked investigations emerge from the complete enterprise view:

1. **Economic quality of growth:** balance scale is available, but cohort funding price, loan repricing, credit losses and capital consumption determine return. A matched improving risk-adjusted cohort would contradict a deterioration thesis. The published CFO/business remits are context, not delegated buyers.
2. **AI value after existing capability:** Lloyds already has platforms, controls, tools and specialist capacity. Investigate a specific customer/control/work outcome after full run, change and supervision costs. Deployment or activity alone cannot prove net return, and existing remedies may leave no external scope.
3. **Continuity through simplification:** application retirement and published change ambitions create a sequencing question, not an assumed failure. Test the service dependency, recovery evidence and disappearing recurring cost. A controlled, effective transition is counter-evidence to a remediation thesis.

These are investigation questions, not invented pain, budgets, contracts or sales opportunities. Existing customer-confirmed qualification and authorised Pipeline hand-off stay separate.
