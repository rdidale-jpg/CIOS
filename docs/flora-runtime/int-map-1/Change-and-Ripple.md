# News, change and ripple-through

**Design contract only.** Reuse existing Source/Research, Financial, Enterprise
memory, applicability, presentation and private commercial owners. No universal
event registry, graph database, autonomous agent, scheduler or second matcher.

## Distinct meanings and dates

| Item | Meaning | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article | Publisher's expression and account of one or more events | Permission to reproduce it; an independent event per outlet |
| Reported event | Identified subject, action and dated occurrence/disclosure | Completed rollout, verified consequence or fresh buying intent |
| Verified change | Supported difference from an identifiable prior state, or a supported newly announced condition | Independent proof of every publisher assertion |
| Inferred impact | Reasoned mechanism applied to supported exposure | A fact simply because its inputs are facts |
| Industry pattern | Qualified inference from a declared comparable sample | Prevalence from one bank, duplicated reports or unlike periods |
| Commercial hypothesis | Possible enterprise-specific consequence relevant to an approved seller context | Customer-confirmed need, budget, procurement or qualification |
| Qualified opportunity | Existing criteria, real customer evidence and explicit reviewed hand-off | Anything automatically created from News or Material Pressure |

These roles do not introduce a seventh truth label. Retain **Reported,
Calculated, Estimated, Working assumption, FLORA Insight and Hypothesis**.
Source quality, semantic type and canonical acceptance are separate dimensions.
“Verified change” means the particular difference/condition has adequate support;
it is not canonical Evidence acceptance or verification of every publisher claim.
A calculation using an assumption cannot become an established reported fact.

Retain separately: **publication date, event date, effective date, reporting
period, retrieval time, verification/as-known cutoff and supersession time**.
Missing dates remain internal; do not substitute retrieval time or invent an event
day. Republication may update source support without becoming another event.
Date-only knowledge must not imply an exact time or changed effective obligation.

BIE-2's 17 News rows are candidate material with `production_news_eligible:false`,
anchored 15 November 2026. Their literal 16 October–15 November bounds include
31 dates; do not silently rename them an exact rolling30-day interval or move the
cutoff to17 November. Legacy timelines include illustrative anchors; Morning
Edition explicitly describes a seeded pilot. None proves a live News service.

## Three directions, with evidence stopping points

**Down: UK → Industry → Enterprise.** Associate a policy with the industry's
actual operating mechanism. That may justify attention. Assert enterprise
applicability only with legal/entity scope, service/capability exposure, effective
period and support. Assert a consequence only with its additional evidence or a
clearly typed inference. CTP designation → technology-provider oversight is
supported; CTP designation → every bank has an unmet resilience requirement is not.

**Across: suppliers and peers.** Follow supported typed relationships. A Google
Cloud brand collaboration is not a contract with Google Cloud EMEA Limited.
Do not join by name resemblance or co-occurrence. A source may disclose a
relationship without disclosing workload, criticality, contract scope or spend.

**Up: Enterprise → Industry → UK.** Keep the enterprise fact in its owner. Before
deriving a pattern, declare the independently supported enterprise sample,
business models/geography, periods, definitions, selection bias, exclusions,
counterexamples and falsifier. Two is a minimum independent comparison, not a
statistical sufficiency rule. A bank's result may be a useful exemplar without
being an industry trend. UK synthesis needs additional national evidence and an
appropriate aggregation basis; never add company growth rates into GDP.

## Smallest reusable dependency / invalidation contract

Derive a **transient footprint** in the existing read model. This is not a newly
persisted intelligence object or source registry:

```text
owner + existing object ID + exact revision
subject namespace / entity / group perimeter / geography
event, publication, effective and reporting dates + as-known cutoff
existing typed association + endpoint scope + supporting IDs/revisions
source-use/lifecycle state + counter-evidence/supersession
semantic / recipe / presentation policy version
caller visibility, workspace/resource boundary where private
```

On read, ask each owner for current eligible output. A rights withdrawal,
contradiction, revision, identity/basis/period change, revoked permission or stale
input invalidates the dependent conclusion. Omit or recompose only affected
derived views. Preserve historical evidence and prior assessments internally;
do not erase records, rewrite source history or make private facts public.
If a previously visible title/amount/count is now private, omit it too. No public
cross-tenant deduplication, shared cache, event count or stale private tooltip.

Reuse the following rather than recreate them:

- Financial `_current_inputs` / `current_projection` and public projection:
  current source/recipe/binding checks, fail-closed omission and typed arithmetic.
- Research `compare_revision`: explicit IDs/same-period supersession, not silent
  date replacement; presentation eligibility/counter-evidence checks.
- Canonical `ExternalChangeInterpretationService`: existing governed applicability
  and consequence assessment. **Working facts do not satisfy its canonical-input
  gates by relabelling.** Any later working-view adapter belongs to the existing
  research/read owner and must preserve its lower authority explicitly.
- `TwinDependencyService` / pending reconciliation: after approved canonical
  package promotion, exact stable-ID references can create existing review work.
  That is not an approval requirement for every ordinary working fact.
- Seller-development dependency digests and explicit reassess: any later seller
  investigation is user-initiated through the shared matcher/development services.

Read-time recomposition is not event-driven dispatch. A later owner-approved
change notification could prompt the same read/reassessment, with bounded fanout,
idempotency and source/visibility checks. That requires separate implementation;
this commission introduces no queue, monitoring schedule or automatic adoption.

## Negative and temporal acceptance cases for a later implementation

| Change/challenge | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Withdraw Envoy source | Remove dependent AI story/conclusion; preserve history and unrelated Banking facts |
| Replace designation effective date with sourced correction | Preserve as-known version, recompute current date/application; never backdate knowledge |
| Google Cloud brand versus designated legal entity | Candidate contextual relevance only; no verified bank contract/exposure edge |
| Nationwide perimeter or accounting basis differs | Reject comparison/derived trend; retain individually valid reported values |
| Three outlets describe one announcement | One event with independent source references, not three confirmations or industry instances |
| Multiple reports copy the same issuer statement | Do not count as independent evidence of an industry pattern |
| Late source retrieved today | Historical context, not this week's event |
| Future-effective policy | Separate “announced”, “effective” and “forthcoming”; no premature duty |
| One bank / outlier / counterexample | Exemplar or qualified hypothesis; no sector-wide prevalence claim |
| Private Offer, source or membership revoked | No private headline/amount/count/tooltip; stale development cannot hand off |
| Seller selects a story | Navigate/read only; no auto-create, pressure promotion or Pipeline write |

## Product experience and selection

Keep global tools independent of contextual streams. A change view presents one
lead explanation and two supporting dated moments, not a card forest or feed.
Use one subject state: scope, country, enterprise focus, lens, period and selected
development. Preserve it in browser history/deep links; drop incompatible detail
with an explicit visible return to industry scope, never silently show industry
content under an active enterprise label. One interaction updates the Main view.

The concept uses a quiet time rail and approved Banking portrait, without arrows,
halo size or map coordinates implying exposure or impact. The historical period
is visible. A future weekly edition requires real eligible coverage; sparse or
absent current changes must not be filled with old events or research questions.
Do not activate a third tab until it has a meaningful view. Source IDs, rights,
trace and research administration remain in authorised inspection. Show material
scope/date/counter-evidence in concise business language, retaining six types.
