### Findings resolved in this revision

- **P1 ownership completeness:** import/review is not the only canonical update
  path. Existing `collect → process_evidence` and source factual contracts are
  mapped. This internal mutator is not a safe new public ingestion API merely
  because it exists; its future caller must preserve owning authority.
- **P1 legal-entity applicability:** CTP designation and Lloyds Google Cloud brand
  collaboration cannot be joined into a verified bank contract or workload.
- **P2 existing change machinery:** Observatory already compares mixed seeded/live
  snapshots. Retain it as historical heuristic functionality, not industry truth
  or the new public significance/ranking algorithm.
- **P2 pressure ownership:** qualification and dependent reassessment live inside
  Enterprise Model state/history, not an invented separate pressure store.
- **P2 time and meaning:** May–July examples are context, not November weekly/90-day
  News. Approximately £50m reported2025 AI value and expected >£100m2026 remain
  different meanings; no Envoy benefit attribution or net-savings conclusion.
- **P2 visual selection:** one industry/enterprise focus governs all prototype
  lenses, including the Lloyds image label. Whole-industry company stories are
  labelled “Enterprise example”, not evidence of a sector pattern.
- **P2 editorial semantics:** Nationwide Forces explains changed contribution and
  comparison, not another profit bridge mislabelled Insight. Internal procurement
  exclusions remain in the decision pack rather than dominate ordinary Main.

Reviewers favoured **A for the smallest first proof under sparse current supply**
and **B as a conditional product direction**. This is a useful tension, not a
claim that the historical concept establishes sufficient current News supply.
