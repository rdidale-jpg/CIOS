## Owner walkthrough

1. Open the study's What's changing? view. Note the explicit historical period,
   chronological moments and one lead explanation. No scores or invented arrows.
2. Select Nationwide. Earnings are a Group example with a changed Virgin Money
   contribution, not a sector-wide trend or claim of a new buying opportunity.
3. Select Lloyds. Its announced platform is an existing response, with operating
   value/benefit limitations retained. No tender or supplier allocation is inferred.
4. Choose Nationwide focus and switch Landscape / Forces / What's changing?.
   The same subject persists; Back/Forward restores it. Lloyds' image label uses
   the same focus state rather than creating another selection model.
5. Inspect iPad portrait/mobile: the scene collapses while the Main explanation
   remains readable. Keyboard and200% text checks are in the CI evidence.
6. Compare with current accepted Banking
   [Landscape](../uxr1-650/previews/after-guest-desktop-landscape.jpg) and
   [Forces](../uxr1-650/previews/after-guest-desktop-forces.jpg), and current
   [public Nationwide](../auth2-655/previews/after-guest-nationwide-desktop.jpg).
   Those are retained baseline previews, not new live-production captures.

Accept the product direction only if the third stream provides a distinct useful
answer. Otherwise choose the smaller existing-view pulse. Full source/rights,
missing applicability and future research questions stay in owner decision
material; core pages retain concise dates, scope and material caveats.
