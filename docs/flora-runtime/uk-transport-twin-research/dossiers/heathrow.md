Research cut: **18 November 2026**. Research-only; no accepted Model or public source grant.

Entity references: `HEATHROW HSP`. Claim trail: `HA-1 HA-2 IN-HA`.

Airport economics combine aeronautical charges, passenger retail and other commercial income. They differ from airline seat/yield economics and public road funding. The public results table reports a holding/reporting perimeter; Heathrow (SP) Limited is verified as a company, but the operator's standalone legal accounts are not silently bound to the same figures. Debt reported for Finance and SP cannot be interchanged.

The analysis separates efficient use of existing runway/terminal capacity from expansion. Proposed durable capabilities include slot/stand coordination, passenger/security/baggage throughput, facilities and asset reliability, airline interface management, retail/property operation and capital delivery assurance. Suppliers, airlines and concessionaires play different roles. Passenger journeys are not all direct airport revenue transactions.

The public release identifies lower charges and higher operating costs alongside resilient performance. Expansion advocacy and wider projected trade/jobs effects are issuer expectations, not realised airport revenue or independently established returns. Thomas Woldbye and Sally Ding are named CEO/CFO in the dated release; that does not assign project procurement authority.
