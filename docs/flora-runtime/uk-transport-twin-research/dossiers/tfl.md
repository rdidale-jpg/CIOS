Research cut: **18 November 2026**. Research-only; no accepted Model or public source grant.

Entity references: `TFL LU`. Claim trail: `TF-1 TF-2 TF-3 IN-TFL HY-TFL`.

TfL is a London authority/group, combining transport operation, contracted services and other income/funding. Its unaudited performance account must retain the distinction between passenger receipts, enforcement/other income, retained rates and grants. Capital renewals and interest are included in the management surplus definition. Places for London is separately identified; do not call the reported transport surplus Group statutory net profit.

The useful operating model is integrated service delivery: plan network capacity, procure/operate services, provide ticket/payment access, keep assets available, inform passengers and manage disruption. Proposed capabilities include fleet/asset maintenance, control-room decisions, accessibility, customer information, fare collection and concession management. Staff competence, depots, telemetry, customer demand and financial controls connect these capabilities. This is not evidence of a specific application architecture.

The quarter report describes TfL Go improvements, mobile coverage and asset renewals. The historical Piccadilly committee paper establishes an existing Siemens train and maintenance-support award, with signalling Stage2 outside that paper's authorisation. The programme's approved envelope and train order are not available supplier spending. Historical paper schedules are not restated as current rollout dates. Exempt Part2 is not accessed.

Patrick Doig is identified as CFO in the quarter report. Current CEO, business-unit budget owners and maintenance decision rights are not independently reverified here. The distinction matters: a relevant leadership role is not a named authorised buyer.

Investigation should differentiate demand shortfall, enforcement provisioning and asset reliability instead of attributing the small surplus to a single technology problem. Existing renewal funding, supplier whole-life support and delivered accessibility works are counter-evidence to an indiscriminate need thesis. For a fleet diagnostic, identify a measurable decision delay, existing fault-triage tools and Siemens support obligations first. The inaccessible Find a Tender lead is retained as blocked, not turned into an award, scope or renewal date. No current tender or sales opportunity is inferred.
