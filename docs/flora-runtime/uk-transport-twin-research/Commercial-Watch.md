The five explicit hypotheses in the ledger are falsifiable. A failed baseline, a current remedy, absent legal data access or already allocated supplier scope can defeat them. None becomes actionable merely because a relevant leader exists or a public programme is large. AI assistance is a possible method after the diagnostic, not a required solution. Safety-critical operations require the existing safety and human decision framework; no autonomous rail/flight/traffic decision system is proposed.
