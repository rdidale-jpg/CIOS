## Source assurance limits

Source ledger identifies the selected original passages and acquisition extent. None is an original-byte archive. Company overviews establish register identity, not audited financial contents. NI/National Highways indices are limited source routes. easyJet is explicitly prior-source reuse. Missing dates remain missing. Search snippets and temporary signed URLs are not committed evidence; technical errors are recorded without retry/bypass. Public availability is not blanket commercial licensing; source-specific restrictions, attribution and stronger canonical authority remain existing-owner concerns.

## Factual and commercial self-critique

- Strong: differentiated funding/risk/property/logistics models; exact context arithmetic; existing supplier remedies; dated/proposed institution handling; no fictional spend or demand.
- Weak: selected reports do not provide all internal capabilities, leadership or legal contracts. UK scope currently leans toward GB/England; Scotland and NI have narrower source coverage; Wales and shipping/freight/ROSCO depth remain open.
- Commercial limit: documented operating issues support investigation, not a seller pitch, buyer need or proof of an influence window. Existing programmes and contrary outcomes can defeat each hypothesis.
- Architecture limit: no current production identity/import read was authorised or available through this research path. No canonical completeness, persisted Transport model or live functional improvement is claimed.
- Source-use limit: Heathrow public PDF wording remains unresolved; public HTML factual analysis does not settle those terms. IAG platform date cannot support a recent-news classification.

This is an explicit **self-review**. No separate independent reviewer or research agent was commissioned in this sprint; owner/Chief Architect review remains required. Passing tests establish reproducibility/boundaries, not research completeness, legal advice or customer demand.
