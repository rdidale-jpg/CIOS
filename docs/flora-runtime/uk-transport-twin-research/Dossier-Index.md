# Enterprise cohort and reading order

The selection covers distinct economic roles, not twelve uniformly complete Twins. Network Rail, FirstGroup, National Highways, TfL, ABP and IAG have substantive sourced economic/operating analysis. DP World has throughput/programme evidence but no UK legal accounts; Heathrow has public results and a separate source-use exception. Royal Mail has identity/service evidence; DHL has UK identity but only global divisional economics. easyJet is an existing-owner projection. The institutional dossier prevents England/GB/UK and proposed/current conflation.
