# Transport economics without a fictional total market

Transport connects households and shippers to operators, infrastructure, terminals, equipment and enabling services. Government allocates funding; regulators constrain delivery; labour, energy and asset availability affect outputs. Money and physical journeys flow in different directions. **No aggregate UK Transport £ market value is proposed**: the reviewed measures have incompatible boundaries, periods and transfer treatments.

|Measure|What it means|What it must not become|
|---|---|---|
|Rail system income/farebox|UK statistical industry funding and operator receipts|Network Rail receipts added again, or total supplier spend|
|Network Rail regulatory statement|GB grants/access income, opex/capex and balance-sheet stocks|IFRS company P&L, England-only total, or technology budget|
|FirstGroup APM vs statutory revenue|Different treatment of gross public-contracted rail flows|A common-denominator margin using mixed accounting bases|
|TfL management revenue and surplus|Fares plus other income, rates and grants; special renewals/interest treatment|Audited statutory profit or all-UK public transport revenue|
|Local Authority Bus Grant|England outside London allocations, separate revenue/capital periods|Operator turnover, contract award or discretionary IT spend|
|GB rail freight quarter|ORR freight moved/lifted, train distance and cancellations; separate from rail finance|Company revenue, annualised demand, all-UK totals or proven modal shift|
|Road freight tonne-km / port tonnes / TEU|Different physical workload/capacity measures|Revenue, comparable units or additive end-to-end freight demand|
|ABP company operating profit|Cargo/property activity with valuation effects|Cash handling margin, Group EBITDA or whole UK port economics|
|Heathrow public results|Airport group revenues and adjusted earnings|Airline turnover, operating legal-company profit or realised expansion returns|
|IAG quarterly EUR result|Global airline group earnings in its own basis|UK airline market or GBP quarterly/annual bank comparator|
|DHL global division result|Mixed-geography/perimeter business performance|UK legal-company sales or customer contract value|

The ten [calculations](Calculations.json) reproduce **reported-input arithmetic**, not investment recommendations. Measurement context includes entity/perimeter, period, basis, currency, unit, state and stock/flow. NR plan/actual comparisons need explicit variance semantics; the ordinary same-state bridges reject a plan substituted as actual. RAB/debt stocks cannot be added to annual expenditure. TfL rounded components leave a preserved residual; no forced exactness or invented adjustment.

Labour and energy matter in different mechanisms: rail maintenance/possessions and traction, TfL service execution and provisions, airline fuel and safety, cargo/warehouse handling and equipment. These are analytical mechanisms with documented examples, not quantified cost allocations for every enterprise. Contract logistics prices, port cargo mix, regulated settlement details and open supplier scope require additional original evidence.

Passenger fares, public grants, operator-to-infrastructure access charges and infrastructure-to-supplier payments form connected flows. Addressable supplier spending requires a specific buyer, legal scope, award/procurement and terms. No reviewed aggregate satisfies that definition. Programmes and efficiency targets must retain whether they are planned, authorised, awarded, spent or independently realised.
