# Public factual intelligence · proposed Source-use policy

Owner decision draft, 19 November 2026. Reviewed main `46ed59295abb0259ee09ffa79c778848f170a5eb`, including the merged LEI-1 pack. This proposal changes no enforcement, admissions, permissions or canonical records.

## Decision

**Permit ordinary public factual display by default within a reviewed use category, subject to specific exceptions.** If CIOS verifies an isolated public fact in an authentic source, records its provenance and expresses it independently, it should ordinarily be eligible for a public, read-only FLORA view under the applicable category decision. Original calculations and interpretation can be displayed when all essential premises are eligible, the method is reproducible and the output distinguishes fact, calculation, interpretation, hypothesis and uncertainty.

An existing Source owner makes a reusable category or series decision once for a defined use and audience. A later fact that matches the decision passes deterministic identity, provenance, meaning and lifecycle checks. It needs no new discretionary rights decision. An exception applies when there is a concrete legal, contractual, security or evidential issue. If a source's applicable terms or actual use are materially unresolved, isolate that source/use and continue with other eligible material.

Start with two reusable categories. **Licensed official numerical releases** use the applicable dataset licence and attribution, excluding third-party and non-covered material. **Issuer financial disclosures** use selected independently stated facts and original analysis, after confirming the method and applicable terms for the issuer series. The second category does not inherit the first category's licence. A revised filing can remain within a series decision, but still needs its own identity and version checks. A new source with matching terms does not inherit a named issuer's existing permission.

The default covers **the minimum independently stated facts and provenance needed for the public view**. A category decision specifies what acquisition and minimal retention can take place, because a lawful output does not settle whether the means of obtaining or storing inputs are allowed. A public calculation does not create a tenant Financial run. Neither operation canonises Evidence or advances an Opportunity.

## Exceptions and the reason for each

| Keep or escalate | Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copyright and other protected material | Excerpt, reproduction of wording, distinctive table/selection/arrangement, graphics, imagery, publisher or third-party material beyond the reviewed independent-facts method | Omit unless a licence, relevant exception or other legal basis covers that exact use. Quotation is a separate fair-dealing question, including necessity and acknowledgement. |
| Database rights and scale | Substantial extraction or re-utilisation, or repeated and systematic use of small parts that can amount to substantial re-use under the applicable rules | Review cumulative conduct and applicable rights. Manually choosing a few figures for one view is materially different from a recurring collector or republishing the series. No fixed count settles the legal question. |
| Binding terms, licences and access | Exact terms apply to the proposed acquisition, storage, link, factual reproduction, redistribution or partner use, or the answer is materially unresolved. Paywall, authentication and technical protections apply independently. | Apply applicable terms or obtain a lawful basis for the exact use. Do not switch mirrors, accounts, formats or hosts to evade the restriction. Public availability, regulator hosting and XBRL format do not by themselves confer permission. |
| Private information and security | Tenant or owner research, access-controlled inputs, confidentiality, personal data, restricted provider output, private run metadata or private overrides | Deny public projection at the owning boundary. Public subject identity is never public authority to read workspaces, disclose record existence or perform a mutation. Preserve access controls and applicable privacy requirements. |
| Accuracy and current provenance | Wrong issuer/legal entity, accounting perimeter, period, currency, unit, sign, version, actual/target state, unsupported inference, conflict, withdrawal or supersession | Omit the failed element and any dependent explanation, calculation or discovery metadata. Keep the historic record under existing authority. A historical observation can be shown as explicitly dated when eligible, without asserting current status. |
| Scope expansion | Archive copies, bulk or recurring collection, broader audience/export, persisted Financial run, paid/provider processing, or acceptance into Evidence/Enterprise Models | Separate the intended operations and route only the change through the existing owner. A category decision for a public view does not approve these uses. |

The UK government’s copyright and AI report distinguishes facts and data from protected expression and treats extraction separately. Original selection or arrangement may attract database copyright. The UK database regulations separately address extraction and re-utilisation, including cumulative activity. Do not rely on the UK non-commercial research TDM exception for ordinary commercial FLORA use. These sources support the distinction, rather than a determination that any specific issuer terms are binding or that a particular reuse is lawful. ## Minimal document and implementation changes

- Record the category decision through existing Source-use ownership with one reviewable revision. Bind authentic issuer and permitted originals/series, public read-only purpose, permitted acquisition/retention/output, independent wording, attribution, excluded material, lifecycle and escalations. Start with an exact Q3 document instance if terms are established only there. Reuse the decision for compatible future documents only when the reviewed series scope supports it.
- Update the owning Source-use guidance and clarify the scope split in F1/F3 cross-references. Fold ADR-027’s retained-document controls into the owner’s next decision if warranted. No additional registry, approval service or runtime architecture is needed. Historical pilot records remain historical.
