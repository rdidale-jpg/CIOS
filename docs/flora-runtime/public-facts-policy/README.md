# Public factual intelligence · proposed Source-use policy

Owner decision draft, 19 November 2026. Reviewed main `46ed59295abb0259ee09ffa79c778848f170a5eb`, including the merged LEI-1 pack. This proposal changes no enforcement, admissions, permissions or canonical records.

## Final proposed policy wording

**Public factual intelligence is permitted by default within an established reusable use category wherever the actual use is lawful.** Verified public facts, independently expressed explanations and original calculations do not require repeated discretionary approval or universal canonical Evidence promotion.

1. **Facts and expression are different.** Copyright can protect original wording, graphics and original selection/arrangement; it does not protect underlying facts as such. Publisher permission is **not presumed necessary for every isolated factual statement or independently calculated measure**. A copyright notice is not, by itself, a prohibition on using facts. Attribution is provenance, not a substitute for any permission actually needed to copy protected expression.
2. **Establish the method once; validate facts routinely.** The existing Source owner records the applicable category, lawful acquisition method, audience, factual output, minimal retained form and exceptions through existing Source-use ownership. A category may cover compatible sources across publishers and industries. It need not be a named-issuer grant. A new issuer, report, number or calculation within that scope requires ordinary source/version/meaning checks, **not another rights application or per-measure approval**. An old named-source permission does not become a blanket licence for other publishers.
3. **Use the legal basis that actually applies.** Independent factual analysis can rely on the distinction above and applicable lawful-user rights; it need not invent a publisher licence. Licensed official numerical releases separately retain their actual licence, attribution and third-party exclusions. Neither route authorises protected report/table copying, an access workaround, a conflicting binding contract or substantial/cumulatively restricted database reuse. The UK non-commercial TDM exception is not a commercial-use permission for FLORA.
4. **Restrict the affected operation, for a stated reason.** Identify the clause/right/control, whether and how it applies, the actual use and material uncertainty. A concrete unresolved restriction can hold that source/use, acquisition method, retention form, link or output. It does not suspend unrelated eligible research. A generic copyright footer, absence of an express factual-use licence, or an unsupported possibility of a restriction is not itself a new approval gate. Binding terms must be honoured; an asserted website term is assessed for incorporation, scope and applicable statutory limits, rather than automatically declared either binding or irrelevant.
5. **Keep intelligence and authority intact.** Preserve authentic Source identity, provenance, exact entity/perimeter, period, units, precision, reporting basis, semantic type, reproducible derivation, uncertainty and withdrawal/supersession dependencies. Public factual eligibility, evidence quality and canonical acceptance remain separate. It confers no private-workspace access, Financial-run creation, Evidence/Observation/Model promotion, Material Pressure qualification or Pipeline mutation.

This is **one proposed reusable factual-use method under existing ownership**, not a new approval service, rights registry, issuer-by-issuer licensing programme or per-measure workflow. The existing Source owner remains the decision owner; legal advice is sought where a concrete material issue warrants it, not as a mandatory second approval for routine facts. Standard cases pass deterministic checks after category adoption. Revisit a category premise when the actual legal/use context changes; do not reapprove compatible figures merely because a document version or issuer changes.

Minimal numerical working data and source/version/locator metadata are distinct from retaining a source PDF, copied table, excerpt corpus or embedding collection. A public calculation is distinct from persisting a tenant Financial run. The category must cover the actual acquisition and minimal retention as well as output. It cannot make an otherwise unlawful input lawful through independent output wording.

## Explicit remaining exceptions

| Actual issue | Proportionate response |
| --- | --- |
| Protected expression, imagery or original table/compilation arrangement | Use only with an applicable licence, statutory exception or other lawful basis. Isolated facts do not inherit the protection of the surrounding prose. Assess copied material, not a numerical-count safe harbour. |
| Database rights, substantial extraction or cumulative systematic reuse | Assess the actual source, qualitative/quantitative extent, lawful-user position and cumulative method. Lawful-user insubstantial-use protections and restrictions both matter. Three numbers is the bounded example here, not a universal legal threshold. |
| Binding contractual restriction or material uncertainty about an identified term | Record the actual clause, contracting/access context and affected operation; resolve through existing Source/legal ownership. Seek publisher consent only if actually necessary, rather than assuming it is always the remedy. A decision on one standard term/method can be reused. |
| Paywall, authentication, confidentiality, technical access protection or private information | Preserve lawful access and all security/privacy controls. Do not change account, format or mirror to evade a restriction. An eligible public subject does not authorise reading private research or discovering a private run. |
| Evidential or financial defect | Omit the defective fact and dependent outputs: wrong issuer, period, accounting basis, actual/target state, units, contradiction, unverified inference, withdrawal or supersession. Keep governed history and unrelated eligible facts. |
| Different operation or scale | Archive/report copying, bulk/recurring collection, protected-content processing, export, broader audience, Financial-run persistence and canonical promotion must match their applicable existing authority. A changed operation can require a scoped decision; persistence of a few bare factual values is not automatically document copying or a private Financial run. |

The [19 November source-use disposition](lloyds-q3-disposition.md) records the primary material inspected and its limits. The [government copyright report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/report-and-impact-assessment-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence/report-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence), Section A, explicitly separates facts from creative expression; it does not grant a licence to bank reports. [IPO exceptions guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exceptions-to-copyright) supports use-specific exceptions and their conditions. [Database Regulations 16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3032/regulation/16) and [19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3032/regulation/19) address infringement/cumulative use and lawful-user protections respectively. Neither a policy report nor these general principles decides the incorporation and effect of a particular contract. No legal reform, issuer licence or complete enforceability opinion is invented.

## Narrow Lloyds Q3 2026 disposition

The proposed factual method applies to three independently stated nine-month measures in LEI-1 S3, subject to the specific contractual question below. Their source, reporting definitions and original locators have been rechecked. **No additional copyright permission is identified merely to restate these numerical facts or retain their minimal factual working data.** This is not an unconditional finding that every possible contract permits the use, nor an executed source permission.

| Operation | Proposed disposition / exact remaining issue |
| --- | --- |
| Read the ordinary public report | Ordinary lawful reading; no additional publisher authorisation identified. No private access, protection bypass or report archive is proposed. |
| Independently restate three numerical facts | Ordinary factual-use category, with source/meaning checks; no per-number copyright licence. The possible contractual reach of Lloyds §2 to this independently expressed commercial use is the scoped remaining issue, not an assumed need to license each fact. |
| Retain minimal numerical working data | Same factual method for values, definitions and provenance, rather than protected documents/tables. Resolve §2's reach to that minimal form once if material; do not equate it with a Financial run. |
| Link to the original source | A conditional home-page link is expressly described by §5. New direct PDF/deep links have a specific home-page-only terms issue: retain bibliographic attribution and the internal original locator; use the compliant home-page route pending resolution. This does not require withholding the underlying facts. |
| Copy protected documents or tables | Outside the factual method. No applicable commercial copying basis has been established here; a licence, applicable exception or other lawful basis is needed for that operation. No publisher table/report copy is proposed. |
| Persist Financial Intelligence runs | Existing F1 Lloyds exclusion and workspace/capability/resource authority remain unchanged. Any extension needs a separately authorised bounded implementation and Source-use scope. Publisher approval is not presumed necessary simply because a run stores numbers. |

**Recommendation:** accept the reusable policy and this copyright-versus-facts distinction. Keep only the concrete Lloyds contractual/deep-link and persisted-run exceptions open. The existing Source/legal owner should determine once whether §2 is incorporated and reaches the proposed independent three-fact/minimal-retention method, and whether an applicable statutory protection changes that result. Do not turn that narrow unresolved point into an issuer-wide ban or a recurring request for permission from Lloyds. If a binding restriction actually reaches those operations and no lawful basis is available, withhold only the affected new use. Ordinary reading, permitted attribution/linking and unrelated eligible material continue. The detailed [basis and uncertainty](lloyds-q3-disposition.md) are part of the owner decision, not a new workflow.

No live Lloyds clearance is claimed. Existing released Lloyds measures and historical Source decisions stay intact; this PR changes no enforcement. A later public slice must come from existing Source ownership, never by exposing the LEI research folder or a private Financial run.

## Minimum implementation delta after owner acceptance

1. **Existing Source-use guidance:** adopt the method/category and its factual, minimal-retention, audience and exception boundaries once. Record the narrow Lloyds term interpretation in the existing Source record/decision material. No new rights registry, approval API, service or per-measure form. Do not amend historical F1/F3 grants retrospectively or mark ADR-027 accepted.
2. **Existing Source resolver only:** replace the publisher-specific ordinary-factual exception with trusted matching of the adopted method under `routine_factual_use`; retain source-level denials, origin/path controls on acquisition, provenance, exact bindings and lifecycle. Add an explicit minimal-facts/provenance integrity path where lawful; do not require a redundant full-text copy or accept missing text in a legacy record that still requires it. Caller-supplied category/rights flags confer no authority.
3. **Existing public projection/presentation only:** supply a finite eligible three-measure Lloyds slice through the existing typed Financial/presentation contracts. Preserve the public/private separation demonstrated by AUTH-2; no run lookup/creation, workspace dependency or public metadata leak. Keep Group underlying income separate from the banking NIM perimeter; no unsupported attribution, automatic opportunity or canonical update. Source withdrawal removes every dependent output, including caches and discovery metadata.

This PR implements **none** of those runtime changes.
