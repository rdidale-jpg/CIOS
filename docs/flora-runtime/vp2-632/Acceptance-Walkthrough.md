## Review sequence

1. Open `/`. Confirm Top skyline, real product menu, role and one active Landscape toggle. Select Banking from Left (expand Left first on portrait/mobile).
2. In Banking select Lloyds directly. Confirm this is two selections from UK, without a mandatory Banking Groups/question screen.
3. Select financial performance. Check deposits versus dated comparison, period profit, target labels, native 2025 reference checkbox, accessible table and material accounting caveats. Open Sources & basis; follow the publisher link if desired.
4. Switch Inside → Forces → Landscape. Check selected company/layer persist and compatible topics retain meaning. Use Back/Forward and a copied deep link. Close detail with its link or Escape. Switch a supplier/regulator layer and inspect the highlighted relevant subjects.
5. Open Leadership Agenda at each scope. Read declarative conclusions and counter-evidence; no suggested seller questions, completeness panel or research administration should appear.
6. Open Explore deeper. Visit the other supported banks, Technology, Aviation, regulators and payments. Check the 20 UK section labels; unsupported sections remain inert. Original active addresses remain usable without JavaScript and when illustration loading fails.
7. Test keyboard skip link, stream focus, native disclosures/checkbox, narrow width and enlarged text. On portrait/mobile open and close Left, then select a subject: Main is prioritised after navigation. Product navigation and workspace/sign-out controls remain real and permission-bound.

## Owner acceptance checklist

- [ ] Three-panel composition matches the accepted visual direction across seven streams.
- [ ] UK → Banking → Lloyds is clear in two selections.
- [ ] Portrait imagery, skyline, Main background and type hierarchy are elegant across desktop/iPad/mobile.
- [ ] Explore deeper makes the retained destinations sufficiently discoverable without clutter.
- [ ] Financial/agenda claims are commercially useful and their dates, targets and caveats are understandable.
- [ ] Context, keyboard focus, history, touch and optional sources behave as expected.
