# Dossier index

All are research candidates. Deep means justified depth target, not completeness.

| Dossier | Economic / operating focus |
| --- | --- |
| [Lloyds Banking Group / Lloyds Bank](dossiers/lloyds.md) | Funding and credit spread coexist with fees, leased-asset exposure and insurance/wealth economics. Use the two profit bridges; lease depreciation belongs above net income. Group profitability is not a capability-level business case. |
| [Barclays Group / UK banking division](dossiers/barclays.md) | Retail/SME spread and service income are different from UK Corporate Bank, investment banking and US cards. Total expenses include levies/conduct as well as operating costs; gross loans include more than customer loans. |
| [NatWest Group / National Westminster Bank](dossiers/natwest.md) | Separate Retail lending/deposits, Private/Wealth AUMA fees, and C&I transaction/financing economics. Acquired wealth balances change the perimeter; assets under administration are not bank deposits. |
| [HSBC UK, HSBC Bank and HSBC Holdings](dossiers/hsbc.md) | UK legal-bank reported USD income, the UK business and combined UK operating banks are three different perimeters. Transaction banking links domestic coverage to international services; Group legal-item movements cannot explain local client outcomes. |
| [Santander UK and TSB](dossiers/santander.md) | Deposit/mortgage banking is being combined with acquired TSB operations. Post-acquisition contribution is already in Group results. Restructuring/specific items are not all integration, and coding hours are not profit. |
| [Nationwide Group and Society](dossiers/nationwide.md) | Mutual value includes member pricing/reward alongside capital strength and retained earnings. A reward distribution and quantified member pricing benefit are not interchangeable expenses. Virgin Money changes reporting composition. |
| [Monzo](dossiers/monzo.md) | A broader app relationship combines banking spread, card/transaction activity and paid propositions. The rounded summary does not support a precise margin or blended customer economics by cohort. |
| [Starling Bank, Group and Engine](dossiers/starling.md) | Deposit bank and exported banking platform have different revenue/cost mechanisms. Engine accounts are not unique bank customers. Estimated primacy is a defined proxy, not a measured comparable household share. |
| [Coventry / Co-operative Bank](dossiers/coventry.md) | Mutual mortgage/savings economics now include a transactional bank. Underlying-to-statutory acquisition adjustments and prior one-off gain explain why unadjusted reported profits mislead. |
| [ClearBank UK / Europe / Group](dossiers/clearbank.md) | Access/platform fees and transaction services differ from deposit-funded retail lending. UK profit cannot be assigned to the Group; managed balances/payment value are not earned fees. |
| [OakNorth](dossiers/oaknorth.md) | Lower-mid-market lending requires origination, underwriting and servicing, with material US activity in the issuer scope. Loan originations are flow, outstanding loans stock and PBT residual return. |
| [Pay.UK Limited and retail systems](dossiers/payuk.md) | Company limited by guarantee is not a shareholder-return bank. Operator funding/fees and service delivery must be analysed separately from payment principal and settlement balances. Current accounts are unreadable here; no invented revenue or fee model. |
| [Bank of England RTGS / CHAPS](dossiers/boe.md) | Central-bank settlement supports stability and finality, not a retail spread/profit model. Bank-held settlement balances differ from commercial-bank customer deposits and payment throughput. |
| [Prudential Regulation Authority](dossiers/pra.md) | Prudential safety, capital and risk controls within the Bank of England; use public mandate/outcomes rather than bank-style P&L or customer sales. |
| [Financial Conduct Authority](dossiers/fca.md) | Conduct, market integrity and competition; fee-funded public regulator; use public mandate/outcomes rather than bank-style P&L or customer sales. |
| [Payment Systems Regulator](dossiers/psr.md) | Payment-system access regulation while institutional transfer is proposed; use public mandate/outcomes rather than bank-style P&L or customer sales. |
| [Microsoft](dossiers/microsoft.md) | Software subscriptions, cloud consumption and infrastructure/device economics coexist; current segment recast matters. |
| [Amazon Web Services](dossiers/aws.md) | Consumption/platform service fees and infrastructure scale, not Amazon retail gross sales or annualised run rate as actual year revenue. |
| [Google Cloud / Google Cloud EMEA](dossiers/google.md) | GCP services and TPU system sales contribute to a global Cloud segment; EMEA legal company and UK market remain separate. |
| [Computacenter](dossiers/computacenter.md) | Sourcing scale and agent/principal recognition coexist with professional/managed services; gross profit matters independently of gross margin percentage. |
| [Equinix](dossiers/equinix.md) | Recurring colocation/interconnection and non-recurring installations differ from hyperscaler compute consumption; AFFO is not GAAP profit or revenue. |
| [Arm](dossiers/arm.md) | Licensing and royalties remain distinct; emerging silicon deliveries add a different fulfilment/cost mechanism to the IP model. |
| [NVIDIA](dossiers/nvidia.md) | Platforms/hardware/software ecosystem capture value differently from cloud consumption or bank training costs; Data Center is a category, not a UK geography. |
| [easyJet airline / Group](dossiers/easyjet.md) | Ticket/ancillary yield, route mix, ownership/fuel/airport and disruption cost interact with fleet utilisation. Airline segment, Group and UK operating airline legal entity are different. Winter loss cannot be annualised. |
| [easyJet holidays segment / legal company](dossiers/easyjet-holidays.md) | Package distribution combines airline seats, hotels and services. Segment revenue includes flights eliminated in Group consolidation. Digital/variable-cost model differs from airline fleet ownership and from standalone11927917 accounts. |
