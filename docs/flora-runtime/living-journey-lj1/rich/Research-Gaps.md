# LJ-1R targeted enrichment requests

Existing-source audit only. These are unresolved inputs, not new research facts or automatically commissioned work. No canonical/historical data has been altered.

## FIN-01 · Current consolidated income and operating costs

Request: Latest official interim/annual income statement and reconciliation; net interest income, other income, total income, operating expenses, impairment, statutory vs underlying profit, GBP units and reporting periods.

Design boundary: Blocks quantified money-flow decomposition; old FY2024 fixture is not current evidence.

## FIN-02 · Comparable growth and profitability

Request: Company-disclosed matched-period income/cost/earnings growth, restatements, organic/FX boundary, margins and segment consistency.

Design boundary: No percentage growth or invented denominator shown.

## ORG-01 · Current segments, brands and legal subsidiaries

Request: Current official segment note, group organisation/subsidiary schedule, ownership percentages and entity IDs; distinguish brands, divisions, subsidiaries and regulated legal entities.

Design boundary: Historical three-division/four-brand profile does not prove current hierarchy.

## OPS-01 · Operations and important technology systems

Request: Official current operating-model disclosures, critical services, named systems/platform purpose, rollout status and dated dependencies.

Design boundary: Envoy/Vertex does not establish full core/payments estate or architecture.

## REL-01 · Suppliers, partners and customers

Request: Official specific relationship announcements/filings, parties/legal IDs, service, geography, date, status, rights and counter-evidence; current workshare/contract/renewal only if disclosed.

Design boundary: No supplier logos, unnamed customers or contracts invented.

## REG-01 · Legal-entity regulatory applicability

Request: Current official PRA/FCA register and entity/activity-level scope, BoE/PRA institutional structure source, permissions/firm numbers and dates.

Design boundary: Sector-policy actor links remain context, no firm-specific obligation graph.

## ORG-02 · Current leadership and accountability

Request: Official current governance/leadership page and report dates; role/entity scope and decision authority; no inferred buyer.

Design boundary: No named executive or buyer is drawn.

## INF-01 · Infrastructure operating relationships

Request: Official current CHAPS/RTGS and other payment-system operator/remit/participation sources and dates.

Design boundary: Infrastructure is depicted as sector function, not a particular bank contract.

## COM-01 · Remaining influence and external need

Request: Human-reviewed validation of unresolved outcomes, decision dates, current incumbent coverage, authorised access and external scope.

Design boundary: No early opportunity classification, budget, procurement or Pipeline write.
