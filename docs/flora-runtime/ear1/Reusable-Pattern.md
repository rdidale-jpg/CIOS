# Reusable admission pattern and commercial usefulness

The reusable output is a bounded checklist over existing owners, not a new engine or generic bank permission. Every next issuer starts denied/unverified; no NatWest decision would admit HSBC or Santander automatically.

1. Select a material decision and at most a small precisely defined assertion slice.
2. Reopen the authentic publisher original and locate the exact cells, entity, period, definitions, currency, scale, sign and measurement state.
3. Record honest source extent: original-byte digest only if real bytes were captured; reviewed-note digest never substitutes. Do not invent archive, extractor, confidence, table coordinates, source ID or approval.
4. Separate candidate truth/source verification from exact-use rights, technical admission, source-type capability, canonical identity, individual Evidence review and downstream visibility. Distinguish internal business review from seller/public reuse.
5. Verify existing financial metric/date/projection support, including persisted round-trip. A constructor, shared adapter or successful report is not accepted durable truth.
6. Reconcile real workspace/Enterprise/Evidence source identities and duplicates through authorised existing services. No public alias, account name or Group registration is an ACL or canonical target.
7. Only with separate authority use existing individual review→mapping→persisted plan→approval→transaction→receipt→Observation→Model reconciliation. Some review/preparation routes write; do not probe production by assuming GET/dry-run means safe.
8. Authorise read-only presentation only through the existing owners and six semantics, with source/tenant/revocation/counter-evidence controls. Stop on unresolved gates. Preserve denied candidates, contradictory/historical records and evidence demands in owner material.

## Commercial assessment

The two NatWest stock definitions improve account-scale accuracy and avoid applying a Group measure to a single bank. The proof identifies what is preventing trustworthy numeric Twin integration rather than presenting another polished financial paragraph as accepted knowledge.

Neither measure alone demonstrates business pain, supplier need, urgency, available investment, buyer authority or opportunity value. No third financial difference, liquidity/capital inference or procurement estimate is produced. A future commercial interpretation would need separately governed consequence, management response and counter-evidence; it cannot follow automatically from a large deposit number.

**Decision usefulness now:** owner can distinguish (a) exact rights/source authority, (b) missing domain compatibility, and (c) real identity/object review. Resolving only one of these will not unlock a safe production path. The next controlled tranche should be the minimal existing-owner implementation and isolated durable round-trip **after** the source-use and architectural decisions, not another volume bank research exercise.
