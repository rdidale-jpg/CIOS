## What the Banking analysis adds

The industry is not represented as a row of similar deposit banks. The register and analysis distinguish ring-fenced banks, Group reporting parents, specialist cross-border lenders, mutuals, platform businesses and payment infrastructure. Deposit/lending spreads, member value, payment fees and software licensing are different economic mechanisms.

Switching, complaints, independent disputes and client governance resolutions offer different customer signals. Q3 total switches cannot be joined to Q2 named-bank gains as a same-period ranking. Received complaints are not harm rates, and firm-upheld is not FOS adjudication. The public pilot therefore provides scale without inventing comparative service failure.

Typed relationships show the actual limits of supplier intelligence. NatWest names AWS/Accenture/OpenAI collaboration; Starling names Google use and NVIDIA/Civo development; ClearBank names customer brands and access capabilities. Those disclosures identify existing responses. They do not establish contracting subsidiary, workload, value, exclusivity, contract expiry or external scope. The candidate graph preserves those limitations at each edge.
