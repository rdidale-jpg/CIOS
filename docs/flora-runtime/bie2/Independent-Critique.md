## Commercial assessment and residual limitations

The six profiles provide different, financially anchored spread-bank, acquisition, mutual, platform and infrastructure mechanisms. They meet the bounded exemplar tranche; they are **not six complete decision-grade Twins**. Operating processes, technology estates, unit economics, legal supplier/customer endpoints and current individual decision rights remain partial. Monzo/OakNorth/Barclays profiles are explicitly thinner and25 bank actors have identity-only research.

Existing responses and counter-evidence materially improve usefulness: profitable banks are not described as distressed by default, acquired assets are not organic growth, announced capabilities are not unfilled opportunities, and completed corporate choices are not early strategic influence windows. No disclosed stock, provision, target or programme is converted into available procurement funding.

The small public FCA pilot is truthful but limited. It adds three-bank service activity, not a comprehensive Banking intelligence rollout. Broader issuer finance/relationships/News remain blocked from runtime by source/rights/acceptance boundaries.
