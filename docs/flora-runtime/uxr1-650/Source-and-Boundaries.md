## Minimal attribution

The legal notice is composed from **sources actually rendered** in the current editorial context, not every source in a dossier. Applicable FCA numerical data, BoE database data, the existing admitted ONS economic dataset and the exact eligible EAI public-sector releases retain a compact public-sector attribution and OGL v3 licence link. BoE Governor copyright and ONS Crown copyright are retained where applicable. This does not assert that every page on a government or issuer host has an OGL licence. Nationwide financial attribution is separate and does not describe the issuer's report as OGL material. Source-document links are omitted from ordinary Main; original locators, source-use decisions and licence detail remain in authorised Research/Financial inspection. No universal legal rule about all publisher links is inferred.

The existing Source ownership determines current rights and lifecycle on every applicable read. Withdrawal/revocation/version drift removes dependent private financial output, including a direct selector, without deleting the run. Unrelated BoE and FCA material remains usable. This sprint neither downloads nor reproduces whole reports and performs no new licence admission.

## Access proof

- Actual session store, offline test authority reconciliation, password login, workspace selection and resource ACL are exercised in isolated storage. Financial numbers are actual retained published facts; identities and approval fixture are explicitly fictional.
- Anonymous and forged-header users never search private runs; private query GET/HEAD and internal inspection fail closed.
- Inspect-only may read its authorised Financial run but cannot prepare or access Owner inspection.
- Foreign workspace, revoked membership, missing inspect permission and same-workspace/no-resource cannot obtain earnings or setup controls.
- `lens=inspection` retains existing Owner gate and adds its own verified read/Financial ACL; adding a private locator does not make a URL public.
- Prepare accepts only the existing CSRF field, or that field plus exact `selected=nationwide`; arbitrary source, redirect/selection and foreign Origin fail. No GET mutation.
- Context-local editorial mode resets on exceptions and is isolated across concurrent calls; it grants no data authority.
- Offers, Pipeline, matcher, source registries, Financial storage/calculation owner and security store are not modified.
