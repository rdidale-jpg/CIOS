## What this slice changes

Lloyds has an executive decision brief, an inspectable coverage inventory and a programme → capability → outcome view retaining the illustrated operating-model assets. The three issue-selected hypotheses now expose source-scoped evidence, internal/partner alternatives, counterevidence, unverified external service gaps and specific customer questions. The Opportunities page uses one concise nine-row decision register rather than another copy of the top three full descriptions. Industry and Enterprise contexts use compact links to the same source-owned hypotheses; no IDs or authored ranks are replaced.

Banking PESTLE, Five Forces and Value Chain add 19 node-specific investigation prompts and explicit pressure → capability direction, with existing evidence/node drill-down. They remain interpretations, not numeric pressure scores, proven causal edges or Lloyds facts projected onto an entire industry. Original six visual-spec contracts, living maps, AI/regulation lenses, Aviation/easyJet and the private Pipeline remain.

## Coverage and remaining knowledge deficiencies

The existing Research Intelligence dossier supplies the fourteen coverage areas; its maturity is not recertified by this change. The interface distinguishes bounded source support and historical context from interpreted/missing information and retained conflicts, with source periods and deficiencies visible.

- Business processes/capabilities: conceptual ownership is retained; actual application/process inventories, runbooks, hand-offs and accountable owners remain incomplete.
- Programmes: published direction is evidenced; named work packages, milestones, funding, procurement and delivery ownership remain unverified.
- Technology/suppliers: a dated Group platform disclosure is more specific; current vendor contracts, exclusivity, renewal and brand/subsidiary deployment scope remain unknown. The new relationship is a supported analytical association, not a canonical fact or contractual topology.
- Controls/outcomes: existing regulation and company claims remain scoped. Independent benefit attribution, customer-owned baselines and complete operating-control evidence are missing.
- Decisions/commercial intelligence: launch intentions, expected acquisition timing and actual completion are separated. Buyer authority, access, budget, demand and external service gaps are not inferred from investment priorities.

## Product walkthrough

1. Open `/enterprises/lloyds`: scan the executive brief, programme/capability associations and coverage inventory. Existing illustrated story and capability map remain available.
2. Select one of the three priority hypotheses. Inspect its qualification gap map, precise primary excerpt/date/scope, competing delivery options and next customer question. Use the original support/counterevidence and full dossier drill-downs for complete history.
3. Open `/opportunities`: compare the nine retained Lloyds questions without conflating research rank, seller fit, access or evidence.
4. Return to `/industries/banking?lens=pestle`, `?lens=porter` or `?lens=value-chain`: inspect structural pressures and investigation direction. Enterprise examples do not establish industry-wide trends.
5. Choose **Print executive brief** for the short presentation; choose **Print full evidence appendix** deliberately for full detail. Browser print defaults to the short brief where the new summary is present. The native no-JavaScript journey remains navigable; without JavaScript use ordinary detailed-page print and native disclosures, not the JavaScript print-mode buttons.
6. With an authorised identity/workspace, deliberately create/link a research record. It remains Discovered with customer validation unestablished. Existing ten-input review/confirmation gates and immutable source provenance remain in force.
