# Reusable industry source-use decision — proposed, not adopted

One genuinely authorised category/document-series decision should cover compatible measurements without a new legal approval for every cell. Each measurement still needs correct source, legal subject, period, definition, basis, precision and semantic validation. Ambiguous or incompatible uses become exceptions. This sprint defines and tests that review contract; it approves no category, issuer, source, licence, audience or purpose.

## Qualified legal/source findings

The [18 March 2026 government report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/report-and-impact-assessment-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence/report-on-copyright-and-artificial-intelligence), Section A, distinguishes facts from protected expression. It describes the broad copyright exception with opt-out as no longer the preferred approach. It is a policy report, not an enacted commercial extraction licence, issuer-contract waiver or permission change. Its own OGL does not cover bank reports.

[IPO guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exceptions-to-copyright) describes conditional non-commercial TDM and fair dealing. FLORA's sales purpose is not made non-commercial by labelling it research. Copyright does not make isolated numerical facts protected prose; equally, acquisition copies, contractual terms, database rights, systematic extraction and redistribution need separate analysis. [Database Regulation19](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3032/regulation/19) addresses lawful-user insubstantial use; [Regulation16](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3032/regulation/16) addresses repeated/systematic extraction. Indexed official extracts were read; consolidated XHTML was not readable through the tool. No proxy or complete legal reform/enforceability audit is claimed. Qualified source/legal review must resolve the exact use, including potentially applicable statutory protections; publisher consent is not asserted to be legally required for every isolated number.

The narrow proposal is **minimal factual measurements and necessary definitions/provenance, with original linking and reproducible derived views**, under an established applicable basis. It excludes full prose, graphics, report/table redistribution, AI training and recurring bulk collection unless separately resolved. Small proposed operating limits are not legal safe harbours. Permission for reading, copying/extraction, processing, storage, numerical reuse, linking, seller display and export must not be collapsed into a public-access boolean.

## Exact source/series decisions

[Register](Source-Use-Register.json) and [machine-readable proposals](Series-Use-Proposals.json) retain the original report URLs and per-use uncertainty. These five proposal records are not SourceRecords or a second rights registry.

- **NatWest:** the original investor homepage links [Group legal information](https://www.natwestgroup.com/hygiene/legal-information.html). Its use/copyright clauses restrict personal/reproduction use; no applicable commercial Financial-use basis has been established here.
- **HSBC:** [terms](https://www.hsbc.com/terms-and-conditions), copyright section, distinguish personal/product-use extracts from other uses/distribution and contain a linking restriction. The legal-bank table in a Group-hosted report does not negate those issues.
- **Santander:** [UK terms](https://www.santander.co.uk/personal/support/customer-support/legal-information), dated 29 July 2024, restrict commercial content use without a licence. Their exact applicability to the separate asset-CDN report remains unresolved; neither broad application nor a CDN exemption is assumed.
- **Nationwide:** no report-specific commercial-use basis was established. The inspected [business-site terms](https://www.nationwide.co.uk/business/terms-and-conditions) do not establish applicability to the investor PDF. A copyright footer, unrelated social-media policy or absent prohibition is not a verified licence.
- **Starling:** [terms](https://www.starlingbank.com/legal/terms-of-use/), Access/IP, restrict commercial use without a right to do so. Personal extracts and content-sharing wording do not establish the proposed Financial-run use.

The legal/source-use review is separate from current technical admission: **all five original hosts remain outside the existing positive-origin policy**. Even a future favourable lawful-use decision does not silently change that policy. Conversely, an origin that passes that policy is not automatically licensed.

## Reuse, change and lifecycle

A future approved series decision should be referenced once by many source-bound measurements. Automatic routine checks can verify compatible originals, document class, issuer/reporting subject, version, scope, dates, intended uses, audience and cumulative extraction. The current proof is a proposal/applicability validator, not recurring admission. Owner-controlled document identity/class checks remain necessary; browser-supplied category/path strings cannot approve a new source.

Changed terms, origin/path/version, source withdrawal/revocation, stale/contradicted/superseded/private state, changed purpose/audience, exceeded extraction scope or unknown third-party rights require an exception decision. Source reliability withdrawal is not the same as termination of a perpetual licence; editorial review dates are not licence expiry. Current output still fails closed through existing owners. Lawful historical retention requires its own actual basis; nothing here deletes history or invents a retention grant.

No outreach, licence purchase, paid processing, policy adoption or approval is executed. The proposed record is not legal clearance. See [the precise decisions](Owner-Decisions.md).
