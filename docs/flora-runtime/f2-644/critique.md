## Commercial assessment

Before: the actual permitted result was readable only as a standalone report. After: the same authorised persisted source data can be inspected within Banking's familiar Main, with the scale of the market stock and the small observed movement clearly distinguished. Honest zero axes make the movement visually modest; explicit difference cards explain it without pretending it is organic growth, a causal explanation, a named-bank comparison or demand.

The composition is reusable because chart/result/semantics depend on typed records and finite recipes, not bank names. The present admitted inventory is deliberately narrow. Fictional duration and bridge contracts demonstrate extension boundaries; they do not make the product a complete multi-bank analyst. Source readiness, genuine legal-entity models, richer periods/metrics and current source verification remain future reviewed work. The default view keeps methods and data detail collapsed; material historical/stock caveats remain visible. No invented commercial interpretation fills the screen.
