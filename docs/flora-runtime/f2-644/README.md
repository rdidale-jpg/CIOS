## What the owner can inspect

An authorised existing Banking Financial run adds a **Financial analysis** link inside Main. Selecting it retains the accepted country skyline, Banking portrait, contextual navigation, subject selection and source ownership. The private `analysis` locator is not part of the anonymous route catalogue. The existing `/ai-financial-report/{run_id}` remains available for detail. Forces and scene navigation continue to work; browser history restores the Financial focus.

The actual permitted data is F1's two BoE LPMZ598 household retail-deposit stock observations: 31 May 2026 **£1,884,726m** and 30 June 2026 **£1,886,281m**. The owner calculates **£1,555m (£1.555bn)** and **0.082505361522046…% (displayed 0.0825%)**. A zero-inclusive GBP billion chart compares the levels; it deliberately does not exaggerate the small movement. Exact source-scale values and reproducible methods are expandable.

These are historical UK MFI sector stocks, excluding notes and coin, not seasonally adjusted. They are not Lloyds or another named bank, the latest release, organic growth, an official adjusted flow, buyer demand or a sales opportunity. Original publication day/frozen release vintage remain unverified; no invented date or original-byte archive is supplied.

Only this real dataset is available. Synthetic bank income, mutual reconciliation, Technology/Aviation monetary statements and an assumption scenario exercise the same component contracts in tests only. No synthetic example becomes runtime intelligence. Passenger metrics, full platform economics, peer ranking, FX and five-bank admission are not implemented.
