Those artefacts are **review inputs**, not new canonical objects or a parallel research infrastructure.

## Four-step analytical discipline

1. **Reported fact:** exact source, party, period/definition, extent and date. Retained facts are not fresh retrieval and a snapshot digest is not original-byte verification.
2. **Mechanism/interpretation:** label conceptual causal structure and competing explanations. A flow arrow is not an Observation or confirmed enterprise relationship.
3. **Hypothesis:** identify what could matter, existing management responses, counter-evidence and a falsifier. None establishes buyer demand or an open decision.
4. **Qualification:** remains with existing owners. ADR-026 requires governed input, enterprise applicability, pressure semantics, identity/singularity, material consequence and lifecycle/conflict handling. This docs-only tranche executes none of those promotion gates and creates no Pipeline record.
