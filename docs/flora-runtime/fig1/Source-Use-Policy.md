# Proposed industry-wide source-use policy

**Proposal only. No source, publisher, licence, purpose, audience or ADR is approved here.** Use the existing Source owners and rights/permission boundaries, not another registry or role framework. A source category can define repeatable validation and an approved lawful-use basis; classification alone grants nothing.

## Legal/evidence findings

UK IPO guidance distinguishes factual results from protected expression, and distinguishes lawful reading from permission to make copies for analysis. Its [Research guidance, printed9](https://www.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/359251/Exceptions_to_copyright_-_Research.pdf) states that copyright protects expression rather than facts. This supports challenging a presumption that every isolated financial number is prohibited or needs publisher consent. It does **not** resolve acquisition copies, systematic extraction, contract, database, retention or public-display rights by itself.

The [IPO exceptions guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/exceptions-to-copyright) and [CDPA29A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/29A) concern text/data analysis for **non-commercial** research with lawful access. FLORA's commercial sales analysis is not made non-commercial by calling it internal research. Quotation/fair dealing is contextual, not a blanket scraper, full-table redistribution or training licence. Applicable exceptions can affect contract enforceability; do not assume either that every website term overrides statute or that every commercial use is permitted.

[Database Regulations, PartIII](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1997/3032/part/III) distinguish substantial extraction/re-utilisation, repeated systematic extraction and a lawful user's insubstantial use. No numerical threshold makes a repeated industry collection automatically safe. Database copyright/right, contractual restrictions and protected expression require separate assessment. No legal conclusion about an entire issuer corpus is supplied.

[OGLv3](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) can support commercial reuse of covered information with attribution and applicable conditions/exclusions. It does not cover everything on an official website or grant third-party/personal-data/logo rights. Source-specific evidence matters: [FCA legal2.2](https://www.fca.org.uk/legal) applies the licence to specified FCA-owned Data materials;2.3 leaves narrative outside that grant. That is not permission for NatWest or other banks.

**Review extent:** original IPO guidance/PDF and FCA terms were reopened16 November2026. Official legislation indexed extracts plus IPO guidance were available; direct legislative XHTML/robot and local network limitations prevented a full consolidated-text review. National Archives licence/guidance direct reads returned403; no proxy/mirror was used, and complete current OGL wording was not independently re-verified. Source-specific existing grants and official references were examined. This is a grounded design/legal decision framework, not a blanket legal opinion, complete reform-status audit or new licence. Qualified source/legal review is needed before adopting new commercial category defaults. [Ledger](Source-Ledger.json) records extent honestly.

## Eight dimensions in one compact source-owner decision

| Dimension | Decision/evidence required | Simplification |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Lawful public/licensed access; verified original origin, safe transport, authentication limits | Reuse verified publisher/profile provenance; no paywall/robot/redirect circumvention |
| Extraction/internal processing | Relevant permission/exception for intended automated/manual processing and purpose | Approved category/series basis reused when applicable, not a fresh legal review per cell |
| Storage/retention | Permitted original/excerpt/measurement retention, audience and duration; honest actual capture/hash | Capture once where allowed; keep only necessary artefacts; never fake a full archive |
| Factual numerical reuse | Applicable factual-use/legal/database/contract basis, extraction extent and frequency | Reuse exact approved use across many located measurements; facts not equated with copied prose |
| Attribution/linking | Actual publisher/issuer/title/date/location and licence/attribution conditions; link terms where relevant | Generate references from existing source metadata, without pretending citation creates permission |
| Seller/public display | Exact audience and allowed outputs, export/aggregation restrictions and legal scope | Data-driven authorised analytical view; source public status does not authorise private tenant data |
| Copyrighted expression redistribution | Separate permission for prose, tables, charts, images/logos or substantial datasets | Default omit expression/full-document republication from factual views; express exceptions reviewed |
| Revocation/expiry | Decision/use revision, source withdrawal, licence conditions, reliability and editorial review | Revalidate changed dependency once in existing owner, invalidate downstream views/caches/exports; retain lawful history |

These are fields/purposes of one existing-owner policy, not eight new engines or repeated clicks. Rights basis, factual verification and canonical status remain separate axes. An enabled SourceRecord, acquisition receipt, AI confidence or successful QA is not permission.

## Category defaults and exceptions — proposed, not enacted

| Source category | Safe reusable default **after applicable policy/basis is approved** | Source-specific exception / stop |
|---|---|---|
| Company annual/interim reports | Verify official issuer/report/version; extract bounded attributed numerical measurements and definitions into analytical candidates under the applicable lawful-use basis; retain minimum permitted provenance | Restrictive/unclear terms, third-party embedded content, substantial repeated extraction, redistribution/retention limits; no presumption that public access is unrestricted |
| Regulatory filings/numerical disclosures | Verify issuer, filing identity/version, scope and actual regulator reuse terms; same financial definitions apply | Regulator custody is not a new licence to third-party expression. SEC restrictions remain unchanged; no relabelling/mirror workaround |
| Government/official statistics | When actual content is covered by a verified applicable open licence, automate attribution and scope checks for permitted data uses | ONS/other current admission restrictions remain. Third-party content, survey estimates/rights exceptions and method/date boundaries need review |
| Company announcements/investor presentations | Official original, exact dated claims, management target versus actual and limited permissible factual reuse | Promotional prose, copyrighted graphics, undisclosed source-use basis, forward targets presented as outcomes; source-type context is not canonical foundation eligibility |
| Open-data sources | Verify exact licence/version, permitted commercial uses, attribution, share-alike/database conditions and publisher authority | An open landing page or unofficial copy is not an open licence; incompatible terms or absent rights evidence stop use |
| Third-party market research | Licensed/expressly permitted small factual analysis with exact market definition, estimate method, period and audience | Paywall/scraping/redistribution/derived-data restrictions; unsupported estimates, authorisation unavailable or scope unclear |
| Licensed/restricted content | Apply the actual subscription/contract/audience/processing/retention/derived-output rights | No acquisition beyond licence, cross-tenant sharing, unauthorised provider upload or public seller republication |

**No blanket company permission is recommended.** A qualified source/legal owner can approve a bounded category/document-series policy where the applicable basis is genuinely established. Unchanged reports meeting that policy can then be checked automatically; changed terms, content class, jurisdiction, audience, extraction volume or missing authority become exceptions. The alternative is not a new legal decision for every chart cell.

The current hard host list protects origin safety but is too coarse to represent all eight uses. The next design should let existing source profiles/reference metadata carry the actual approved decision and allowed purpose/audience, while retaining host/path/redirect safety. Do not broadly add issuers or use a permissive callback. FIG-1 changes no source policy; all five financial originals remain unadmitted by the current positive publisher test and no new factual-use grant is inferred.

## Lifecycle and permission nuance

Editorial freshness/review expiry is not licence expiry. Withdrawal of a report can change reliability/current presentation without necessarily terminating an existing perpetual licence. A CIOS source/use revocation must stop current dependent use according to the actual decision; retention of historical records depends on the applicable licence/policy and legal obligations. Preserve source/claim history, contradictions and supersession instead of silently rewriting or deleting it. OGL-style conditions, contractual termination, source withdrawal and epistemic staleness must not collapse into one boolean.

Resolve source/use and private scope before facts, derived values, titles, counts, exports or caches are emitted. Keep dependency/source-use revisions in reproducibility metadata. Recheck at use/execution; a previously successful cached analysis must not confer present authority. Existing lifecycle helper inconsistencies are explicit implementation work, not permission to use restricted material.
