# Five banking exemplars: analyst breadth without false comparability

These are bounded research/design examples using authentic original sources and preserved BIE2/BIE3 references. They are not newly accepted Evidence, source permissions, full-bank research or a live seller release. [Source ledger](Source-Ledger.json) records dates, locations, capture limits and unresolved use. [Structured examples](Banking-Exemplars.json) preserve exact selected measurements. All five financial originals remain denied by the current positive publisher policy; no bypass is supplied.

## The financial analyst specification

| Dimension | Required analytical output | Definition checks |
|---|---|---|
| A Income/profitability | Signed income/cost/credit/PBT/PAT bridges; relevant return measures | Statutory/underlying/adjusted, reported/constant-currency, net/gross |
| B Balance sheet | Assets/liabilities/equity and material lending positions | As-at date, accounting carrying value, legal/consolidated scope |
| C Deposits/lending/funding | Mix, maturity, stocks and clearly labelled movements | Total/customer/retail/ex-central definitions; gross/net; acquisition/FX effects |
| D Capital/liquidity/risk | CET1, leverage, LCR/NSFR, RWA and asset-quality context | Regulatory perimeter, averages versus period-end, denominators |
| E Revenue/cost structure | Spread/fee/other-income and segment economics, ordinary costs versus exceptional charges | Reported definition, cost allocation, gross versus net benefit |
| F Credit impairment | Charge versus allowance, coverage and risk-cost effects | Signed flow/stock, acquired day-one charges, denominator and horizon |
| G Cash flow | Genuine cash-flow evidence where meaningful, plus bank funding/liquidity analysis | Deposit growth, liquidity stock and maturity tables are not operating CF or industrial FCF |
| H Investment/transformation | Incurred expense/capitalised investment, commitments, targets and realised benefit | Intragroup versus external spend; no automatic supplier budget or benefit attribution |
| I Trends | Within-issuer same-definition series and disclosed bridges | Duration, restatement, acquisition, FX and organic/total distinctions |
| J Comparison | Matched measures or explicitly reconciled side-by-side context | Entity/perimeter/currency/unit/basis/time/business model; no unsupported league table |

The [50-cell dimension assessment](Analysis-Specification.json) covers A–J for all five banks using selected-verified, partial, located-not-reconciled, unavailable-in-selected-material and bridge-required dispositions. These are honest dimension slices, not a completeness percentage or a claim that every metric has been researched. Full capital/cash-flow/segment/investment reconstruction remains open where shown.

## NatWest Group: deposit meaning before financial interpretation

**Source→fact:** original [Q3 statement](https://investors.natwestgroup.com/~/media/Files/R/RBS-IR-V2/results-center/30102026/nwg-q3-2026-results.pdf), PDFpp4–5/printed3–4. Group operating PBT£6,543m for9M2026; Group deposits£454.3bn total versus£453.4bn excluding central items at30 September2026. Existing BIE3 references remain unchanged.

**Verified metric→calculation/estimate:** normalise the same reported PBT as `6543 GBP million × 1,000,000 = 6,543,000,000 GBP` for a comparable scale/display. This is a representation of one measurement, not another fact requiring acceptance. Distinguish the two deposit-stock definitions; no organic inflow, bank forecast, Treasury-only attribution or supplier budget is inferred. The broader CAL acquisition comparator is different again and must not be called deposit growth.

**Analytical view:** Group scale and earnings can be understood immediately with source and definition detail on demand. Existing strong earnings/capabilities constrain generic distress narratives. **Optional durable knowledge:** select reusable source-reported baseline measurements only through existing owners; neither the comparison paragraph nor a chart point needs its own accepted Observation.

**Current friction:** issuer admission/use unresolved; deposit metric and instant-date support missing; unapproved canonical IDs cannot be guessed; bespoke public release wiring would require implementation. Those barriers do not prove a general doctrine that every attributed view must first become canonical.

**Calculations:** within the stated Group definitions, revenue movement is `887.4−940.0=−52.6m`; PBT movement `217.1−223.4=−6.3m`. **Current friction/gaps:** source/use unresolved; selected HTML lacks complete bank/Engine financial split, impairment/capital and cash-flow data. Publication-time surplus commentary is not a verified period-end CET1 measure or unrestricted spending authority.

## Comparisons that are allowed now, and those refused

The research permits a **qualitative comparison of economic mechanisms**, with source-specific scope/year/currency shown: large-bank earnings, legal-bank USD reporting, acquired integration, mutual member distributions and platform economics. It does not justify ranking their profitability, liquidity, growth or supplier opportunity from these unmatched selected figures.

For any numerical peer comparison, acquire/reconcile the same perimeter, period, reporting basis, currency method and denominator first. If unavailable, show scoped facts separately or omit the calculation; do not fabricate an EST label to legitimise missing inputs. Bank-specific estimates are intentionally not invented. The separate synthetic proof demonstrates a visible assumption-led estimate without implying any real bank need or forecast.
