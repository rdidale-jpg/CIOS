## Implemented analysis

There are **44 real dated measurements** in 22 year-paired lines, through one finite Source-bound selection. The [retained minimal record](../../../enterprise-knowledge/banking/research-model/source-snapshots/EEI2B-Nationwide-AR26.json) contains exact locations, dates, units, basis, perimeter, original signed displays and sealed factual bindings. The verifier matched every selected monetary pair against the two published Group columns. The risk statement separately supplies reported capital and leverage ratios, with their PRA basis.

- **Income.** Statutory Group net interest income, total income, administrative expenses, pretax and after-tax results. Total statutory income for FY2026 is £6,379m. The existing £6,378m underlying presentation remains separately labelled. The consolidated flows for FY2025 start on 5 April 2024, cover 361 days and include six months of Virgin Money. The view shows reported periods side by side and rejects a like-for-like annual growth calculation.
- **Balance sheet.** Compare like-for-like date conventions for Group assets, cash and central-bank balances, customer loans and debt securities. F1 difference, with line revisions, returns +£14,451m, +£8,928m, +£10,594m and +£3,712m respectively from 31 March 2025 to 31 March 2026. These are shifts in reported closing balances. The reported CET1 and leverage ratios use regulatory rather than statutory accounting definitions. The prior-year restatement in note 1 applies to the Society column, which is outside this view.
- **Cash flow.** The FY2026 Group reports £3,904m operating, £2,331m investing and £2,124m financing inflows. The signed investing and funding evidence includes property, plant and equipment purchases, financing issuance and repayments. Investing inflows include maturities of securities and the previous year includes the acquisition cash line. The view does not compute a cash conversion, return on capital or free cash flow ratio without a supported definition and comparable inputs.
- **Insight.** Existing mode control switches to the revision-bound balance changes and a statement supported by the current cash subtotals. The useful investigation is which cash movements recur and how much depends on assets or funding. The report supplies no separate authority for enterprise-specific demand, Material Pressure qualification, technology investment benefit or procurement.

## Ownership and critique

The existing Source import owner accepts one **exact document path** on the already-recognised Nationwide host. Its finite statement selector binds the reviewed file, Source ID, official URL and reporting subject. It rejects unknown keys, symlinks and wrong provenance. Financial's EEI-2A reader rechecks ordinary-use scope, source/row integrity and actual statement periods on each read. Current source withdrawal removes the full annual supplement and any derived outputs while the existing eligible preliminary view remains available. No new private selection, route, registry, calculation formula, canonical or Pipeline write channel.

The shared annual composition has no issuer-specific template. The Source-to-view contract can take another eligible Source selection once it exists. This draft proves one real issuer. Prior EEI-2A two-issuer synthetic coverage remains a separate control; technical block cases here are not evidence of real second-issuer support. A one-document FY2026 model is deliberately bounded. To add interim observations, the next increment needs dates in columns as distinct periods and audited-vs-unaudited distinction. To add a second actual issuer, resolve a named Source issue, with no need to change the renderer or repeat discretionary per-value decisions. ADR-027 remains Proposed.
