# FLORA-UX-UNIFY-1 · Facts first

Implements [#670](https://github.com/rdidale-jpg/CIOS/issues/670) and the owner-approved [experience contract](../../product/flora-experience/FLORA-Experience-Contract-v1.0.md) on verified main `b2c7f168b4ff7b42a4518cf9ab9fed0aeda22618`.

**Try it:** UK Landscape → Banking → Payments & settlement. Facts follows initiation, clearing and settlement. FLORA Insight follows the same subject through customer principal, service economics and continuity. The flow is labelled conceptual in each mode. Open Nationwide Group earnings and compare both financial bases. Then open Banking Forces and follow the historical provider development to Lloyds Inside → Technology & data. Changing level or selecting a new subject resets the reading mode to Facts. Reload and history restore explicit Insight links.

The existing Top control changes perspective. A native segmented navigation at the top of Main changes the reading only. Finite `reading=insight` serialises explicit insight, and absent or explicit `reading=facts` selects Facts. Compatible perspective changes preserve the reading mode. Unsupported detail or agenda resets restore Facts with a new Main heading. UK and Banking have Landscape and Forces. Lloyds has Landscape, Inside and Forces. Original paintings and responsive scene geometry are retained. The payment and capability sequences extend Main using the same palette. A permanent change view remains contingent on adequate eligible dated content.

Nationwide is an enterprise financial selection through the existing Banking Financial route. The scope trail and period identify it. A new standalone Nationwide route, general Enterprise coverage, dedicated contemporary change views and enriched easyJet art remain future work.
