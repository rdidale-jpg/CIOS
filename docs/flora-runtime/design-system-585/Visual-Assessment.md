# Visual assessment and editorial accounting

The system now shares a warm paper background, forest-green text, editorial serif headings, readable sans-serif body, rounded native actions and one compact header. The visual world, industry illustration, shallow comparison banner, enterprise facts and seller workspace use appropriate densities within that language. No new artwork was commissioned: all approved images are byte-preserved, with lightweight CSS feathering/blending only.

The Enterprise archetype is deliberately editorial. NVIDIA needs commercially substantial information, not a speculative organisational illustration. It retains the complete financial/strategy/pressure/relationship/programme content and three existing sales hypotheses while removing repeated source disclosures and duplicated section/global navigation. The Equinix model is the bounded demonstration of reusable image-native model interaction; other analytical SVGs retain their existing meanings.

## Acceptance focus and limitations

- Desktop and landscape iPad keep the approved world/image dominant. Portrait iPad retains natural aspect ratio and calm space; inspect that balance with real device text size.
- Phone Home folds the same full-economy illustration into two halves. Phone Technology reframes the conceptual campus and keeps all eight labelled controls on-image. No extra legend/navigation list is added below it.
- PESTLE keeps six labelled controls available around the selected note at all five sizes. Source detail remains in the shared header, with preserved original review cutoffs and country scope.
- Financial values retain global/UK, period, currency and metric basis. The visible industry shares belong to broader SIC sections; the source disclosure explains why no narrower Technology/Banking/Aviation subtotal is claimed.
- Private screenshots use **synthetic company, sellers, approvals, proof references and demonstration Pipeline records**. They are not real commercial relationships, customer intent or approved live offers. Authority/HTTPS/CSRF are exercised through the real local paths, not disabled.
- Mandatory storage/security warnings, employer/private scope, review controls, uncertainty that changes a decision, and stage-versus-evidence distinctions remain. This sprint does not upgrade research or configure production readiness.

Chromium emulation is not physical iPad/Safari, VoiceOver/NVDA, high zoom, colour-perception, slow-network or production-performance certification. Those remain owner/manual acceptance checks. No new art, international research, agents or paid runtime model is activated. Wider specialist/editorial refinement can follow measured owner feedback; this draft does not claim every historic research body has been rewritten.
