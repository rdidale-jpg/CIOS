## Acceptance walkthrough

1. Compare desktop Home and both iPads. Confirm the complete 20-sector UK world, five restrained benchmark shares and £2.62tn GVA context survive, with only Technology/Banking/Aviation clickable. There are no legacy briefing modules or bottom navigation.
2. Open Technology from its image. Eight names are visible initially; on phone they remain on-image rather than becoming a duplicate list. Use keyboard/touch. Check the narrower researched industry is not equated with the broader Information & communication GVA share.
3. Open Frontier and sort/search. The shallow industry banner stays; only company names navigate from rows. UK values remain sourced/EST-qualified or blank. Open the single header Sources control to inspect full methods and preserved source periods.
4. Open NVIDIA. Review At a glance, Commercial ecosystem and Potential sales plays. Global annual/quarterly figures remain separate from UK operating metrics. Every original relationship/programme remains; three hypotheses name buyer roles and do not silently assign a buyer account or create a Pipeline record.
5. Choose PESTLE from the header in one action. Select all six factors without scrolling to a detached explanation. Close/escape the note, inspect optional sources and country relevance, and check the other factors remain unobscured. Open Inside Equinix to try the same restrained native pattern on its existing two conceptual areas.
6. In the synthetic seller workspace, open Offers, switch to account-led discovery and select Lloyds. Read the same match/entry play and open its complete uncertainty, proof, source lineage/cutoff/policy from Sources & method. The match is not a customer requirement or approved deal.
7. Follow **Open manual Pipeline qualification**. This is a GET to the existing form, not an automatic save or copied offer. Inspect original editable fields, qualification/evidence versus stage, version/CSRF controls and demonstration warning. The seeded synthetic record remains Discovered; user confirmation and valid evidence are still required for later actions.
8. Open Explore: existing employer/offer/review/Studio, live Pipeline, contextual and specialist routes remain addressable. Check workspace switching/sign-out and confirm signed-out users cannot read Offers/Pipeline. Advanced destinations retain their own permissions.
9. Compare phone 390/320, landscape/portrait iPad and print. Check labels, table scroll region, focus/skip and required alerts. Physical iPad/Safari, screen readers, high zoom and slow networks remain manual acceptance checks.
