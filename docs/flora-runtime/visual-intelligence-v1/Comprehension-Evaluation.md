# Executive comprehension evaluation

**Status: unmeasured. No human participant has completed this protocol.** Browser automation verifies behaviour, not understanding. The 30–60 second overview is a design target; neither a speed improvement nor superior comprehension has been demonstrated.

## Matched conditions

Use the pinned 10 November 2026 assessment, v3 review and presentation profile for each enterprise. Record the three input digests from the visual’s snapshot disclosure. Give both conditions the **same facts, source passages, dates, Unknowns and conceptual labels**; exclude information unique to either presentation. The conventional condition is an equivalent text dossier assembled from those records, not a deliberately impoverished competitor. Existing-main screenshots illustrate the interface change but are not a controlled comprehension experiment.

Recruit 6–10 executive, research or strategic-sales users who were not involved in building the feature. Obtain consent and do not use customer/private information. Randomly counterbalance text-first versus visual-first and Lloyds-first versus easyJet-first. Give a short neutral navigation introduction. Use matched, different questions in each condition to reduce recall effects. Keep device, viewport, time allowance and source access identical. Include landscape tablet users and keyboard users.

## Tasks and scoring

| Task | Example prompt | Accuracy rubric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industry structure | Name three participants and two structural pressures; identify their geographic scope. | One point per correct, scoped answer; no point for invented rank or relationship. |
| Enterprise operation | Explain how customer activity connects to three core activities and one supporting capability. | Correct business purposes and conceptual flow; explicitly distinguish modelled structure from verified implementation. |
| Dependencies | Find a supplier disclosure and establish what it does **not** prove. | Supplier, passage, reporting period, historical/current distinction and unknown activity-level dependency. |
| Evidence status | Distinguish a supported report, an interpretation, a scenario and a conflict. | Correct classification, with the source or missing evidence needed for each. |
| Change and gaps | Find a dated commitment and an undated research gap; explain readiness. | Accurate timing/precision; no inferred completion, fabricated expiry, current failure or buying intent. |

Record elapsed seconds from prompt to submitted answer, task completion, source-navigation errors, classification errors and confidence (1–5). Ask the overview question after 60 seconds, then allow deeper investigation separately. Score answers blind to condition against a prewritten answer key reviewed for source scope. Record qualitative observations without interpreting navigation speed as commercial correctness.

## Analysis and reporting

Report participant count, device/role mix, order allocation, exclusions and incomplete tasks. Compare paired median time **and** accuracy, including dispersion and individual results; do not declare improvement when faster answers have more unsupported assumptions. With this small exploratory sample, avoid population claims. Report qualitative confusion, particularly equal-sized actors, conceptual arrows, publication versus go-live dates and supplier history. Refine the design only from observed issues, then retest with new matched tasks.
