Scale, scope & sources

United Kingdom · 2024 · GVA at current basic prices. £2.62tn is a rounded display of the ONS £2,623bn total. GVA is output less intermediate consumption; it is neither company revenue nor GDP at market prices.

District shares are the published 2024 weights divided by ten, not estimates of sales. Rounded sections total 100.2%; no normalisation is applied. Real estate includes imputed owner-occupier rents. Area and building size are illustrative, not a quantitative map.

SIC 2007 matches these national-accounts data; the newer SIC 2026 is not silently mixed in. All domestic sections A–T appear. U is extraterritorial activity and is outside the published domestic denominator.

Technology, Banking and Aviation are researched subsets or cross-sector views. Their links do not claim coverage of their whole host section. Shares belong to Information & communication, Finance & insurance and Transport & storage respectively. No unsupported sub-sector share, employment or growth is shown.

Solid green labels open existing research. Other district labels are inert. The illustration is conceptual geography, with no claimed corporate relationships. No personalised company information is read by these screens.
