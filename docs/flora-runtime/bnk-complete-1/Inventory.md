## Comparative synthesis for the first release

| Industry mechanism | Existing evidence and analytical finding | Limitation and next measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Funding and credit intermediation | BoE benchmark rates give policy context. Nationwide FY2026 retained results show income, operating expense and impairment working through to profit, with acquisition and member-value effects. Compare with the Lloyds Group intermediation and service model; retain different ownership and disclosure bases. | Obtain comparable net interest margin and deposit pass-through by segment, with hedges and balance-sheet scope. Do not claim a uniform sensitivity or count consolidation as organic growth. |
| Payments, switching and servicing | Payments operators, settlement systems and bank participants occupy different layers. BIE-2 separately tracks CASS, FCA and FOS. Legal-bank FCA counts establish service activity, alongside a switching mechanism that supports buyer choice. | Align customer cohorts and denominators before assessing outcomes. Use volumes, scheme access and fee economics at matching layers. A conceptual payment flow is not a contract. |
| Differentiated entry and substitutes | Mutuals, universal banks, specialists/digital challengers and payment-led banks compete at different points in the value system. BIE-2's ClearBank candidate differentiates integration and clearing from mortgage lending. Payments and wallet providers can substitute at the interface without replacing all regulated functions. | Test switching, direct scheme participation, funding/regulatory barriers and wallet distribution as separate mechanisms. Verify market share and intended customer segment before judging rivalry or entry. |
| Supplier leverage and resilience | Treasury designations and Lloyds' disclosure establish different types of relationship. They justify studying concentration, substitutability and continuity alongside existing controls. | Verify actual services, workload, operating alternatives and legal endpoints; a designated provider count cannot measure concentration or supplier power. |

## Source-directed analytical gap closure

1. **Payments and customer value.** Reuse BIE-2 and the payments Twin. Check Pay.UK reports and scheme participation; BoE RTGS/CHAPS reports; PSR card market reviews, FCA/PRA registers, CASS and FCA outcomes. Build an origination → processing → clearing → settlement → servicing map, marking an operator, standard, system, participant and customer separately. Reconcile volumes to the same payment rail and date; capture customer mix, switching frictions and perimeters. Missing: fee/volume yield, owner of key operating capabilities, documented customer endpoints.
2. **Economics and competition.** Start with BIE-2/BIE-3 measures and unchanged BoE/Nationwide slices. Source comparable net interest income, funding pass-through, impairment, fees, expenses, CET1/RWA and stock/flow bases from reviewed reports. Separate spread, fee, infrastructure and platform business models; measure profit pools only where allocation and comparable coverage support them. For Five Forces, seek switching/entry evidence, seller concentration, substitutability and supplier dependence; each inference needs counter-evidence and time horizon. Compare across models rather than treat one exemplar as sector evidence.
3. **Provider exposure and operating model.** Existing Treasury and Lloyds facts first. Seek legal provider accounts and governance disclosures, bank-reported programme and exact service statements, and Pay.UK scheme and corporate disclosures. Investigate live/adopted versus announced stage, service criticality, substitutability, named people and their actual accountabilities, material vendors and observed remedies. Missing: verified Google legal contracting entity, workload, criticality, provider UK costs and decision ownership. Record scoped unknowns.
4. **External environment.** Reuse macro PESTLE and Banking PESTLE. Pair dated monetary policy, prudential/conduct requirements, workforce and digital access, technology/AI/cyber, connectivity/energy dependencies and financed/operational climate exposure with actual applicability and counterexamples. Capture primary source dates, jurisdiction, applicable party and release revision. Source/use review only for genuine exceptions; established factual reuse proceeds via its current contract.

Any seller hypothesis stays in authorised investigation with a plausible mechanism, existing response, contrary evidence, decision owner class, timing and a falsifier. A document that disproves the premise ends or revises the case. Customer demand, spend and seller fit require distinct evidence.
