## Evidence and current-state limits

The [machine-readable evidence register](../../../enterprise-knowledge/aviation/research-model/Assessment-Overlay.json) contains 22 claims, 14 source records, 19 framework assessments, nine conceptual capabilities and six programme signals. Thirteen short source-excerpt records are retained: eleven direct official-document renderings and two official search-index extracts. Original HTML/PDF bytes are not archived; hashes cover only retained text, never unavailable originals. Exact short quotations and page/section locators allow reinspection. Publication dates remain Unknown when not established; retrieval dates do not substitute for them.

Primary publishers: easyJet/easyJet holidays (including its investor CDN), IATA, UK CAA, UK Department for Transport and EASA. The register links to each official document and distinguishes:

- FY2025 actuals and dated fleet/network counts from FY2026 quarterly signals and future targets.
- IATA's June **2026 forecast** from actual market results; UK airport journeys from unique passengers or global revenue.
- Historical 2023 maintenance and 2024 operational-AI reports from current contract scope and adoption.
- October 2026 conversational-channel launch from announced 2027–2028 connectivity rollout.
- Management disclosures from customer-verified demand and analyst interpretations.

Specific gaps: direct FY2026 H1/Q3 releases, their original publication dates, any final-year results, latest ownership/approval status and decision rights, measured operational outcomes, current supplier contracts, eligibility and residual external demand. The FY2026 Q3 investor page renders an empty dynamic shell; only its indexed official excerpt was retained. The 9 November holidays article was also available only in the official search index. EUROCONTROL's autumn forecast returned HTTP503 and remains citation-only, with no supported numerical claim. The older acquisition update is not evidence that the transaction has completed.

The existing offline importer accepts verified downloaded HTML/PDF/text with provenance attestation and exact reviewed quotations (`tools/import_flora_primary_source.py --help`). New official publishers are explicitly allowlisted. Imports remain proposals and never qualify sales or update accepted intelligence automatically. No network restriction was bypassed.

## Cross-industry reuse

Banking data, original facts and research history are unchanged. Both industries use `load_assessment`, schema version 1, stable candidate identities, SourceSegment, CandidateClaim, EvidencePackageItem, claim validation, temporal metadata, graph relationships, research ranking and commercial capture. A finite repository-owned registry supplies routes, snapshot boundaries and public enterprise references; arbitrary slugs/paths do not create an industry or authority. The existing Explorer shell, diagram pattern, breadcrumbs, source cards, account controls and Pipeline service are reused.

New reusable pieces are the industry catalogue, a data-oriented sector presentation and eight-activity linked operating-model SVG with mobile equivalents. Banking's existing presentation stays compatible; the new operating-model renderer also accepts its existing value-chain data unchanged. Seller fit is an optional fourth commercial dimension, leaving Banking's existing three-dimension ranking intact.

Genuine sector content: flight/crew operations, fleet/MRO, airports/air navigation, holiday fulfilment and fuel/assurance responsibilities. Safety/airworthiness decisions remain with responsible humans and certified processes; a roadmap is not approval for autonomous operations. UK SAF duties apply to qualifying fuel suppliers, not automatically every airline. Neither distinction warrants a duplicate research or permission engine.

Expansion method: register the sector and stable candidate asset IDs; retrieve and locate primary evidence; distinguish actuals, forecasts, plans and unknowns; assess forces and value activities; link supported facts and explicitly interpretive edges; rank propositions across the four separate criteria; validate residual need, sponsor and sourcing access before the existing qualification workflow. Wider industry sources must support industry-wide conclusions—an enterprise announcement alone cannot establish a sector trend.
