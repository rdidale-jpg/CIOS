## A peer comparison that changes the Lloyds conversation

NatWest Retail reports improved deposit hedge income alongside mortgage-margin pressure, with broadly stable deposits and ISA/current-account growth offset by other savings reductions. That **challenges a uniquely Lloyds-specific deposit or margin-crisis thesis**. Lloyds' disciplined savings pricing and Group funding growth need a cohort-specific test, not a generic retention platform pitch.

NatWest's higher Retail impairment reflects unsecured growth/seasoning; it also reports stable arrears/default trends and scenario/model effects. This is a competing mechanism, **not evidence that Lloyds shares the same exact portfolio or cause**. It reinforces decomposing Lloyds' own charge before alleging credit-system failure. NatWest's source-defined CIR excludes litigation/conduct; it cannot be placed beside Lloyds' remediation-inclusive ratio as a winner/loser measure.

## Economics, value chain and Five Forces — interpretation

Value creation: funding/savings and capital → product/origination/underwriting → assets/services/payments → credit/claims/service outcomes → spread/fee/insurance earnings after funding, operating and capital constraints. The mix explains why income and cost ratios need different denominators. A customer deposit or AUM stock is not revenue; a deferred-profit stock is not external budget.

- **Rivalry:** reported mortgage-margin/hedge and savings-mix mechanisms establish testable pricing/retention trade-offs; they do not measure a current product-market share or supplier opportunity.
- **Entry:** deposit-taking licensing/capital/liquidity/service trust differ from specialist fintech or nonbank distribution. No current entry-cost curve or arbitrary barrier score is acquired.
- **Substitutes:** alternative savings/investment, market funding and wallet channels differ by use. Curve completion is an existing decision, not proof of open strategic procurement or measured substitution.
- **Supplier power:** disclosed platforms enable capabilities; actual contractual concentration, workshare, exit cost and bargaining leverage remain unknown. CTP oversight is an existing system response.
- **Buyer power:** household pricing/service choice differs from corporate treasury mandates. Balances, recommendations and complaints are different evidence; no causal elasticity is invented.

**PESTLE transmission:** political/financial-services policy remains context, not allocated budgets. Economic rates/hedges/pricing and vehicle residual values have observed issuer-specific mechanisms. Social/customer-service outcomes require exact firm/product/cohort evidence; national averages cannot prove individual harm. Technology includes current internal/incumbent capabilities and unknown net journey benefits. Legal reporting/CTP/ring-fencing scope must be tied to the actual entity/service/effective rule; no compliance breach is alleged. Environmental subsidence/portfolio-transition mechanisms require exact portfolio and existing-response evidence; no universal bank ESG need is admitted. Macro and20-sector RIE-1 sources remain pinned candidates, not silently updated or promoted.

**Relational SWOT:** reported profitability, existing controls and internal capability are strength/counter-case context. No unproved internal weakness is asserted. Strategic opportunity hypotheses concern specific residual service/asset/measurement choices. Pricing/credit/operational exposures are conditional threats tempered by hedges, stable performance and existing remedies. Research missingness is a weakness in the research, not automatically in Lloyds.
