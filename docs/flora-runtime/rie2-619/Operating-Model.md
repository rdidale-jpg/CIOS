## Value creation and customer operations

- Retail: deposits, current accounts, mortgages, cards/unsecured, motor finance/leasing and European mortgages/savings. Pricing/funding/origination → ongoing service and credit/asset decisions → interest/fees less funding, credit, depreciation, operating and capital consequences. A current-account journey is not the whole Retail P&L.
- Commercial: BCB/CIB management serves SME/corporate/institutional relationships, lending, working capital, transaction banking and risk products. Origination/credit/treasury/payment/servicing choices have different owners and permissions; exact unit-to-legal allocation remains unknown.
- IP&I: wealth/pensions/protection, customer assets, insurance service and deferred-profit release. SchrodersPersonalWealth acquisition/current Lloyds Wealth is an existing integration response, not new open procurement. Group/segment/legal dividends and customer funds are different economics.
