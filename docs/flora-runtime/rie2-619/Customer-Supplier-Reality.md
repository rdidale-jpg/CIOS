## A measured service question, not invented dissatisfaction

FCA published its2026H1 firm returns on22 October 2026 (**R2-FCA-H126**). For **Lloyds Bank PLC, January–June 2026, all product groups**, Open/Closed tables show **118,762 received /118,861 closed**. The separate closure tables show55.25% within3days and31.49% after3days but within8weeks; Upheld shows66.12% upheld by the firm. Closure/uphold denominators concern closed complaints, not a matched incoming cohort or number of customers. Counts establish complaint-handling work and upheld cases, not every complaint's harm, deterioration, repeat failure, unit cost or a current procurement need.

The Consumer Credit table instead has12,308 received/12,222 closed/21.50% upheld; this narrower table must not replace or be added to the all-product totals. FCA publishes thresholded firm-reported returns, not an independent re-investigation of every complaint. Product mix and scale still matter; no bank-quality ranking is constructed. Source **R2-FCA-METHOD** describes thresholds and context.

FOS's page titled“Half-yearly H1 2026” explicitly covers **April–September 2026**, not calendarH1. It reports **3,764 new Lloyds Bank PLC cases**, of which3,612 banking/credit,117 mortgage,14 general insurance,10 investment and11 pension. These sum to the new-case total. The page covers firms with30new and30resolved cases. It does not supply a matched resolved/upheld cohort here. **3,764/118,762 is not an escalation rate**: dates, case stages and cohorts differ. Source **R2-FOS-APRSEP26**; publication date unverified.

Ipsos's17 August 2026 GB personal-current-account survey covers July 2025–June 2026 and eligible service users. It measures willingness to recommend, not actual churn, complaint incidence or Group-wide harm. Methodology excludes dont-know/nonusers by service window. Original PDF extraction drops chart provider labels and the screenshot capability is unavailable; **no bank score or rank is admitted** from unlabelled percentages. Sources **R2-IPSOS-AUG26/METHOD**. This is an explicit evidence requirement, not a guessed chart mapping.

The remaining commercially useful test is specific: which product/journey/cohort accounts for material repeat contacts, resolution quality or customer outcomes after current remedies? Complaint volume alone cannot connect a Retail deposit movement, AI performance or a bank-wide weakness.

## Operational resilience and cyber — exact scope

HM Treasury's10 July 2026 announcement designates specified provider **legal entities**, effective13 July: Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited, Google Cloud EMEA Limited, AWS EMEA and OracleUK (**R2-HMT-CTP**). This establishes a system-level oversight response. It neither identifies the bank's contractual counterparty nor proves a Lloyds service's criticality, missing controls, cyber incident or compliance failure. A provider designation is not a bank outsourcing requirement or open procurement.

No complete technology inventory, vulnerability finding, breach, vendor spend concentration, BPO footprint, renewal/exit position or critical-service supplier map is established. A job advertisement/search hit is not adopted as an inventory or current contract. The useful remaining question is the actual service/control/outcome decision after existing safeguards, with precise permitted evidence and an accountable owner.
