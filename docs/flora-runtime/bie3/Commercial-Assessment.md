## Before versus now

BIE-2 already contained useful figures and caveats, but its broad NatWest deposit label did not capture the exclusion of central items; several measurements lacked an atomised model/period/acceptance path. BIE-3 preserves those historical bytes and proposes the narrower definitions against an original investor report. It resolves the NatWest publication day using two first-party references. The CAL bridge explicitly separates differing comparison bases instead of turning them into a dramatic but false accounting contradiction.

HSBC UK's original numbers are not presented as newly discovered. Their value here is the explicit signed reconciliation, USD legal-entity boundary, existing-contract field path and refusal to assign Group transformation or leadership authority to the UK bank. Santander's contribution/cost decomposition deepens the earlier integration narrative without describing incurred costs as future seller revenue.

The source-to-model path identifies actual blockers rather than labelling every research item prohibited. Rights/use review, current host admission, source-type capability, exact accounting definition, real canonical identity/receipts and owner Evidence acceptance are separate. The same field-level compatibility check deliberately fails when real canonical/Evidence IDs are blank. It is progress toward usable Enterprise Models, not a claim those Models have been created.

## Public slice and its limit

The selectable FCA views add a small amount of directly usable legal-bank service intelligence. A user can see received activity, closed activity and firm-upheld outcomes with the same period/entity and optional original source. The default caveat prevents interpreting different cohorts as backlog clearance or counts/percentages as a performance ranking. Santander UK Plc is not silently consolidated with TSB.

This is **service operations context**, not the financial/operating-model depth in the candidate dossiers. The issuer financials remain unavailable to core FLORA until their specific source-use/admission/identity/acceptance path is resolved. Neither the small slice nor this paper conceals that boundary.

Nationwide, Starling and ClearBank remain candidate work; ClearBank's UK profit versus Group loss distinction is retained. Relationship proposals do not establish workload, contract value, expiry, exclusivity or remaining external scope. Current supplier/customer edges cannot be promoted just because an actor register or a collaboration article exists.

The next highest-value owner decisions are the exact three document/use proposals, actual reporting-entity/receipt reconciliation and individual Evidence review. Broader actor/profile collection, invented estimates or generic sales entry plays would not resolve those blockers. Owner research questions and falsifiers remain in the review pack, not seller-facing core screens.
