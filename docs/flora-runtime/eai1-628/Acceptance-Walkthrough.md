# Owner acceptance walkthrough

Review this draft as an isolated application candidate, not the deployed site. Current Render SHA and canonical acceptance receipts are unverified. No Evidence acceptance, import or deployment was executed.

1. Open `/` and keep the default artwork. Open Leadership Agenda, select the cyber issue, then inspect its Estimated inputs, fieldwork/recall, exclusions and counter-evidence. Switch Landscape/Forces; subject and selected issue persist.
2. Select Banking in the UK panorama, then Lloyds directly from Banking. No intermediate screen. In Banking's agenda inspect provider oversight: four exact legal providers and publication/effective dates, no asserted bank contract.
3. At Lloyds inspect funding-base: dated deposits, Calculated £6.3bn difference and composition limitations. Switch Inside/Landscape/Forces and use browser Back/Forward and deep links.
4. Inspect service-recovery: received/closed cohorts and firm-upheld rate remain distinct; no closure-speed claim, Group proxy, harm/cost or FOS inference. Optional sources lead to the original FCA table; numerical-data licence is available on demand.
5. Verify no core research gaps, seller questions, recommendations or acceptance administration. Existing owner-only research inspection must deny an unauthorised/foreign selector.
6. Review each of the eight source forms individually. None is selected or executed. Do not treat conditional research eligibility as source admission, verified canonical identity or owner acceptance. Review Proposed ADR-027 separately.
