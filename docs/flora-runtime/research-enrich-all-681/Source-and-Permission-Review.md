## Source lifecycle and negative requirements

A withdrawn, retracted, prohibited or core-contradictory source suppresses its dependent factual and interpretive candidate projections, including calculated results and labels. Unrelated sources remain usable; history is preserved. Review of an exact legal name must not create a bank contract, a system must not become an enterprise, and a policy proposal must not become an effective duty. This research pack tests documentary dependencies and existing restriction semantics; it does not implement a new runtime cascade or modify canonical history.
