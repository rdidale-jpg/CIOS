## Same-subject Facts and Insight requirements

- UK: dated output composition → bounded sector divergence; no company growth allocation.
- Banking: funding/payments/provider roles and measured facts → bank-specific repricing/continuity mechanisms, with unproved exposure retained.
- Enterprise bank/mutual: exact financial/legal perimeter → funding/member-value/benefit interpretation; Inside keeps activities separate from enduring capabilities.
- Airline: sourced tool/operation/segment facts → mechanism with current outcome gaps, not a demonstrated technology problem.
- Technology provider: exact global financial and role cuts → economic mechanism; no UK allocation, customer workload or inferred contract.
- Operator/network/institution: common-service or regulated-capital/funding facts → appropriate economics; payment principal, regulated investment and budget plans are not interchangeable revenue or seller spend.

Fact/Insight mode does not grant source use, canonical status or permissions. A displayed candidate is classified as awaiting admission where appropriate; a canonical null alone is not grounds to block lawful ordinary analytical research. Important caveats, actual versus plan, entity, periods and required licences survive both modes. Research gaps/questions/source administration stay in authorised Research/Owner; this register is an internal review artefact, not proposed default Main copy.
