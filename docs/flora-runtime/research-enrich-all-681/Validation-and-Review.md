## Material limitations

- Exhaustiveness applies to tracked file/member metadata and the declared route catalogue. The 251 identity/subject-key extracts are selected heuristics and include schemas/fixtures, not 251 accepted Models; TEL alone has 1,060 packaged NDJSON rows. The source index includes citation leads, not 1,290 verified publishers.
- The subject census preserves 250 owner-qualified occurrences, including capabilities, actors and repeated/composite/institution scopes. It is not a count of unique legal enterprises. Coverage records requirements and known state; it does not establish completion of every requirement.
- Candidate packages and prior partial research remain valuable but are neither wholly absent nor wholly public. No original production/canonical receipt session was available. Production acceptance is unverified; no false zero-state conclusion follows.
- The first wave is substantive across multiple verified families, but airline/media accounts, detailed operations/outcomes, all 20-sector foundations and the complete provider pass remain open. BBC/Barclays/easyJet acquisition failures are explicit. No synthetic numbers fill those gaps.
- Original bytes were not archived for new web sources. Excerpt hashes are genuine excerpt hashes only. Existing ZIP hashes establish retained package bytes, not publisher source-use authority.
- Actual source restrictions, exact identity and intended-use decisions remain with existing owners. A public route or a technical allowlist is not permission; lack of canonical promotion alone is not a blanket bar on lawful analytical research.
- No new canonical engine, source/graph database, scheduler, autonomous paid service, model calls, private records or commercial qualifications were introduced. All research outputs are additive documentation and isolated QA.
