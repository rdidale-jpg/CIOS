# Commercial, architecture and visual self-critique

This is a self-critique, not an independent opinion or owner acceptance. No paid reviewer or autonomous research agent was used.

## Commercial usefulness

The strongest improvement is the move from isolated claims and repeated three-step explanations to an explorable business: Group → different segment mechanisms; customer journeys → durable capabilities; product/platform → the exact disclosed relationship; strategy → dated change → conditional SWOT. Four-quadrant SWOT is visible in one Main composition and each theme has a genuinely different falsifier/next investigation. Acquisition completion is a decision already made, not an early opportunity or open scope.

The existing legal-bank complaint outcomes add measured operating context, while different cohorts and the absence of a customer denominator prevent service-quality or backlog claims. The existing stock-date calculation explains the two balances without suggesting organic flow.

The biggest limit is financial admission, not presentation. Two Group deposit dates and rounded nine-month PBT cannot explain segment returns. The economic model deliberately distinguishes mechanisms from reported allocation, and Central / Equity explains the missing tie-out without inventing it. The owner can evaluate the experience now, but cannot accept the requirement to see reported segment profits as complete. Existing Source/legal and Financial owners must resolve that bounded reuse before eligible inputs can extend the current financial components.

The operating model is useful orientation, not a verified application estate or decision-maker map. Disclosed Envoy controls/Google collaboration and Copilot licences are materially different facts; deeper fields explicitly show Unknown instead of fabricated installation versions, contract ends or renewal opportunities. Commercial conversations remain questions; no pain, buyer authority, spend or supplier work package is invented. Unsupported leadership mandates and legal-entity dependencies remain omitted.

## Architecture

One Lloyds view composition reuses the actual existing frame, public research projection, eligible-statement adapter, financial guards, original artwork and compact interaction script. No database, new canonical Economics object, registry, matcher, Financial run or approval system exists. Finite Source-derived prose and diagrams are recomputed on every request. Exact route evolution pins retain every historical compatibility decision and reject unreviewed mutations.

Trade-off: the finite mapping between illustration subjects and current claim IDs is explicit code, consistent with existing Lloyds compositions. It must evolve with the owning source projection rather than becoming an independent content store. It is not a claim that all future enterprises should share a banking template. Do not generalise capability/contract fields to other business models without appropriate evidence and existing owner contracts.

## Visual and interaction judgement

Real desktop renders retain the original rich interior, quiet ivory workspace and restrained green/gold hierarchy. Group has one editorial opening, three business cards and two dated numbers. Analysis has four visible quadrants rather than two tiers of choice controls. The other subjects use the representation that explains the content: comparison tables, capability/value-stream matrix, application dossier or dated timeline. No Main accordion is introduced.

The image areas are navigation geometry, not premises, economic weight or division ownership. On smaller screens, the existing single scene disclosure trades simultaneous context for readable Main width. The original illustration remains available on one action and without JavaScript. Long Level 3 records necessarily require scrolling; they are not default content.

Potential weaknesses for owner inspection: application examples are still a very small part of a major bank; conceptual capability tables can look more authoritative than their verified status, so the status stays explicit. Group figures must never be mistaken for segment figures. Five subjects per perspective use the allowed upper bound and need real iPad assessment. Check that the evaluation notice/return choices feel restrained. Physical iPads and the ten absent owner screenshots remain unverified; automated viewport checks are not a substitute.

## Acceptance recommendation

Review the complete optional runtime in one sitting: Group, Economics, Retail, Inside applications, one programme and all SWOT themes. Accept useful supported depth separately from unavailable financial data. Keep both previous experiences until the owner approves replacement. No merge or deployment in this sprint; no Source authority is granted by this critique or by passing CI.
