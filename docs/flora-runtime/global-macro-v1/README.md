## Delivered experience

- Six illustrated systemic-force categories, Global/UK/France context selection, one four-part intelligence inspector and direct evidence access.
- Three researched themes represented by five deduplicated force/instrument records: cyber threat and proposed reform; UK social-value procurement; UK sustainability rules and EU/French reporting context.
- Shared Banking/Aviation PESTLE and operating maps with interpreted pressure highlights. Exact legal entity, ownership and enterprise exposure remain Unknown where unsupported.
- Existing Lloyds third-party resilience (`RCO-LBG-005`) and easyJet sustainability-reconciliation (`RCO-EJ-006`) hypotheses are accessible where a narrow thematic association exists. No new opportunity IDs, buyer demand, budgets or procurement schedules are invented.
- Country changes preserve explicit force selection for comparison; a newly selected French sustainability category chooses its local source context. UK requirements do not silently become French requirements.
- Phone defaults prioritise the visual and quick country links; advanced offering/depth filters are available through **Refine context**. Tablet and phone selections reveal the inspector and provide **Back to visual**.
- Native links, keyboard interaction, browser Back, no-JavaScript fallbacks, one-page executive exports and complete evidence appendices.

## Three validated scenarios

| Seller | Tested path | Commercial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New to the industry | Declare AI transformation/integration offering → cyber force → Banking PESTLE → Lloyds context → existing hypothesis | Glossary and next question support orientation. Seller relevance is potential alignment, not buying demand or territorial eligibility. |
| Experienced | France sustainability context → Evidence first → located French/ESMA passages, dates and transposition limits | Direct deep access; no requirement to traverse multiple reports. Enterprise duty remains unresolved. |
| Intermediate | UK social-value context → compare France → PESTLE → Back, retaining force/country/depth | Out-of-scope policy does not propagate; unknown exposure and empty hypothesis mappings stay visible. |

All three paths were exercised in Chromium at desktop, tablet and phone sizes. This does not establish human comprehension speed, commercial usefulness or physical Safari behaviour.

## Explicit gaps and next increment

This is a context/navigation proof, not a global compliance engine, verified enterprise footprint or complete French Industry Twin. Two markets and three themes are researched; other categories remain explicitly unresearched. Full original legal/source bytes, current national implementing texts and exact legal-entity identities need further governed research. Existing sources retain their own age, limitations and reporting periods.

Macro lineage is navigable but is not yet appended to saved Pipeline provenance; existing immutable capture stays unchanged. Seller offering is a stateless declared filter, with no persisted seller profile or territorial permissions. Original industry/candidate pages retain their own UI and use browser Back for the Macro return path.

Next: verify one exact legal entity/instrument scope per country, deepen one sector-specific causal path with counterevidence, then design an adapter through existing owners for immutable Macro capture references and authorised seller coverage. Do not infer or seed qualified opportunities.
