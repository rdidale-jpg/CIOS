## Contradictions, false joins and remaining evidence

| Issue | Resolution or outstanding evidence |
| --- | --- |
| £6.6bn public PBT vs £6,625m Q3 analytical result | Same nine-month statutory Group result, different original display precision. Keep the existing eligible rounded figure. |
| £10,827m statutory vs £11,084m underlying vs £11,470m banking underlying interest income | Same nine months, different reporting bases and banking perimeter. Banking NIM uses the last measure, annualised over average interest-earning banking assets. Reproduce using the issuer’s actual day convention and operand precision before any reuse. |
| Statutory total income vs net income after lease depreciation | S3 reports statutory and underlying income on different bases. Even when headline arithmetic yields nearby numbers, preserve the issuer bridges and signed classification, including lease depreciation above net income. Net fee income is one part of the broader non-interest story. |
| Actual CET1 fell, pro forma CET1 increased | The December baseline moves from 14.0% actual to 13.2% pro forma after buyback adjustment. The September reported and pro forma measures both read 13.3%. Do not mix their parents or claim cash available. |
| Lower cost ratio vs slightly higher operating cost and reported technology reductions | Income grew and prior remediation fell. The technology cost statement lacks a matched GBP baseline and programme attribution. Capitalised software, lease assets, reported strategic investment and expensed technology costs are different concepts. |
| Aggregate deposits grew while Retail deposits fell | Different Group components moved in opposite directions. Margin effect remains unallocated. Obtain a dated balance, rate and product migration view. |
| Higher impairment and stable arrears are both reported | Charge is a flow affected by portfolio growth, models and scenarios. Arrears and loss allowance are distinct indicators. Obtain matched vintage default, arrears migration and charge decomposition. |
| H1 division returns vs later Group accounts | Use separate reporting horizons. Obtain the next issuer segment reporting and the corporate-to-legal-entity inclusive/exclusive map. Do not fill differences with a proxy. |
| Supplier and delivery responsibility | Group Envoy/Google Cloud context does not establish contracting entity, commercial terms or delivery obligations. Obtain explicit lawful contract-scope evidence and programme accountability before attaching a dependency. |
| Investment and 2030 plans | No verified net AI return, contract spend, capex allocation or outcome-by-initiative. Track actual costs, amortisation, capital consumption, change adoption and risk-adjusted benefits under exact definitions. |
