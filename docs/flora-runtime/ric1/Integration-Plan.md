## Additional integration blocker: publisher policy

Current `research_intelligence/source_import.py:PUBLISHERS` permits FCA and the Lloyds issuer domain, but **does not permit www.sec.gov or www.ons.gov.uk**. The retained SEC and ONS excerpts are factual owner-review candidates, not immediately admissible visual-source imports. The existing reviewed-excerpt *shape* is compatible; its host admission is not. RIC-1 neither widens the allowlist nor relabels a SEC extract as issuer-hosted. Before a later visual adapter, obtain a separately reviewed owner/architecture source-policy decision for the exact official hosts/documents, or independently verify an equivalent issuer-hosted source through the already permitted route and preserve the new provenance. Do not bypass, replace or weaken official_url. This policy blocker is explicitly classified E for the proposed unauthorised adapter use; A denotes the separate bounded evidence-review eligibility.
