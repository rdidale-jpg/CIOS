# Evidence decisions and their limits

These A–E labels are **review-pack dispositions**, not new canonical CIOS states, rights grants or qualification statuses. Each record in Evidence-Acceptance-Matrix has an individual disposition/reason and lineage. Original records remain lossless in Claim-Ledger; broader tables retain exact pinned input pointers and digests.

| Class | Admission boundary | Permitted next step |
|---|---|---|
| A — eligible for owner evidence acceptance | A specific factual value has been rechecked in a dated, scoped original passage/table and a bounded reviewed excerpt retained. Eight narrow proposals satisfy this review threshold. | Owner decides whether this exact evidence/citation use is acceptable. Canonical identity, rights, freshness and importing remain separate gates. |
| B — further source verification | Exact passage, full applicable definition, entity/legal applicability, chart labels or permissible use remains incomplete. Compound facts require decomposition. | Acquire permitted source/excerpt, preserve publication versus reporting/retrieval dates, verify source-specific rights and definitions, atomise and independently check. |
| C — interpretation/hypothesis | Scoped analytical mechanism, issue, role hypothesis or commercial investigation question. | Read-only synthesis with source lineage, competing explanations, unknown demand and falsifiers. No conversion to factual Observation or MaterialPressure. |
| D — contradicted/stale/rejected inference | The proposed inference fails evidence, period, identity or authority checks. | Retain history and its reason. Do not delete correct source facts merely because a proposed inference was rejected. |
| E — proposed use not eligible | A specific use lacks the required existing authority or conflicts with rights/atomicity/permission rules. | Exclude that use; seek a separately authorised compatible route. Unverified rights alone do not prove every factual use prohibited. |

All original source records retain copyright/terms and acquisition limits. Public access is not permission for unrestricted document storage, reuse, full-file redistribution or cross-tenant ingestion. No legal opinion or source-rights acceptance is asserted. The three retained short table excerpts are factual, attributed review material; no whole source is archived. They preserve their retrieval references, original URLs, locators, normalized rendered-separator extent and **excerpt** digests. Original-file hashes remain null. Reviewed-web-excerpt is an existing research_intelligence.model contract, not a new evidence type.

A source can support a reported management target or policy plan as an attributed statement without supporting the target's attainment. Existing published AI capabilities are evidence against a generic missing-platform pitch. Source trust, Observation lifecycle, financial measurement state, accounting basis, freshness and import runtime status remain separate.

For financial facts retain issuer/legal or reporting-segment identity, geography/perimeter, period start/end, point-in-time versus flow, unit/sign, APM versus statutory basis, actual/comparator/guidance/target and represented comparator version. Proposed expense amounts are spoken as positive charges but their source payload retains negative signed P&L values. Never overwrite an original comparative with a represented value without history.

The eight proposals are not a financially complete FoundationFact import: the existing financial-intelligence constructors demand additional original-document/extraction metadata. Do not invent hashes, confidence, entity IDs or owner decisions to satisfy them. The existing Researcher adapter losslessly retains proposal fields; custom review annotations do not extend canonical enums or enterprise model paths.

The current visual publisher allowlist accepts FCA but rejects the SEC and ONS hosts. An A research-review decision does not authorise their current visual ingestion. Direct use through that adapter without a separately governed policy decision is E; see Integration-Plan. No policy is changed.

All B compound bundles remain outside the import set. A parent's repeated facts do not create additional accepted observations; the narrower subclaim has one stable proposal ID and a parent link. Policy plans and reported regulatory judgements stay attributed. Neither correct arithmetic nor 388 passing security tests proves original-source truth or owner acceptance.
