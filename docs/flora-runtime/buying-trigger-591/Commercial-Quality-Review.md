## Before / after assessment

Before, each of the five Offers repeated one library first-conversation paragraph across all ten enterprises: enterprise context and a name-prefixed deciding question did not make that entry play account-specific. [Commercial-Before.json](Commercial-Before.json) retains the exact published before texts and their source matrix hash; [the current matrix](previews/Assessment-Matrix.json) contains the complete after state.

After, ten supported Offer–enterprise pairs have ten distinct first conversations, diagnostics, deciding questions and stop conditions. Digital covers Santander, Lloyds, Computacenter, BT and NatWest; AI-first covers Lloyds; Cyber covers Computacenter; Legacy covers Santander, Lloyds and Computacenter. Each asks about a particular decision supported by existing fact IDs, specifies a bounded diagnostic artifact and challenges whether internal/incumbent delivery already resolves the issue. These are proposed questions, not newly researched customer problems or agreed consulting scope.

All 50 tiers, stages, factual why statements and counter-evidence are unchanged. No new source, date, identity, numeric value, buyer name, procurement, customer demand or external supplier scope is introduced. All Research-only cases remain Research only and explicitly decline to recommend a programme diagnostic from insufficient evidence. ESG remains entirely Research only; NatWest point-tool AI, Lloyds existing cyber controls and the unbound Technology candidate limitations remain intact.

| Quality criterion | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Account-specific opening | One generic paragraph per Offer | Ten distinct openings anchored to retained enterprise facts |
| Diagnostic output | Generic Offer workshop/proof | Specific journey/hand-off/benefits/dependency/assurance/process-role decision artifact |
| Buying boundary | General policy caveat | Conditional invitation plus explicit account-specific stop condition |
| Falsification | Generic question with enterprise name | Distinct residual-need, simpler-change or incumbent-sufficiency test |
| Auditability | Broad assessment lineage | Required fact IDs and exact retained source/date links on each tailored play |
| Missing evidence | Generic programme entry play still displayed | Research-only wording; no programme diagnostic recommended |
| Private review freshness | Source/Offer versions | Source/Offer plus commercial-content revision; old review retained but stale |

## Ownership, safeguards and sensitivity

`buying_entry_plays.py` contains authored questions and required evidence references only; it has no collector, ranking engine, approval or writer. The existing `offers.assess_fit` remains the only matcher and selects a play only after its existing scope/exclusion checks and positive-fact admissibility. Every required fact must be present at the chosen cutoff, and every required positive must survive withdrawal, freshness, closure and supersession. No renamed enterprise or generic template is counted as a tailored diagnostic.

The candidate adapter adds the deterministic play-content revision to its read-only research bundle. Existing assessment identity and shortlist research-digest checks therefore invalidate stale recommendations without changing private records. Old shortlist versions are retained, marked stale and never silently rewritten. Saving requires explicit human review through the existing employer/Offer/entity/workspace/CAS service. There are no Pipeline changes. Original source registry bytes and SHA-256, publication/verification dates, source rights, canonical research, history guard, approval functions, comparator and baseline remain unchanged. Original Sprint A policy stays unchanged.

Tested counterfactuals: withdraw each required positive or counter fact; advance beyond freshness; exclude the enterprise; change the commercial revision after saving a reviewed shortlist. Tailoring is suppressed, stale submissions fail, old review is retained, and no Pipeline file is created. The full 50-case before/after comparison asserts no ranking or factual-context drift.
