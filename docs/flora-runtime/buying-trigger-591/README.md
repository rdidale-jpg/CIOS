## Detection policy and audit

Structural pressures, dated catalysts, announced future/conditional plans, reported programme activity, completed initiatives, closures and counter-evidence are distinct. Publication, effective date/period and last verified remain separate. Undated facts cannot supply a dated catalyst. Future publication/verification cannot enter an earlier cutoff; subsequent withdrawals and supersession suppress old facts. A completed or closed programme invalidates only its linked programme, not unrelated activity.

The 90-day event/review window is an operational pilot policy, not a measured demand half-life. Tests shorten it to 60 days, advance cutoffs, close programmes and supersede sources without changing canonical or Offer snapshots. Linked current catalyst + programme + scope + approved Offer + identity are required for Prioritise; partial programme/structural support gives Investigate, missing evidence gives Research only. Private Offers are capped at Investigate; exclusions cannot be shortlisted. Missing access, funding, sponsor, authority, intent, incumbent obligations and procurement remain unknown.

False-positive audit: exclude product AI from enterprise AI demand, existing controls from cyber need, completed CRM from an open transformation mandate, energy outcomes from ESG procurement, and conditional transfer from approved migration. False-negative risk: private programmes, unpublished tenders, buyer relationships and stale public reporting can hide real demand; Research only means insufficient retained evidence, not no commercial potential. Verification is bounded and not exhaustive. No ESG positive is manufactured to fill a matrix.
