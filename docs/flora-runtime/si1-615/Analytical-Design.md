## Economics, segmentation and value capture

The design calls for industry size/growth, segment definitions, revenues/profit pools, cost/margins, capital intensity, investment patterns and value distribution. Current public fixtures do not supply a reconciled current Banking series. Finance & insurance's 8.1% of UK GVA (2024) cannot be substituted for it. The proof consequently keeps profit/capital and segment comparisons **Unknown**, with targeted source requirements.

The value-chain model reuses eight activities: product/strategy; acquisition/channels; deposits/funding; lending/credit; payments; service/outcomes; risk/compliance/resilience; data/platforms/people. It asks how value is created, delivered and captured, and which participants or dependencies affect a decision. A conceptual activity remains different from an actual Lloyds process, named customer, supplier contract or payment-system participation.

Financial inspection must preserve: exact legal/Group/segment perimeter; reporting dates and duration; currency; units; accounting definition; nominal/real basis; actual/target/estimate; comparable/restated denominator; source rights and review extent. Banking analysis must use appropriate income, impairment, capital and liquidity measures rather than treating bank cash flow/EBITDA like an industrial company. Estimates require visible EST, method, range and uncertainty; none is introduced in the proof.

For Lloyds, the selected current disclosures are £502.8bn Group deposits at 30 Sep 2026, £6.6bn statutory pretax profit for the nine months to that date, and a below-45% cost:income **2030 target**. These three cannot form a current cost-to-serve calculation, growth estimate, investment budget or supplier contract value.

## SWOT is relational synthesis

Strengths/weaknesses concern internal conditions at the relevant scope; opportunities/threats concern external conditions and choices. Each factor links to an argument and counter-argument, not just one of four headings.

- Lloyds' announced Envoy capability/controls is a source-reported **strength/counter-case** against a generic missing-AI-platform thesis; it does not prove effective deployment.
- Core renewal and efficiency targets are **strategic responses/intent**, not established successful capabilities or adverse pain.
- A possible platform transition can create a **conditional threat** to continuity; no outage is alleged.
- A residual workflow improvement can be a **strategic opportunity hypothesis**, conditional on outcome evidence and actual remaining choices; it is not a sales Opportunity.
- No evidenced internal weakness is invented. FIN/ORG/OPS gaps are weaknesses in the research, not in Lloyds. Empty weakness quadrants are deliberate.
- A supplier platform may both enable capability and constrain alternatives. The first is disclosed; actual dependency/lock-in and leverage remain unmeasured.

The three Lloyds issues are distinct decisions: core service/architecture continuity, evidence for operating/efficiency choices, and benefit on existing AI capability. They can interact; do not add their hypothetical benefits or turn them into three deal values. The agenda remains one shared scope/cutoff synthesis across streams.
