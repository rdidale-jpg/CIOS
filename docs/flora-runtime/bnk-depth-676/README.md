## Withheld inputs and evidence demands

The pinned [#665 industry handoff](https://github.com/rdidale-jpg/CIOS/pull/665/commits/46f0ed42e10f2c8a2cabbf344498898e94072990) and [#666 enterprise handoff](https://github.com/rdidale-jpg/CIOS/pull/666/commits/412c086391194ec4e9d2f1b76841b0bb1df8917d) remain candidate research. Current public admission is assessed per document and claim.

- Customer market shares, switch and retention rates, channel costs and population-normalised service outcomes need comparable eligible denominators and dates. CASS is distinct from the payments operator and has no new admission here.
- Nationwide Group results do not set an industry margin. Society-level service and Group financial scopes are separate. New funding and Bank Rate figures from draft research need exact period, use permission and public owner bindings.
- Pay.UK Limited, its schemes, participants, guarantors and switching services are separate. Participation and guarantor evidence is review-only. The Faster Payments guide has unresolved conflicting use terms and the FY2025 filing lacked readable retained evidence. Publication would need explicit document-specific rights and an owner-validated financial perimeter, unit, period and income basis.
- Provider concentration, fees, named workloads and continuity exposure require eligible evidence identifying the actual contracting entities, service and period. Neither HMT designation nor the Group announcement meets that demand. UK policy does not establish individual entity exposure.
- `BK-ENT-003` remains unresolved. Banking-Knowledge-Manifest uses it for Nationwide/Virgin Money; the portfolio owner uses it for NatWest Group. No alias or identity changed. Links use the existing scoped selections.
