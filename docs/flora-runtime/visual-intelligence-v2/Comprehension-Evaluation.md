# V1 versus V2 executive comprehension protocol

**Unmeasured. No real users have completed this evaluation.** Automated rendering, keyboard checks and screenshots are not human comprehension evidence. No percentage or time improvement is claimed.

## Conditions and fairness

Compare the v1 Industry Signature Landscape with the v2 Living Industry Map, using Banking and Aviation and the same pinned assessment, review and source passages. Record code versions and input digests. V1 baseline screenshots are from `650b92c`; v2 screenshots include a new Bank of England indicator, so those before/after pictures alone are **not a controlled experiment**.

For a fair study, supply the identical evidence dossier (including the new RWA passage) to both conditions, with equivalent access to activity purposes, participants, conceptual flows, AI scenarios, uncertainties and source links. Alternatively render v1 against that same candidate snapshot in an isolated evaluation harness. Do not alter production or canonical intelligence to conduct the study. Exclude any question for which only one condition contains the answer. Do not train participants using the answers.

Recruit 8–12 executive, strategic-sales or industry-research participants. Counterbalance v1-first/v2-first and Banking-first/Aviation-first. Use matched but different questions to reduce recall. Keep device, viewport, instruction, source availability and maximum task time the same. Include landscape-tablet and keyboard users. Obtain consent and use public research only.

## Tasks

| Task | Expected evidence of understanding |
| --- | --- |
| Explain an airline journey from demand to service outcome | At least four correct activities and their purposes; distinguish conceptual flows from a verified easyJet implementation |
| Identify principal Banking participants and roles | Three supported names, two distinct roles; no invented market ranking or exposure from label size |
| Describe three dependencies | Explain money, resource, information or service flows; identify which actual supplier relationship is historical and what is still unknown |
| Identify two AI exposure areas | Explain a plausible mechanism and the evidence needed to test it; no invented competitor, displacement or deadline |
| Distinguish fact, interpretation and hypothesis | Classify source-supported measure, generic activity model, dated company disclosure, contradiction and undated scenario correctly |
| Explain scale and timing | Preserve RWA versus assets/GVA, journeys versus people, industry versus enterprise, and announcement versus completion |

Give the overview task a 60-second initial exposure, followed by separately timed investigation. Record accuracy against a prewritten source-scoped answer key, elapsed time, navigation actions/backtracks, source-finding success, unsupported-assumption errors and perceived clarity (1–5). Collect a short think-aloud explanation after each task. Score answers blind to condition where possible.

Report paired times and accuracy with dispersion, task completion, confidence errors, role/device mix, ordering and all exclusions. Faster answers with worse evidence classification do not establish improvement. Treat this small study as exploratory, not population proof. Use confusion about flows, actor placement, historical supplier facts and Unknowns to guide a bounded refinement and retest.
