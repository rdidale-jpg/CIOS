## Composition and visual grammar

**Understand:** one illustrated system with integrated scope/scale rail. Banking centres on customer money → deposits → lending and payments, with risk/control, data and supervision forming supporting layers. Aviation centres on passenger demand → booking → schedule → aircraft/crew → airport/flight → recovery, with manufacturing, maintenance, fuel and airspace dependencies.

**Explore:** select an illustrated place/activity, participant, flow or pressure. A single contextual inspection area shows its purpose, associated actors, capabilities, dependencies, cost/risk interpretation, provenance and Unknowns. AI and regulation lenses highlight mapped activities without changing their factual state. Evidence-backed dated context links to the existing enterprise timeline, with applicability caveats.

**Investigate:** existing dossiers, evidence pages, enterprise understanding, source periods, conflicting findings, frameworks and hypotheses remain reachable. Fold secondary lists together rather than deleting them. No Pipeline change.

Forest/cream = operating context; blue = money/passenger/service flow; amber = risk/resource constraints; plum = scenario; rust = unresolved conflict. Colour is paired with explicit words, line styles and shapes. All industry activity-flow arrows are **conceptual**; source-backed company announcements do not prove sector-wide deployment, supplier incumbency or actual dependency topology. Spatial zones represent business roles, not quantified influence. Participant markers use equal visual weight unless comparable research explicitly justifies another scale. Architectural artwork does not size an enterprise.

Scale indicators must retain definition, entity/geography, unit, reporting period, publication and evidence links. Risk-weighted assets are not total assets, market value, GVA or revenue. UK banking employment/GVA remain unresolved where only broader financial-services estimates are found. Aviation global forecasts and UK airport journeys remain distinct; no cross-metric ratio or actor ranking.
