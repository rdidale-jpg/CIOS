## Existing ownership

`enterprise_economics.py` is a transient HTML composition, analogous to existing `experience_composition` and `lloyds_recovery`; it stores no intelligence, permissions or identity. It is not a canonical Economics object, Source registry, approval system, Financial engine or new dashboard. Existing Source/ISP-1 eligibility resolves facts on each controlled render. Existing `leadership_agenda._deposit_change` reproduces the released two-date stock comparison. F1/F2/F3 calculation, typed measurement and Financial-run owners remain byte-identical.

Original artwork, existing styles/scripts, Top/Left/Main frame, mode selector and access services remain unchanged. Aviation adds three finite Landscape-only detail values; incompatible Inside/Forces switches reset that economic stage while retaining easyJet and reading mode. Existing capability selections still persist across their compatible perspectives.

Accepted ADR-002/005/011/012/013 govern durable ownership, lineage, rapid analytical candidates and optional canonical acceptance. ADR-014/024 retain their accepted interpretation boundaries; ADR-026 alone controls Material Pressure qualification. ADR-023/025/027 remain Proposed, not new authority. The accepted product Experience Contract permits explicitly labelled conceptual structure in Facts mode. No historical Unknown/contradiction records are removed: unsupported core output is omitted, while material claim limitations remain inspectable or adjacent. No accepted ADR is amended.

## Architectural and commercial self-critique

**Strengths:** one economic story replaces repeated bank sections and investigation prompts; selected stages are ordinary URLs; no new global navigation, persistence or approval; dates and definitions sit beside useful scale, and one source disclosure keeps core reading concise. Bank and airline use different mechanisms, icons and accents with the same interaction contract. An explicitly illustrative model remains useful when a number is withdrawn.

**Limits:** aviation remains an operating-scale-backed qualitative economics view rather than the financial comparison in the design. Profit/cash/capability allocations are unavailable and not manufactured. Lloyds lending has no new admitted income measure under §2. Adjacent stock and profit amounts require their distinct period labels and the unweighted-model caption. There is no new research, source admission or actual qualified-pressure claim. A separate financial rollout would require eligible existing-owner inputs, not approval of this UI alone.

**Trade-offs:** a small finite industry presentation policy is justified for differentiated banking/airline mechanisms; it is not a reusable calculation engine. New sectors can reuse the stage renderer with their own already authorised projections. Do not grow it into a financial registry or source-policy configuration. Evidence rendering reuses ISP-1 and keeps sensitive/internal research out of public views. Qualified-pressure links are conditional and may be absent; fabricated completeness would be worse.

**Review status:** self-critique only; no independent agent or legal clearance is claimed. Final visual and implementation acceptance remains with the owner. No paid call or autonomous research was used.
