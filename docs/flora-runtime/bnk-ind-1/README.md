# BNK-IND-1 · UK Banking industry intelligence

UK Banking combines three distinct economic activities. Deposit takers price funding and credit risk, payment participants and operators execute and settle instructions, and technology businesses sell services or capacity. The same customer transaction can touch all three. Its face value measures funds moved, with no direct inference to operator revenue or technology spending.
