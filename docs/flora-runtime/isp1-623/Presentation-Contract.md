# ISP-1 transient intelligence presentation contract

Audience: the existing authorised public/core visual journey. Purpose: declarative intelligence with optional source/method inspection. Input ownership and access are resolved before this policy. The policy reads no private repository, registers no source, changes no canonical state and has no mutation/approval endpoint.

| Semantic type | Meaning / required admission | Presentation |
|---|---|---|
| Reported | An attributed statement or evidence-backed fact with source/scope/period and suitable verification. A report of a target remains a target. | Accessible “Reported”; ordinary facts need no visible badge. Explicit target/guidance/priority/proposal labels where material. |
| Calculated | A deterministic stated formula and exact compatible inputs. Preserve units, entity, period, basis and measurement state; assumptions/estimates propagate transitively. | Quiet Calculated label; formula/inputs and inherited uncertainty on demand. Never a new reported fact. |
| Estimated | A quantitative value using a stated method and material uncertainty/sensitivity. | Estimated label, method and caveats. No unsupported estimate in the live pilot. |
| Working assumption | Conditional owner/version-specific input; not a newly accepted Twin fact or an editable scenario here. | Working assumption label, owner/version/conditions. Test fixtures only in this sprint. |
| Insight / inference | A declarative, traceable interpretation with reasoning and counter-evidence. | FLORA Insight label; supporting basis, alternative/countercase on demand. Not observed pain or demand. |
| Hypothesis | A declarative falsifiable proposition with evidence chain, counter-evidence and falsifier. | Hypothesis label and optional falsification reasoning. No new hypothesis or opportunity store; no live research hypothesis promotion. |

Three independent dimensions are retained in the view object: `semantic_type`, `verification` and `canonical_status`. The renderer does not collapse them to confidence or infer acceptance from semantic type. A reviewed source excerpt may support an attributed Reported statement while remaining candidate intelligence. A calculated result with accepted inputs remains a derived view; calculations with estimates/assumptions retain their limitations. Internal acceptance and repair tasks are not serialised into public HTML.

## Scope, time and measurement

Every material statement retains the specific issuer/legal entity/reporting segment, reporting/statement period, source publication date where supplied and accounting/measurement meaning. A consolidated Group result is not a bank legal-entity result. A future management target/expectation is not achieved performance. Sources are resolved from existing validated owners; source snapshots/quotes remain unchanged. Historical/superseded claims cannot silently appear as current; source-owner `updates`/`supersedes` links govern omission.

The pilot’s Industry share is **Calculated**: published section weight ÷10. It is whole SIC K Finance & insurance, not Banking market size or sales TAM. GVA and real GDP have different valuation/period definitions. Display rounding/unit conversion does not manufacture a new economic fact. Lloyds deposits remain a dated stock; statutory profit remains a nine-month flow.

## Omission and information flow

Unsupported semantic kinds, empty or unsupported claims, unavailable source chains, owner-only content, stale/superseded/withdrawn views and unresolved core contradictions are omitted. Missing information is never converted to zero, a negative fact, a blank metric or an invented answer. No issue displayed does not certify complete evidence, absence of risk or approval.

No research gaps, completeness counts, missing-slot headings, research questions, next-action recommendations or internal Evidence administration are placed in text, title/alt/ARIA, HTML data attributes, JSON, source disclosures or public exports. The guard is **not** an AI text classifier: adapters supply explicit types; a small rejection check stops task/question copy. Only reviewed disclosure projections are shortened; their original statements, source quotes and material caveats remain with their owners. If the statement changes beyond its reviewed projection pin, the full changed statement must satisfy the policy rather than reusing an old curated fact.

Material caveats and counter-evidence are retained with the statement. A core contradiction omits the claim rather than selecting the commercially convenient source. No active pain, funding, urgency, budget, buying authority or external scope follows from a priority, programme, calculation or inference.

## Worked example and acceptance boundary

The Issue623 Lloyds H1 example is exercised **only in policy tests**: Reported £130m and £129m movements → Calculated £1m difference → labelled offset Insight → falsifiable residual-improvement Hypothesis with existing mitigation/counter-evidence. It is not a leasing margin or a live new company fact. RIE/RIC remain unaccepted inputs; no source/Observation import is performed to make this example appear in production.

Future News and Leadership Agenda may consume this same policy after separate commissioning and source/permission acceptance. Questions and persona-specific recommendations belong to the existing protected owner inspection or future Role-Specific Actions; no such action capability is implemented here.
