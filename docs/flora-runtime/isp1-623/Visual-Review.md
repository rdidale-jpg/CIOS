## Design assessment

Retained: expansive continuous imagery, twenty original UK industry labels, green available-industry outlines, readable financial context, compact global navigation, active stream identity, native link/keyboard/touch navigation, responsive folding and optional text view.

Removed from core presentation: nine-gap filler, missing-slot headings, decision/research questions, proposed actions, technical claim IDs, source-administration links and unsupported supplier/customer details. Material caveats remain on demand. A claim without adequate support does not produce a blank metric or an empty detail panel.

Added: one consistent quiet semantic vocabulary. Most reported facts are unbadged but identified to assistive technology. Target and Calculated/Insight labels identify a real distinction; they are not warning badges. Source disclosures retain a44px native keyboard/touch target. Text and typography remain in the existing FLORA palette; the default illustrations are unobstructed.

Trade-off: omitting unsupported Lloyds customer/operating detail reduces some clickable content; it does not certify that research is complete or that no risk exists. Scope illustrations remain conceptual. Research tools are now owner-restricted; the existing owner inspection keeps original data, while PR603 remains separate. Richer accepted evidence can supply additional declarative detail later through the same owners, without manufacturing content to fill space.
