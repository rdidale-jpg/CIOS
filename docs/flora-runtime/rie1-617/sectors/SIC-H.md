# Transport & logistics — UK SIC 2007 H

**Controlled foundational tranche; partial, not an accepted Industry Twin.** As of 14 November 2026.

## Boundary and measured economics

Road, rail, aviation, shipping, warehousing and postal services; GB traffic includes private cars and excludes NI.

Official 2024 GVA weight: **36/1000 (3.6%)**. September 2026 real GVA volume: **+1.4% versus September 2025**. These are different bases/periods, not current sales, sector profit or addressable budget. [ONS GVA3 appendix, page index 29](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/grossdomesticproductgdp/bulletins/gdpmonthlyestimateuk/september2026/pdf). Rounded shares are not normalised.

**Original source candidate:** 343.5bn vehicle miles, up 1.5% year-on-year; cars represented 76% and vans 17%. Traffic volume is not logistics revenue or UK-wide H GVA. [DFT-H26](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/provisional-road-traffic-estimates-great-britain-july-2025-to-june-2026/provisional-road-traffic-estimates-great-britain-july-2025-to-june-2026). Period: Twelve months ending June 2026.

## Actors and value creation

DfT is the statistical actor; existing Aviation/easyJet research is narrower and not revalidated by this traffic source.

**Interpretation:** Infrastructure/capacity → movement/storage → delivery/service. Utilisation, fuel, reliability and regulation affect returns. Actual named contracts, spend concentration and profit pools require their own evidence.
