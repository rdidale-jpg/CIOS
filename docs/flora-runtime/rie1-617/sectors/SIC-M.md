Official 2024 GVA weight: **87/1000 (8.7%)**. September 2026 real GVA volume: **+7.4% versus September 2025**. These are different bases/periods, not current sales, sector profit or addressable budget. [ONS GVA3 appendix, page index 29](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/grossdomesticproductgdp/bulletins/gdpmonthlyestimateuk/september2026/pdf). Rounded shares are not normalised.
