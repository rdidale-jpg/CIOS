# Foundational and deep-tier standards — defined before collection

A sector dossier is an inspectable research proposal, not proof of an accepted Twin. Dimension statuses are **source-backed candidate**, **bounded interpretation**, **partial**, **unknown/not acquired**, **contradicted**, or **not applicable with reason**. No overall completion percentage or document-count proxy.

## Minimum Foundational Industry Standard

Each of the exact 20 displayed UK SIC 2007 sections needs: precise inclusion/exclusion and identity mapping; primary measured size/role and period, growth or explicit missingness; important segments; sourced participant roles/examples or named gaps; customers/suppliers/dependencies with general mechanisms distinguished from contracts; relevant Political/Economic/Social/Technological/Legal/Environmental coverage; all five competitive-force mechanisms with segment boundaries and evidence limits; value creation/delivery/capture; grounded structural conditions and relational SWOT; zero to three supported strategic questions; decision-role categories, next evidence tasks, counter-evidence and source/date/rights trace.

A foundation is **not met** merely because all headings exist. Missing current metrics, participants, competitive economics, supplier coverage or a primary scope-specific source prevents a claim of substantive completion. For households employing domestic personnel, do not force a conventional corporate market/firm rivalry model; mark the method's applicability explicitly. Sector membership is not a buyer/supplier relationship. SIC K is wider than Banking; GVA is not revenue/TAM; imputed rent is not an addressable real-estate service budget. Section U is outside the displayed A–T domestic denominator.

## Deep Tier

In addition to the foundation: reconciled matched-period financial/economic measures and definitions; current segment/group/legal identities; real operating/technology/leadership boundaries; source-supported dependencies and actual outcomes; alternative/internal/incumbent remedies; dated regulatory applicability; material consequences distinct from hypotheses; relevant direct and conditional transmission traces; distinct leadership decisions and open/closed/unknown status; specific falsifiable seller learning actions. Market shares/relative returns require genuinely comparable sources. A large programme or company investment plan cannot supply supplier scope or contract value.

Lloyds gap dispositions require exact supporting claims, reporting scope and review limits. The nine visual questions, 13 overlay gaps and nine RCO hypotheses remain distinct. New research may propose partial resolution; it does not edit their accepted owner or retrospectively confirm buying intent. No demonstrated pain is asserted without suitable direct adverse-outcome evidence; legitimate reported adverse consequences still do not establish external demand.

## Acquisition / acceptance gates

Prefer primary sources. Track original URL, publisher, publication/event/effective/reporting/retrieval dates, geography/legal scope, methodology, revisions, rights, extraction extent and confidence reasons. Unknown stays Unknown. Verify URLs and material facts where technically possible, record failures without invented browsing. Retain contradictory old facts/history and separate a later revision from a genuinely different accounting or entity basis.

Collection → dedup/entity resolution → source validation → candidate Observation/Enterprise Model proposal → existing authorised receipt/acceptance. Package validity never equals accepted Evidence/Observation/model truth. Dry-run only; no privileged import, approval, ADR-026 qualification or Pipeline transition. Rights-limited sources use bounded paraphrase/citation and at most 25 quoted words per source. Do not commit whole copyrighted articles, private ledgers, credentials or raw diagnostic logs.
