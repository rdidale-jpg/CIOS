# UK financial system — separate from SIC K and Banking

**Candidate assessment, 14 November 2026.** This dossier follows household, business and government financing through banks and market-based finance. It does not treat all national debt or financial flows as the Financial Services industry's market size.

## Households, firms and government

ONS Q2 2026 accounts report real household disposable income/head +1.0% qoq and saving ratio 8.8%; PNFC net lending £4.3bn, households net lending 2.4% GDP and government borrowing 5.2% GDP. Institutional-sector balances are flows, not loan applications or corporate cash stock. [ONS sector accounts](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/nationalaccounts/uksectoraccounts/bulletins/quarterlysectoraccounts/apriltojune2026).

In September, net house-purchase approvals were 53,400 and different-lender remortgage approvals 42,600. Newly drawn mortgage effective rates were 4.63%, versus 4.03% on outstanding stock. New time-deposit rates were 4.42%, versus 3.44% outstanding time and 1.66% sight. Those differences create testable funding/retention/repricing questions, not a measured Lloyds margin loss. [BoE Money and Credit](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/money-and-credit/2026/september-2026).

The Q3 Credit Conditions Survey covers three months to end August, with August–September fieldwork. Lenders reported reduced SME credit availability and demand, versus unchanged large-corporate availability/demand. Expectations for the next period are not realised outcomes, and survey balances are not rejection rates. This predates the November policy move. [BoE survey](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/credit-conditions-survey/2026/2026-q3).

Public net debt of £3,005bn at end September and monthly borrowing of £24.5bn are different measures. Refinancing and interest-rate exposure can affect fiscal choices, but the reviewed figures establish no particular public procurement or vendor budget. [ONS fiscal release](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/governmentpublicsectorandtaxes/publicsectorfinance/bulletins/publicsectorfinances/september2026).

## Intermediation, capital and market risks

The July FSR's major-bank CET1 aggregate (Table 6.A: Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, Nationwide, NatWest, Santander UK and Standard Chartered) is **14.2% for Q1 2026**, not a November current-bank metric or an individual requirement. Its household mortgage debt-service ratio is **7.5% for end-2025** with mortgage interest plus principal in the numerator and aggregate adjusted nominal post-tax household income in the denominator (pension adjustment, excluding gross operating surplus/FISIM and adding interest paid), not a typical mortgagor burden; later stress/scenario paths are forecasts, not current outturns. [FSR](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-stability-report/2026/july-2026).

September's FPC judged banks appropriately capitalised with high liquidity while describing interconnected sovereign-debt, risky-credit/asset and AI/cyber vulnerabilities. Market-based finance includes leveraged funds, private credit and gilt-repo connections; those risks cannot be attributed equally to every bank or investor. Resilience and existing controls are counter-evidence to a system-collapse or missing-control thesis. [FPC record](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/financial-policy-committee-record/2026/september-2026).

The intermediation value chain is household/company/government savings and funding → deposits, capital markets, investment/insurance/pension pools → underwriting, investment and risk transfer → payments and customer outcomes. This is a functional sector map. No bilateral customer contract, bank ownership or actual counterparty exposure is implied.

## Commercially useful distinctions

**Research question:** which customer financing or servicing journeys are materially affected by repricing, credit availability or transition constraints, and what response already works? Candidate role categories: bank treasury/product/risk/customer operations, corporate finance and public debt-management teams. Actual authority, access and openness remain unknown. Ask for matched-period funding and outcomes, segmentation, existing hedges/arrears support/incumbent coverage, and process-only alternatives. A tighter system condition does not qualify an enterprise Material Pressure or external opportunity.

**Counter-case:** better aggregate household income, net lending and strong bank resilience may make intervention unnecessary. Conversely an aggregate good outcome can conceal a specific vulnerable group; obtain direct, rights-eligible segment evidence before claiming customer pain.
