## Four distinct Leadership Agenda candidates — FLORA interpretations

These are **FLORA-derived investigation questions**, informed by company-stated priorities; they are not leadership endorsements, qualified opportunities or independently verified buyer needs. [Full SI-1-shaped evidence/decision records](Agenda-Candidates.json).

1. **Operating leverage after existing improvements.** COO/CFO categories: which core-platform/service intervention still changes a measured unit outcome after funded modernisation? Income rose, recurring operating costs did not materially fall; lower remediation improved total costs. Obtain baseline, current remedy and remaining choice before proposing another transformation.
2. **Retail customer value and deposit mix.** Retail/product/treasury categories: does management’s Q1 savings/pricing explanation fit actual cohort and customer-outcome evidence, and is any choice still open? Group deposits rose, so this is not a liquidity rescue. Test cohort balances, planned pricing, retention and internal alternatives; no causal attrition claim.
3. **AI benefit assurance within committed platforms.** COO/finance/risk categories: which use case has an unresolved net-benefit or customer-outcome measurement decision beyond Microsoft/Envoy and internal controls? £50m reported historical benefit counters “no value”; expected £100m is not realised or available budget. Ask for permitted benefit definition, net costs and who can still change the measurement/control design.
4. **Credit/customer outcomes after scenario changes.** CRO/credit/customer operations: does the higher impairment expense reflect model/scenario effects, actual cohort deterioration or another explanation after existing support? Stable arrears counter a blanket distress thesis. Require decomposition, actual outcomes and existing credit controls before any intervention recommendation.

Current strategy's circa £2bn gross savings and below-45% 2030 cost:income ambitions are **targets**, not realised values or external spend (LBG-STRATEGY).
