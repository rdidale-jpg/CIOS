## Economic patterns that cannot share one domain template

| Family | Useful economic/value structure | Common misleading substitution to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial bank | Funding stock/cost → earning assets and services → income, costs, impairment, capital/returns | Treating deposits as revenue, a stock delta as organic inflow or capital as a P&L deduction |
| Insurer | Premium/service result, claims, reserves, reinsurance, investment income and solvency | Reusing a lending-spread diagram or treating written premium as a bank's income basis |
| Airline / holidays | Seats/frequency/yield/ancillaries, aircraft/crew/fuel/maintenance, cash/capex; holidays separate | Fleet/routes as profit, revenue−pretax profit as cost, or Group airline allocations to a route/capability/holidays |
| Cloud / software / services / data centres | Consumption/subscription/licence/utilisation; services labour/contract delivery; facilities capex/power/interconnection | One “Technology margin” across SaaS, hyperscale platforms, consultancies and colocation; global→UK allocation |
| Telecoms | Network build/access/utilisation, segment customer economics, opex/capex/cash and wholesale obligations | Homes passed as connected customers, or Group financials as Openreach/retail segment results |
| Energy / water | Generation, network regulated asset base/allowed returns, retail; physical service/assets and environmental outcomes | Treating generation, networks, retail and water as one business or treating a multiyear commitment as available spend |
| Media / Sport | Audiences, rights/content, advertising/subscription/licence; league/club/institution distributions and funding | Monetising the same rights twice or treating a regulator/funder as a commercial broadcaster/club |
| Public Sector / MOD / regulators / payment infrastructure | Mandate, public/fee funding, service/output/outcome and exact fiscal/operating perimeter | Public budget/payment throughput as revenue, buyer budget or open procurement; regulator activity as bank economics |

## Dependencies and risks

1. **Integration baseline:** #701 remains separate; any later implementation must reverify main and reconcile overlap rather than stack on unmerged design/runtime branches.
2. **Evidence/identity:** reuse current public-eligible inputs. Candidate rights, unread source or unresolved Group/entity identity blocks only the affected claim/relationship, not unrelated eligible material. Resolve through existing owners.
3. **Art and interactions:** only UK/Banking/Lloyds plus easyJet preview have the current demonstrated shell. Source geometry and destination coverage must survive any adaptation. The new Industry-art demand is a real dependency, not a reason to substitute generic building blocks.
4. **Access:** production private records and current canonical receipts require authorised read-only services. No global/private run enumeration to make an inventory look complete; existing tenant and Source denial remain fail-closed.
5. **Editorial/commercial safety:** legacy scenario scores, seeded “known incumbents”, research hypotheses and opportunity rows cannot become verified customer need by restyling. Review content and semantics before a new default route.
6. **Lifecycle:** stale/withdrawn source removes dependent visual, text, inspection/accessibility, calculations and current print/export. A UI asset hash and a publication timestamp alone are insufficient evidence of current applicability.
