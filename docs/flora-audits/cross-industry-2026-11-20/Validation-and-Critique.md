## Self-critique

**Strongest evidence:** actual route handlers, finite selectors, dataset declarations, immutable source paths, reproducible named populations and fresh representative renders. The inventory deliberately keeps canonical maturity, public eligibility, runtime capability, visual quality and financial readiness independent. Concrete counterexamples (Nationwide versus NatWest, preview versus legacy easyJet, NVIDIA versus a generic provider shell) prevent a blanket sector score.

**Most important limit:** current private production acceptance and deployed runtime cannot be derived from GitHub or a historical trace. Model counts, tenants and accepted receipts remain unverified. Absence from a public catalogue is not absence of private intelligence. Likewise, a source configuration is a potential input, not a completed research dossier.

**Coverage limit:** 371 owner-qualified rows cover declared/registered provider, source, bank, dossier, actor, TEL/TMS/MOD and supplier-role populations. They are not every incidental company mention in prose, every source reference in an archive or a legal-entity master list. No company identities are fabricated to make a single additive count. New candidates beyond the bounded populations require the existing owners' identity/source interpretation.

**Visual judgement limit:** template/route structure and current observations substantiate presentation gaps, but automated no-overflow is not proof of beautiful simplicity, readable dense tables, accessible touch usage or a successful sales conversation. This audit adds no fresh independent design panel or user study and makes no numeric maturity or commercial-value score. Its priority order is an architectural/product judgement, with reasons, for owner review.

**Source/legal limit:** existing flags, permits and restrictions are inspected and preserved; no new legal opinion, issuer permission or copying right is inferred. A readable report, a fixed dataset hash, a design calculation or a current public route does not automatically authorise a new analytical use. Specific source exceptions remain with the current Source/legal owners; ordinary eligible public research need not be globally blocked.

**Recommendation challenge:** a wide “align every industry” implementation would risk repeating a bank's model everywhere, restyling unqualified commercial content and exposing research administration. The proposed first sprint is intentionally only entry-point continuity, exploiting useful already-built content. It does not solve Aviation Industry art, Technology lifecycle parity or portfolio-wide canonical integration; those are finite subsequent workstreams, not concealed completion claims.

**No independent agent review is claimed.** This is the requested self-critique and evidence-based stocktake. All existing runtime, canonical, Source, IAM, Offers and Pipeline owners remain unchanged. Owner decisions are listed in [Next-Decisions.md](Next-Decisions.md); stop before implementation, merge or deployment.
