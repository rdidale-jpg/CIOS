## EMPTY-STATE CLASSIFICATION

**True Unknowns:** Explicit unresolved buyers, budgets, precise private procurement timing, detailed cloud/OSS/BSS estates, TalkTalk metrics, VodafoneThree combined metrics and enterprise-specific operating details. They remain Unknown until governed evidence resolves them.

**Source gaps:** Openreach/VodafoneThree standalone financial reports; consistent named accountable executives; CityFibre/TalkTalk supplier detail; longitudinal buying/behaviour evidence; several private contract values and renewal dates.

**Extraction gaps:** Explicit purpose/value mechanics and some economics/ecosystem facts are embedded across organisation, strategy, financial, procurement and relationship Evidence but are not canonicalised as such. Do not fill where source material merely makes them plausible.

**Reasoning gaps:** Common pressure → commercial implication → validation/evidence-demand reasoning; ecosystem “who matters and why”; prioritised confirm/disprove learning; defensible reinvention timing.

**Runtime gaps:** Canonical leadership/governance, Enterprise Economics and Enterprise Behaviour owners are absent. Their architectural sources are Draft/Review, so implementation also needs owner-authority confirmation.

**Presentation gaps:** Relationship facts do not become an executive ecosystem explanation; research contracts are reachable but do not answer “learn next” beside each opening; pressure facts appear but their decision consequence is not systematically exposed.

**Architectural gaps:** None proved for the recommended increment. Cross-enterprise account ranking is not presently owned by accepted Enterprise Foundation architecture and would mix provider/account preference with enterprise truth; defer rather than invent governance.

## OPPORTUNITY DECISION CHAIN

**Strongest existing chain:** VMO2 Evidence → financial/technology observations (2026 investment, Lumi/RAN) → change programmes → associated governed opportunities → dated watchpoints. Equivalent lineage exists through IDs/evidence references without relabelling every object.

**Weakest common boundary:** fact/change/pressure → explicit, governed commercial implication and then → opportunity-specific confirmation/disproof need. The executive derivative currently selects a leading programme/fact and says associated opportunities expose a potential supplier-change surface, but does not establish why a particular pressure strengthens, weakens or times a particular hypothesis.

**Commercial consequence:** A Sales Director can see evidence and openings but must manually decide urgency, relevance and next learning. This increases the risk of treating association as causation, chasing weak signals, or missing pressure-triggered timing.

## ALTERNATIVES

**Recommended candidate vs #2:** Both are fast and reuse existing uncertainty. Pressure threads first identify *which* fact changes urgency and qualification; #2 then turns its material uncertainty into “learn next.” Building demands first risks a generic missing-field list rather than a decision-changing validation need. Defer only the opportunity-specific demand projection, not existing Research Gaps.

**Recommended candidate vs #3:** Ecosystem intelligence improves “who matters,” but significance is uneven and requires relationship-role reasoning. Every enterprise already has strong pressures, so the recommended capability offers broader and earlier prioritisation value with less risk of overstating supplier relationships.

**Evidence that would change ranking:** #2 becomes first if tests show pressure/opportunity reasoning already exists canonically while executives still cannot locate confirm/disprove needs. #3 becomes first if governed relationship roles, incumbency, decision rights and access routes are demonstrably complete across six. Leadership rises only with consistent sourced accountability and links to programmes/opportunities. Economics rises if cost/value-driver coverage becomes strong and its owner gains accepted authority.

## RECOMMENDED NEXT SPRINT

**Name:** Evidence-Bounded Material Pressure Decision Thread.

**Objective:** expose, without persistence or promotion, a traceable pressure → affected change/opening → commercial implication → confirm/disprove watchpoint thread for each enterprise, preserving truthful absence.

**Canonical owner:** Canonical Factual Projection remains owner of supplied pressures; semantic relationships retain Programme/Opportunity ownership; accepted ADR-014/ADR-024 constrain reasoning and lineage. Any EI-004 pattern is explicitly Draft input, not authority.

**Gap type:** **REASONING** with the smallest necessary **PRESENTATION** increment.

**Why this is the highest-value next move:** It directly improves account/opportunity prioritisation, proposition shaping, timing and weak-signal avoidance across all six, using already strong evidence rather than commissioning a new data domain.

**Existing Evidence/runtime it reuses:** five pressure facts for every enterprise; financial/technology/transformation facts; 13 programmes, 17 opportunities, 308 relationships; procurement/timing fields; Evidence references, Unknowns and Contradictions; existing read-only executive derivative.

**Smallest implementation increment:** next sprint, add a read-only, bounded decision thread to the existing executive derivative for only explicitly supported pressure/change/opportunity associations. Show the source fact IDs/Evidence, material caveat and one confirmation or disproof need when supplied/derivable under accepted reasoning. Do not add persistence, ontology, pressure scores, opportunity mutation or inferred procurement.

**Expected executive outcome:** A Sales Director can state “this evidenced pressure is why this opening matters now; this is the trigger to monitor; this uncertainty would weaken it,” and can deprioritise where that chain is absent.

**Acceptance principle:** Every presented link resolves to governed objects and Evidence; inference is labelled; Unknowns/Contradictions survive; absence is truthful; no association is presented as causation or procurement; underlying Twin, Opportunity and Evidence tuples remain byte/structurally unchanged.
