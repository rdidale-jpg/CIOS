**Status:** Owner-agreed **product experience direction**, 18 November 2026. **Proposed repository design contract for owner confirmation and downstream implementation; not an accepted architecture ADR, not a runtime change.** This records the owner's express acceptance of the three-level presentation matrix and the *two-option* **Facts | FLORA Insight** selector at the top of Main. It does not reclassify IT-001 (Review), EIF-001 (Review), EI-001 (Draft), EGM-001 (Founder Review), or the INT-MAP-1 draft as accepted canonical architecture.

**Mission:** One comprehensible intelligence experience across **UK → Industry → Enterprise**, with **Facts as the default in Main at every level and every primary perspective**. FLORA Insight is a deliberate alternative rendering of **the same selected subject**, not a route to obscuring or modifying factual knowledge.

## 1. Two independent choices

1. **Primary perspective (Top):** *What am I exploring?* Landscape, Forces, Inside (Enterprise only) or What's changing? These change the semantic **content in Main**, not merely visual styling. The Left illustrated context may change appropriately to the selected perspective, but remains navigable and meaningful.
2. **Reading mode (top of Main):** *How do I read the selected subject?* **Facts (default) | FLORA Insight**. The two labels are always explicit and visible in the same location, with Facts selected on initial entry, new subject/level navigation and ordinary link sharing unless an expressly selected authorised insight deep link is used. Mode change does not change scope, subject, selected capability, primary perspective or time period.

**The mode selector does not turn knowledge governance on/off.** Both modes use source-use/permission controls; only the presentation/interpretation changes. Facts may include attributed statements, sourced numbers, reproducible calculations and **explicitly labelled conceptual structure**; never present an invented entity relationship or business flow as a verified fact. FLORA Insight contains supported interpretation, mechanism comparisons, implications, explicit alternative explanations, and carefully labelled hypotheses/recommendations where eligible. No hidden synthetic customer need.

## 4. Exact Top / Left / Main interaction contract

- **Top:** hierarchy `UK / Industry / Enterprise`, subject name, context and level-allowed primary perspective control. Four Enterprise choices may need responsive overflow, but never display unsupported administrative options. Changes to the perspective update Main in place via finite authorised read routes or native links, with Left coherently illustrating new context.
- **Left:** rich premium ivory/forest-green/gold editorial scene. UK = national economic landscape; Industry = value/economic ecosystem; Enterprise = value stream/capability operating system. Left selects a *subject* (UK sector/force, industry role/enterprise/stage, enterprise capability/dependency), never manufactures relationships via mere spatial proximity. Wide layouts persist; on tablet portrait/mobile a compact accessible disclosed scene precedes Main and selection brings Main into view.
- **Main:** selected primary perspective + same selected subject + **Facts | FLORA Insight** two-option segmented control **at the top of Main**. Facts first; insight not inserted as a large forced `FLORA INSIGHT` paragraph into Facts unless a user-selected inline quoted analyst attribution genuinely belongs to factual reporting. Separate title/reading-state semantics, primary source dating and concise scope.
- **Context selectors** for region, bank group/legal entity, business segment, industry submarket, subject/capability and period are *secondary* and only appear when their owning model supports them. Don't create a wall of tabs. A selected subject can move between perspectives if semantically compatible; if not, use an explicit, user-comprehensible reset to the closest supported scope. On level changes default Facts.
- **Shareable URL/history:** serialise exact level/subject/perspective/time scope; reading mode may be included in deliberate insight sharing, but ordinary/default links open Facts. Browser Back/Forward, direct reload, no JavaScript and keyboard/touch must reproduce the meaningful view without hidden role/workspace claims.
- **Status:** a timestamp is never a substitute for model freshness. Distinguish source publication, event occurrence, period of measurement, legal-effective date, retrieval, and study cut. “Latest”, “today” and “recent” are prohibited unless supported by genuine current input and known horizon. An announcement is not actual implementation.
- **Cross-level links:** UK force → Industry mechanism → Enterprise exposure is a *contextual question* until exact evidence supports target applicability. Enterprise→Industry pattern requires materially comparable independent examples, not a count of headlines. Do not draw causal arrows merely because pages are linked.
- **Default editorial style:** large visual structure first, selective measured facts, short strong declarative copy, no geek speak, no giant source tables, nested evidence disclosures, redundant disclaimers, decorative unsupported scores or hidden owner data. Internal provenance accessible in authorised Research/Owner; compact mandatory licence attribution in Main only as required.

## 5. Facts and interpretation boundaries

| Property | Facts | FLORA Insight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Types | `reported`, `calculated` when reproducible; conceptual models labelled **Conceptual**; reported management ambitions remain **Reported plan/claim** | `insight`, `hypothesis`, estimates and assumptions only as correctly classified, sourced and caveated; recommendations only under their existing owner |
| Permitted content | Defined perimeter, date, units/basis; actual reported values and relationships; methods of arithmetic; status of programmes/events | Consequence mechanisms, comparisons, plausible competing causes, supported opportunities for further investigation and what would falsify the interpretation |
| Forbidden confusion | Company outlook as delivered result; source quote as independent effectiveness; brand as legal entity; scheme as operator; payment throughput as operator fees | Unknown as established exposure; commentary as canonical Material Pressure; speculative sales intent as qualified opportunity |
| On source withdrawal / rights change | Suppress precisely dependent Facts and their labels/counts/accessibility text | Invalidate dependent Insight also. Never retain an inference after its input is no longer eligible |
| Insufficient evidence | Present eligible bounded facts or a concise grounded unavailability state; no invented filler | “No supported FLORA Insight for this selection” with optional different subject; never an unsupported confident conclusion |

**Critical reconciliation:** CIOS already has an `intelligence_presentation` six-type policy. This product contract proposes **two reading modes** over those types; it does not create two new canonical Evidence classes, accept a new enum, replace underlying six-type classification, or loosen the public use / access owner. The source owner decides what may be displayed, not the user's choice of Facts/Insight.

2. Banking Landscape Payments opens Facts with *conceptual* initiation/clearing/settlement and true scheme/operator distinctions; Insight explains mechanism without fictional Pay.UK income or public-admitting research-only candidate participation.
3. UK/Banking Forces July 2026 CTP event Facts identifies exact designated legal entity; Insight can explain industry relevance but **does not** join Lloyds Bank plc Envoy critical workload to Google Cloud EMEA Limited.
4. Nationwide Enterprise Landscape Facts reconciles 6,378−4,021−331=2,026 and 2,026+1−138−399=1,490 (GBP millions, Group FY2026); Insight discusses member-value/integration comparison **without** double-counting £127m already in underlying operating costs or treating Society complaints as consolidated earnings.
5. Enterprise Inside choose Fleet/Network/Customer (easyJet concept) or bank capability; selecting Facts gives actual dated measures and labelled operating stages, Insight derives explainable mechanisms; both modes retain exact capability and no fabricated systems/vendors/outcome metrics.
6. For What's changing? filter completed/announced/upcoming separately; changed publication date, superseded effective date and dated historical example never turn into current-week News. Missing eligible supply either withholds the tab or shows genuinely substantive historical period clearly.
7. Shift perspective and reading mode, browser Back/Forward/reload, 200% text, iPad/mobile, keyboard/touch and no JS. Native semantic controls, visible focus and accessible labels represent same state; no ghost selected unsupported subject.
8. Invalid, withdrawn, owner-only, restricted, cross-tenant or conflicting source removes both reading-mode dependent projections in HTML, metadata, URL, export, cached output and assistive labels; unrelated facts survive.
9. An enterprise-level insight does not persist/change canonical Enterprise Model, accepted Material Pressure, qualified Opportunity, Pipeline, permissions or source-use decisions.
10. Source revisions in one level trigger **reassessment of relevant derived views**, not automatic copying a country rule to all enterprises or silently rewriting historical records.
