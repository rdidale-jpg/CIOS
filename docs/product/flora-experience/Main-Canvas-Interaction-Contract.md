# Main Canvas Visual Interaction Contract

**Authority:** Mandatory owner product decision, 25 November 2026. Applies to executive Enterprise and Industry presentation. This refines the accepted Top / Left / Main experience; it creates no architecture, intelligence owner, source permission or canonical model. It does not restore the superseded Facts / FLORA Insight selector. [Delivery Doctrine](../flora-delivery-acceleration/README.md) and existing evidence/security ownership remain binding.

Main is a visual intelligence canvas. Executive subjects must be explored through meaningful visual regions, not a document of navigation labels.

## Required interaction

- The complete business/division, financial stage/bar, operating layer, capability, organisation branch, goal/programme event, SWOT quadrant or theme is its interaction target. A heading inside an otherwise inert decorative region is insufficient.
- Use native semantic links for navigation and buttons only for actual local actions. Keep stable URLs, direct reload, browser Back/Forward and selected scope/subject. Regions must work without JavaScript.
- Provide clear hover, visible keyboard focus, selected state and touch feedback. Targets must be at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels; important visual targets are materially larger. The artwork and Main context must remain available on desktop/tablet, with accessible compact equivalents on mobile.
- Use subject-specific compositions: reporting branches, economic flows and bars, capability/value-system layers, dated disclosure/goal rails and a four-quadrant SWOT. Do not turn the whole product into a uniform card grid.
- A whole SWOT quadrant and each full theme region must be independently selectable without nested links. Keyboard order and touch hit areas must match the visual hierarchy.
- Retain actual dates, entity/perimeter, financial type/basis, source dependencies, important caveats and counter-evidence. Visual geometry must not imply unmeasured financial shares, progress, legal reporting lines or verified installed dependencies.

## Financial and operating substance

Use whole-region charts, financial reconciliations and operating maps to answer a named executive question with enterprise-specific evidence. Show period, currency, unit, basis, signs and comparable perimeter on financial views. Make carried assumptions and a route to provenance available during exploration. Keep targets separate from reported actuals and show a progress comparison only when the measures and timing support it. Where evidence is limited, label the conceptual model and explain the unresolved dependency.

## Prohibited in executive Main

Underlined inline navigation; lists of text hyperlinks; standalone Explore/Investigate/More text links; navigation concealed in paragraphs; tiny clickable chart labels; decorative compositions whose separate small heading is the only link. Removing an underline with CSS alone does not satisfy this contract.

## Evidence and specialist exceptions

Necessary external citations, mandatory legal attribution, evidence inspection and authorised specialist inspection retain their real semantic hyperlinks. Keep them unobtrusive and distinguish them from principal executive navigation. Do not disable, conceal, remove or blanket-restyle legal citations to satisfy an underline check. Parent-return controls remain obvious, focusable control surfaces, not disguised prose.

## Lloyds implementation boundary

This sprint applies the contract to the current Lloyds CEO/Gold Main renderer across Landscape, Inside, Forces and its contextual drill-downs. Preserve the original Left illustration/geometry and existing URLs. Customer-outcome and funding selections use the already-supported canvas detail destinations and their existing owning services; historical agenda URLs stay directly addressable. Legacy predecessor and optional historical preview routes remain compatibility evidence, not new executive defaults. Other industries inherit this mandatory product rule; their runtime rollout is not claimed by the Lloyds change.

No new facts, financial calculations, data stores, permission grants, canonical promotions or Pipeline actions are authorised. Existing Lloyds §2 holds and §5 PDF-link deferral remain in place.

## Acceptance evidence

1. Audit all Lloyds default subjects and finite detail states. Every internal executive navigation anchor in Main must identify a full visual region, except documented inspection/return controls.
2. Test physical hits away from the title, including chart bars, business body, lower operating layers, timeline bodies, quadrant background and themes. A computed `text-decoration: none` check alone is inadequate.
3. Verify hover/focus/selection, 44px touch minimum, keyboard and no-JS, screen-reader names and source-dependent accessible text, history/reload/direct links and desktop/iPad/mobile overflow/zoom.
4. Compare actual equivalent desktop and both iPad orientations before/after. Preserve original artwork, Source withdrawal, public/private isolation and regression/security tests.
5. Obtain owner visual acceptance before merge or deployment. Passing tests establishes implementation evidence, not commercial or visual acceptance.
