## Critique

The owner’s independent iPad findings and #677 owner-side assessment drive the corrections in [the scope audit](scope.md). Further independent specialist review of this draft remains open. The design self-review identified three tradeoffs:

- A common interaction shell can make dissimilar subjects feel interchangeable. Keep PESTLE connected through sector effects, Industry anchored to value and competitive mechanisms, and Inside structured around actual activity and capability.
- A sparse screen can hide a structural or evidence omission. Lead with what is supported and the missing discriminator, then provide the closest eligible content. Treat empty wording alone as a failure in a root view with admissible facts.
- A smooth industry-to-company journey can overstate applicability. Use the precise legal and reporting owner for each view and contextual links where linkage is unproven. For screenshots, cropped Left on small layouts has no bearing on accessibility or touch until tested in a live implementation.

The observed captures and HTTP sweep use the exact source baseline as Guest. Static proposal captures verify visual fit, not navigation, browser-state preservation, permissions, domain evidence admission or deployment. Those tests are reserved for authorised implementation.
