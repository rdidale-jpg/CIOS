# Content readiness and fallback contract

This is a review checklist for existing owners, keyed to [the original 16 design requirements](requirements.csv). It does not register new runtime requirements or source permissions.

## Common admission and availability

Evaluate the **owner-qualified level, subject, perspective and period** together. Verify identity (including legal entity versus Group, brand, scheme or role), exact source and intended use, as-of/reporting/basis/units, permitted public form and source lifecycle. Record evidence provenance and dependency internally. Keep candidate rights/identity/acceptance and stale/conflicting claims outside the approved composition until the existing owner resolves them. Absence of a canonical receipt blocks a claim of canonical acceptance; it does not disqualify separately authorised analytical facts. A visible legacy rendering or configured source is not proof of public eligibility.

- **Ready**: meaningful eligible baseline exists. Facts starts with it. Insight requires at least one admissible premise, an explanatory relation for that selection, clear inferential bounds, and a material alternative or discriminating gap.
- **Partial**: eligible fragments remain useful; show them in the perspective's normal composition, the time/scope and the missing relationship or measure. Insight preserves those anchors, and gives a scoped mechanism only where the dependency passes. Otherwise show the eligible anchor and a precise reason no interpretation can be made. Never redirect a mode switch to populate a different subject.
- **Unavailable**: no meaningful eligible composition remains. On an existing ordinary root show brief public-safe unavailability and the closest valid contextual link. A valid leaf with lost supply retains its subject and offers an explicit navigation choice. Invalid or unauthorised requests retain existing handling and do not disclose existence. For Change omit an unearned tab; an old link uses an unavailable state or a clearly labelled navigation return. Never substitute recent retrieval for recent events.

Change readiness distinguishes publication, event, legal effect, reporting period, retrieval and assessment. Tests must suppress restricted, contradictory, superseded or withdrawn dependencies without removing unrelated content. Owner chooses the useful supply threshold per subject and period before activating the tab.
