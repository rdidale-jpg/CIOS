## Evidence and validation

- 552 unique route/query observations, 522 HTTP 200, 22 HTTP 403 and eight HTTP 404. The negatives include restricted specialist views and intentionally invalid selectors. All 160 Technology provider requests across the two route families returned 200. There was no page-level horizontal overflow in the desktop sweep.
- 34 running application captures and 66 static proposal captures. All proposed viewports passed a page-width fit check. The appendix includes both reading modes for the proposed examples. Original Safari PDFs were not attached; direct visual comparison with those files remains open.
- Existing Source owners provide the examples used in the design: HMT historical designation date, Banking payment roles (conceptual), Lloyds Group facts and management target, and existing conceptual Technology and easyJet operating subjects. Their exact business, regulatory and contractual applicability is limited as shown.
