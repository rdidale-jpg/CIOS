# Design critique and owner acceptance

This is a self-critique of a working **Phase A design arrangement**, not an independent review or an operational Enterprise Model. No independent reviewer was commissioned for this bounded design-only phase.

## What improves; what remains imperfect

**Commercial usefulness.** Landscape now answers who the bank serves, what it supplies and how funding/lending/earnings connect. Two clearly scoped Group figures ground the business without inventing divisional allocations. Inside distinguishes activities from durable capabilities and disclosed technology direction. Forces connects a dated signal to a conditional business mechanism, preserving counter-evidence and the eight existing SWOT themes. The design does not turn those themes into sales opportunities.

**Density.** A single headline and explanatory visual sit together on desktop and tablet landscape. There are four integrated image regions, no duplicate subject-button row and no Main accordions. Forces is longer because the existing timeline and SWOT remain useful; they follow the selected explanation rather than displacing it. Material caveats still take space, particularly for AI and regulatory applicability. The next implementation should edit repeated wording, not remove those qualifications or hide a wall of disclosures one click deeper.

**Visual identity.** The original architectural interior gives Lloyds one recognisable identity. Selected regions, labels and pressure emphasis change by layer. This deliberately reuses one original image, **not three newly commissioned illustrations**. The spaces are conceptual; a credit/risk label over a meeting room does not verify which teams work together. Owner inspection must decide whether these overlays communicate enough differentiation before any application work. All original illustration files remain untouched.

**Interaction.** The current Top perspective control switches the layer. Left supplies subject selection; Main explains. Native links, focus outlines, selected-region state, Back/Forward and reload work. Where a subject has no supported cross-layer counterpart, its name stays visible but the explanation is explicitly Group-level. This finite view correspondence is navigation only, not an inferred dependency model. A more granular capability-to-division mapping needs actual evidence and is outside this sprint.

**Tablet trade-off.** At 1194 × 834 both scene and message are visible. At 834 × 1194, the scene collapses behind one `Explore Lloyds` control to prioritise Main; opening it necessarily pushes the explanation below the artwork. Selecting a region returns attention to Main. This is simulated Chromium touch, not physical iPad/Safari certification. The inherited product header wraps at portrait widths; no new global navigation redesign was attempted.

**Lifecycle.** The local design renderer uses existing source eligibility and withdrawal guards. The downloadable HTML is a dated public design snapshot; it cannot receive future withdrawal notifications and must not be deployed as live intelligence. Existing internal source inspection remains available in actual authorised Flora; this public-only prototype does not pretend to authenticate or exercise Owner permissions.

## Owner acceptance checklist

- [ ] Landscape answers **what is the business?** without SWOT, process detail or a large Curve banner.
- [ ] Inside answers **how does it operate?** without duplicate financial statements or unsupported team/system assignments.
- [ ] Forces answers **what is changing and why?** with dated context, conditional mechanisms, all eight SWOT themes and a usable timeline.
- [ ] The same richly illustrated enterprise feels recognisable across all three layers and distinct from Banking's external ecosystem.
- [ ] Four image regions are understandable, selected state is unmistakable, and room placement does not suggest actual premises, proven ownership or relative economic size.
- [ ] Overview → Explore → Investigate remains a coherent journey without another Main navigation row.
- [ ] Retained subject context is useful and does not imply unverified divisional exposure.
- [ ] Desktop, iPad landscape and compact/open portrait compositions are acceptable; inspect the actual previews, not only the check results.
- [ ] The source-dependent Curve marker is restrained and its completion wording does not imply integration, procurement or buying demand.
- [ ] £502.8bn is visibly a Group stock at 30 September 2026; £6.6bn is Group statutory pretax profit for nine months. Neither is a division allocation.
- [ ] Material scope and uncertainty remain legible without source administration dominating Main.
- [ ] Accept the proposed contract amendment and bounded Phase B scope, or identify the specific visual correction needed first.

## Smallest subsequent implementation

After visual acceptance, create a **separate implementation branch/PR** from revalidated main. Adapt the existing Lloyds Left composition and current main renderers; add approved image hit geometry and finite view state under those owners. Keep the current frame, original assets, legacy URLs, navigation, financial services, source guards and private inspection. Reorganise current content rather than cherry-picking old renderers or introducing another model. Test #728 behaviours, full strict security/comparator, withdrawal in every representation, browser history, public/private isolation, Financial/Pipeline boundaries and Banking/Aviation journeys.

Do not ship temporary render patches, self-contained snapshots or a second prototype server into application routes. No new canonical Economics/Twin object, research system, rights registry, automatic pressure/opportunity qualification or financial-run creation is needed.

**Scaling.** Reuse this responsibility/selection contract, not the bank's subjects. easyJet can use fleet/network/customer operations and aviation economics already held by existing owners. Telecoms needs service/network/customer dependencies; Technology needs provider-specific infrastructure/platform/service mechanisms. Each uses its own existing art, business model, permissions and evidence. Do not claim the other enterprise portraits are implemented by this Lloyds design.
