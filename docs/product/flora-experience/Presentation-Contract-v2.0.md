# FLORA Presentation Contract v2.0 — proposed amendment

**Phase A proposal for owner visual acceptance, 23 November 2026.** This amends the [existing Experience Contract](FLORA-Experience-Contract-v1.0.md), rather than introducing a second presentation framework or intelligence model. The [Product Delivery Doctrine](../flora-delivery-acceleration/README.md) remains authoritative for delivery. Application implementation is not authorised until the visual acceptance below.

PR #728 already implements unified eligible facts and interpretation. The old v1 instructions requiring a separate Facts / FLORA Insight selector are historical and superseded. Retain the underlying six semantic types, source lifecycle, actual/target distinction, material uncertainty and separate canonical authority. Do not restore the reading-mode fork.

## Four responsibilities

| Region / perspective | Commercial responsibility | Include | Keep elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Landscape** | **What is the business?** | Identity/perimeter; customers, products and divisions; business model, economic value creation, scale, positioning and competitive context | Detailed processes, long SWOT, repeated pressure analysis and large unrelated announcements |
| **Inside** | **How does it operate?** | Value streams distinct from capabilities; functions and supported organisational relationships; people/process/data/technology; disclosed suppliers, dependencies, transformation delivery and operational performance | Generic company introductions, unconnected industry commentary and duplicate financial statements |
| **Forces** | **What is changing and why?** | Market, economic, regulatory, competitor and technology pressures; enterprise-specific exposure where supported; strategic responses, SWOT, dated change and conditional implications | Full business-model repetition; assumed enterprise responses to generic conditions; automatic sales opportunities |
| **Left** | **Where am I in the enterprise?** | A persistent, illustrated enterprise identity with selectable subjects, supported relationships and restrained change markers | A second text menu, document list, decorative unrelated image or duplicate Main |

**Main explains the selected subject.** Prefer one key message and one meaningful principal visual together, supported by a small number of correctly scoped facts. Use the existing Top / Left / Main frame, editorial typography and ivory / forest / restrained-gold palette. No new global selector or extra subject-button row. Legal attribution and material factual limitations remain; optional detailed inspection uses the existing authorised Research/Owner route.

## Selection and continuity

- Each enterprise layer has **three to five** meaningful image regions. A native link is the accessible hit target; a visible outline and label identify the selected area. Region area, colour and location never imply economic weight, severity, legal ownership or a real building layout.
- Left retains a recognisable enterprise identity across Landscape, Inside and Forces. Layers change the relevant visual emphasis and corresponding Main content. Industry artwork remains an **ecosystem**; enterprise artwork represents its own business/operating system.
- Preserve enterprise, original selected subject, compatible focus and URL/history state. A subject is not automatically evidence of a cross-perspective relationship. If no direct counterpart exists, use the nearest supported **Group-level context**, display the retained subject and state the applicable perimeter; do not imply a divisional system or proven exposure.
- Main may include a genuinely useful contextual link: **Overview → Explore → Investigate**. In the Lloyds concept these correspond to Landscape → Inside → Forces. Preserve #728's behaviours and eight interactive SWOT themes; relocating them does not authorise removing existing runtime deep links.
- On desktop/tablet landscape, Left remains visible while Main explains. On portrait/mobile, a single compact disclosure opens the same scene and returns attention to Main after selection. Without JavaScript, native links and all relevant content remain addressable.
- Support keyboard focus, Enter, touch, text zoom, Back/Forward and reload. No hover-only information. Unsupported, private or malformed state fails closed.

## Lloyds allocation

Funding, deposit stocks, profit, costs and financial structure belong in Landscape. Capabilities, processes, technology and evidenced supplier scope belong in Inside. External pressures, strategic responses, eight SWOT themes and strategy timeline belong in Forces. Curve is a **source-dependent contextual marker and relevant change explanation**, not a large general arrival banner. Completion of an acquisition establishes neither integration progress, procurement nor buying demand.

The [Living Enterprise prototype and allocation matrix](living-enterprise-v2/README.md) demonstrate this amendment with existing art and current eligible views. Illustrated regions and flows are **conceptual**, not actual premises, a verified organisation chart, measured divisional allocations or a canonical Enterprise Model. Missing information is omitted from executive views and retained in existing research/owner records.
