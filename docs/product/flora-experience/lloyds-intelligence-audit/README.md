## Executive assessment

**Flora holds materially more Lloyds research than the current screens explain, but it does not hold a complete, verified operational Digital Twin ready to publish.** Three different deficits are being compressed into the same visible “Unavailable”: a scoped source-use hold on researched financial values; candidate facts without a current entity/process/application binding; and genuinely unestablished internal or contractual information. More decorative navigation cannot resolve these deficits.

1. **The financial research is not absent.** LEI-1 holds 13 reproducible reconciliations. Draft RIE-2 has 264 candidate records, including detailed segment/period measurements and operating notes. RIC-1 consolidates RIE-1/RIE-2; its 362 records must not be counted as an independent extra corpus. Current Source/legal §2 decisions still withhold the affected new issuer output. Arithmetic success, an accessible report, or a draft PR cannot resolve that authority. Reuse existing Financial and Source owners; do not invent another engine or impose canonical Evidence promotion on ordinary lawful analytical use.
2. **Some divisional screens contain almost no divisional intelligence.** `FOCUS_FACTS['commercial']` and `['retirement']` are empty. Retail's direct list is two branch-access statements. All three businesses have conceptual product/value descriptions but lack eligible matched contribution, operating ownership and differentiated current outcomes. H1 reporting segments, legal entities, brands and the November management organisation need a dated crosswalk, not guessed joins.
3. **Navigation and retained acquisition work are disconnected.** The current data has ten conceptual capabilities, five programme records and 123 relationships. Only `REV-ENVOY-GOOGLE` is marked verified/source-supported; 108 edges are interpretations. The other records do not establish installed technology, accountable teams, supplier contracts or procurement. Nine navigable process bands remain conceptual. Separately, the merged Initial Factory retains six commercial replay claims (including CIB/remit and appointment facts) and a responsible-AI committee Evidence/Observation proof using the real owners in isolated test authority. Gold does not read that model projection. Those receipts are not production imports or permission to expose private records; they are concrete evidence that repeating research alone will not repair the integration gap.
4. **“All facts surfaced” used a self-selected denominator.** All 19 Lloyds claims in the bounded released projection are observable somewhere in the finite journey. That says nothing about completeness of enterprise research or commercial understanding. The current projection totals 24 statements including external context and a dated government CEO attribution. The wider current owner has 45 claims, of which 23 pass its adapter; many older statements are citation-only or superseded. No evidence-backed global extraction or relationship-completeness percentage can be calculated.
5. **There are actual CSS defects as well as content-design problems.** Technology-region/container class reuse forces credit-layer titles into a 65px column and lets icons intersect text. Large conceptual targets push the few economic facts down the page. The credit and strategy views are roughly 2.5–2.8k CSS pixels tall at tested widths. These are separate from the source and research deficits. The A4 economics export spans three pages with only 63 extracted characters on the last page. No repairs were made.
6. **Commercial insight remains investigatory.** Existing renewal, AI, Curve and regulatory facts can sharpen specific customer conversations. They do not establish buying intent, budget, a named buyer's authority, unallocated external work or a qualified opportunity. Reuse the existing Offer matcher, seller-development lifecycle and human-reviewed Pipeline hand-off. Actual private development/Pipeline records and accepted production canonical objects were not inspected and remain unverified.
