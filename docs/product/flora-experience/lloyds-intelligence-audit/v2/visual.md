# Lloyds experience audit

The baseline is repository SHA `70319e7905e16eb845b91974ca0fda0aca593b8f`. The public [deployment endpoint](https://flora-current.onrender.com/deployment) reported that same SHA on 28 November 2026. Current browser evidence came from an isolated local Chromium runtime of that SHA, with touch enabled in the two iPad viewports and on mobile. This matches the published release version. It does not confirm a production account's browser, permissions, preferences, iPad Safari, or the owner's original print export.

## Direct browser findings

| Perspective | What works | What prevents executive acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landscape | Opening identifies three businesses and explains selected drivers. The original artwork frames navigation and signed detail is inspectable. | Shared map reads like a total-to-parts chart despite incompatible periods and bases. The wide blank canvas around mapped nodes and repeated story push quantitative economics down. On the ordinary group view the main height was 2,604 px on desktop, of which its map used 860 px. |
| Inside | Three routes carry business context. Some actual operating facts and leaders come through. | Large pale operating diagram reads as description before evidence. On mobile the map occupied 1,567 px of a 3,236 px main area. Retail took 7,671 px of scrollable main content, with conceptual explanation and repeated detail delaying useful facts. |
| Forces | Decisions, scoped goals and management response are easy to find once selected. | The horizon timeline and link cards give space to strategic intent without equivalent presence for competitive position, SWOT, risks and evidence checks. The agenda map used 1,662 px at mobile width. Similar artwork and region styling across perspectives make the reading mode less distinctive. |

Forty navigations to the ten [top-level routes](../../../../flora-runtime/lloyds-financials/browser.py) passed HTTP and document-width checks at 1440×1000, 1366×1024, 1024×1366 and 390×844. The probe measured `scrollWidth`, viewport width, main height and `.ce-map` height after fonts loaded. It checked candidate clipping in SVG, canvas node, executive summary and lead text containers. This did not reproduce document-level horizontal overflow or overflow in those sampled elements. Token-level client rectangles found no split amounts among sampled £ values and percentages in visible paragraph and emphasis elements. Cell-level wrapping of long text, small captions, large touch targets and light figure/label treatment are visually evident. Owner-observed token breaks, overlap and low contrast remain specific issues to test on the owner's exact route, viewport and browser settings. This scope does not certify site-wide clipping or WCAG contrast. Test ratios against real composited backgrounds, including text over original art, with WCAG 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text or relevant non-text components.

For archival print differences see [#734 visual diagnostics](../Visual-Diagnostic.md) and its [print evidence](../previews/print-economics-page1.jpg). Printing can introduce fragmentation, thick shapes or clipping through different style rules or page dimensions. Those earlier cases establish an export issue on the captured predecessor, with no automatic reproduction on the current screen. Print reproduction was outside this audit's current screen measurements. Test print separately with the exact affected routes and print settings before assigning a screen root cause.

## Requirements for the next presentation sprint

- Keep original artwork, the existing navigation frame, whole-region links and topic continuity. Put the business result, date and basis first. Avoid lines or hierarchy that imply addition without a valid bridge.
- Show a substantive quantitative contribution or operating outcome before asking readers to explore a large map. Use a compact hierarchy and put provenance, audit language and low-frequency notes behind accessible disclosure.
- State uncertainty where it changes the question. Keep units joined to their figures, test contrast and reflow at compact widths and 200% zoom, and ensure keyboard focus is visible. Use separate print constraints and page break rules.
- Give each perspective a different question and commercial visual grammar using the current visual system. Landscape explains economics and strategy, Inside explains actual operating anatomy, and Forces explains evidence-backed pressure and choices. Keep cross-links to the same verified intelligence and avoid recasting a diagram as measured ownership.
- Owner acceptance needs actual browser runs of business decisions, readable screenshots and a check of comparable reported data, including on a real iPad where possible. Browser tests and successful rendering do not establish usefulness.

Reproduce the interactive baseline with `python docs/flora-runtime/lloyds-financials/browser.py OUTPUT` from repository root using the established Playwright and Chromium setup. The route census uses the script's `TOP_LEVEL` and device dimensions; evidence access and navigation are exercised by the unchanged existing tests. The screenshot and geometry summary are supplied alongside this review.
