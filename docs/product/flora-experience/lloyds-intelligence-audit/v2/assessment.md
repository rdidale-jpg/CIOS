Illustrative SWOT slices for review, **not new durable observations**. Strength is the measured Group improvement and diverse businesses, tempered by the lower remediation comparator. Weakness is increased costs and impairment in matched nine-month results, tempered by income movement and uncertainty over recurrence. Opportunity in the strategic sense is greater customer use of payments after the reported Curve transaction, with realised retention and economics unknown. Threat is service interruption during retirement and migration with regulatory constraints, tempered by stated controls and no measured probability. Materiality, direction, timeframe, evidence confidence, affected scope and management response need their own review before a full quadrant or priority is shown. Business pressures start with margins, growth, market share, retention, credit quality, capital, conduct and execution. Technology appears where a process or response is genuinely affected. Current finance and process sources support bounded questions, not a comparable ranked heatmap. Apply the proposed materiality weights in the standard only after calibration with evidence and independent reviews.
