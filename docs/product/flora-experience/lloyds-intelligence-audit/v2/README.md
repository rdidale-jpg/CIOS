## Owner assessment

**There is enough retained evidence to fix the opening’s financial explanation and deliver a bounded Group strategy chronology.** There is substantially more research than Flora currently connects or makes useful. We reviewed **32 named requirements, one per domain**. All 32 have relevant retained research, 21 have verified evidence for the assessed slice, 14 are connected through a governed model or existing read projection, and 15 are directly usable in the checked Flora experience. One visible slice lacks a governed connection. Levels are 11 Researched, 7 Verified and 14 Operational for the sampled requirements. This intentionally selected set of decision-relevant examples and gaps does not estimate completeness of the full enterprise, its divisions, or production canonical records. No independent confidence percentage is inferred. [Coverage method and rows](coverage.md)

**The financial problem is an implied comparison and a partial bridge.** The £3.926bn H1 underlying subtotal for three businesses needs £289m Other, −£34m restructuring and +£112m volatility and other to reach £4.293bn H1 Group statutory profit. Add £2.332bn Q3 Group statutory profit to reach £6.625bn for nine months. The £216m difference in the nine-month movement is −£35m restructuring plus £251m volatility and other. Current detailed routes already reconcile both. [Financial audit](financial.md)

**Recover before commissioning.** Prior Tier 1, recovery, draft and industry artifacts contain purpose and strategy inputs, identity/role evidence, estate and operating facts, and market comparisons. Use those before acquiring missing facts. The material new research needs are original strategy measures with historical matched actuals, outcomes and economics for real operating stages, comparable competitive metrics, and market and challenge coverage for each major business. Recovered candidates, documented verification, canonical commitment and public rendering have different evidence requirements. [Attributed inventory](inventory.md)

**Integration and presentation are separate from research.** Authorise the smallest financial opening correction using existing signed projections. Test a shared, withdrawable read association across the three views, using verified identity and scoped data. Live workspace binding and grants are unverified. A genuine architecture change would need evidence that existing services cannot represent or resolve a stable, dated, withdrawable relationship. No replacement architecture is justified by the audit. [Architecture and traces](assessment.md#current-connected-architecture-and-target-relationships)

**Next sprint recommendation.** Recover the strategy chronology and complete one measured operating slice for each business. In the same sprint, if implementation is separately authorised, fix the H1 versus nine-month grouping and show the complete signed nine-month movement at the opening. Defer broad visual redesign until the underlying core answers and shared associations are ready. The reported value of technology should be discussed in the context of specific business results. [Prioritised gap register and roadmap](assessment.md#prioritised-gap-register)
