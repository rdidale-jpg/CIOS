# Lloyds Group and divisional coverage

The [standard](../../../../architecture/enterprise-twin-production/enterprise-intelligence-standard-v2.md) supplies the full minimum for each ID. Each row assesses the Group and the material divisional slices, with one **named requirement** tested to keep the quantitative readout auditable. R/V/C/U respectively mean substantive retained research, verified by existing evidence, connected through an existing governed model or read projection, and directly usable in normal Flora. Four digits record independent gates for the named requirement. Gates describe that exact requirement only. A partial or conceptual presentation does not count. **Level** applies to the named requirement, never to the whole domain. Operational proof is baseline local/browser or predecessor archived proof, with no assumed live workspace visibility. C can reflect a governed read projection and does not assert durable canonical membership.

Scope shorthand **G** Group, **R** Retail, **C** Commercial Banking, **W** Insurance, Pensions and Investments. **Gap** refers to the [priorities](assessment.md#prioritised-gap-register). The [inventory](inventory.md) resolves all source keys, evidence states, dates, and exact locations. Where a field remains unverified or unconnected, no implied fact or system edge is authorised. All 32 domain rows are applicable. Absence of a fact in this repository does not independently establish that it is unavailable publicly.
