This is an inventory and plan, not source admission, legal clearance, an Evidence approval queue or a new rights registry. The precise retained paths and immutable draft heads are in [Audit.json](Audit.json) and [Inventory.json](Inventory.json). Original publisher sources/locators remain in their existing owners; no issuer PDF, table or long source extract is copied here and no deferred PDF link is activated.

## Four questions for each item

1. **Does research exist?** A narrative or extracted candidate can exist on main or in a draft branch without integration.
2. **What is its quality?** A checksum proves that retained bytes did not change, not that a claim is true. A prior original-source inspection is attributed to that pack; it was not repeated here. Period, entity, metric basis, date and counter-evidence must travel with reuse.
3. **Which operation is permitted?** Ordinary lawful factual analysis uses the accepted Source-owned reusable category. A `public_eligible:false` research flag or lack of canonical acceptance is not a conclusive legal prohibition. Conversely, this audit cannot override a current scoped hold, infer a licence, activate protected copying or disclose a private Financial run.
4. **Is it connected and presented?** A named supplier or a navigation link is not a verified process dependency. A renderer's finite claim list is not a general Enterprise Model query.
