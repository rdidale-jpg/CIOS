Value within existing capabilities

Complaint handling is a substantial service activity

FLORA Insight Lloyds Bank PLC’s 118,762 received complaints in January–June 2026 identify a substantial service-handling volume, without establishing deterioration or avoidable cost.

No matched historical or customer denominator is used. Closed cases can include earlier complaints; FOS April–September cases are a different window and cohort.

AI benefit ambitions sit on existing capabilities

FLORA Insight Lloyds reported about £50m of AI value for 2025 and expected more than £100m for 2026; its announced Envoy platform and Google Cloud collaboration place benefit realisation within an existing capability base.

Expected 2026 value is not achieved net benefit, supplier revenue or a budget. Company-described controls do not establish effectiveness; internal teams and incumbents may already cover delivery.

Existing management response

Envoy is an announced platform with described controls. Google Cloud is a disclosed collaborator; an announced collaboration does not establish contract value or available external scope.
