# Artwork continuity and adaptation decision

[Artwork-Provenance.json](Artwork-Provenance.json) records all prompts, generated PNG master hashes, delivery derivative hashes, original Git paths/hashes and chosen comparison crop positions. Original creation authority remains design commit `c6bf2280594922db6951c5c36205984c85ca4aea`; all seven original blobs are independently checked from Git. Current application 21 WebP variants are unchanged.

The `original-*.webp` files in the review bundle are exact **1152px accepted delivery references**, not the historical original master bytes. The comparison captions state this distinction. Original masters are retained at their pinned historical paths/commit, not replaced or falsely re-labelled.

## A versus B

A uses a deliberately chosen subject-focused CSS crop of the unchanged reference. B is a newly generated portrait composition, reference-guided from the corresponding original. No original pixels are edited for A; no post-generation composition edits are made to B. PNG masters remain available; WebP delivery uses proportional encoding only. A 3:2 scene filling a 2:3 frame retains about 44% of its original width, even with a well-chosen focal point.

| Concept | A: principal retained focus / limitation | B: purpose composition / tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK Landscape | Central economy corridor; many lateral sectors disappear | Rural, industrial, public/service, financial and household environments unfold vertically. More breadth survives, but imagery is a concept, not twenty quantified districts. |
| UK Forces | Winding valley/river and national atmosphere | Broad highlands/sea and sky currents remain distinct from banking channels. Metaphor does not encode measured pressure strength. |
| Banking Landscape | Crop shifted toward the classical central-bank environment; loses much contemporary district | Classical civic/regulatory context, varied bank environments and customer activity fit vertically. No building is a verified real site or bilateral relationship. |
| Banking Forces | Braided central channel; loses outer terraces | Multiple structured channels and terraced banks retain system character. Channels are not contracts or transaction volumes. |
| Lloyds Inside | Atrium/stair focus; much operations space disappears | Tall conservatory, connected spaces and discreet technology environment retain architectural richness. Not an organisation chart, floor plan or system topology. |
| Lloyds Landscape | Central enterprise; surrounding actors partly disappear | Central enterprise and surrounding customer/technology/civic environments are distinguishable. Only text grounded in existing sources makes relationship claims. |
| Lloyds Forces | Intimate central lake/path environment | Focused valley with converging paths stays more intimate than national forces and less braided than Banking. No actual customer need is encoded. |

**Recommend B for owner review**, with A retained as the lower-cost/no-regeneration alternative. New portraits retain warmth, atmospheric depth and architectural craft; they are not yet owner accepted. Three prominent controls leave more art visible but offer less immediate discovery breadth than the original panorama. Full interaction preservation remains a later implementation gate.

## UK skyline

[New skyline master](artwork-masters/uk-skyline.png) combines recognisable heritage and modern UK motifs, principally London with distant wider-UK cues. It remains identical at UK, Banking and Lloyds scopes. Its deliberate left-side light space supports FLORA/navigation; a faint treatment protects typography. It is a conceptual country identity, not a map, city-position claim or assertion that UK industry equals London. A future country needs separately approved artwork and existing country-scoped intelligence, not a recoloured UK claim.

## Main treatments

Nine original decorative SVG line treatments: UK landscape/civic plan, Banking landscape/network, Lloyds landscape/enterprise context, Inside/architectural arches, three Forces/current variants, News/editorial columns, Tools/instrument geometry. Base opacity 4.5%, compact 3.5%, masked toward the text area; no real network or quantitative values are encoded. Disabled in forced colours. These are backgrounds, never substitutes for the rich Left artwork.

Master generation is stochastic; prompts and exact returned files are retained, not a promise of byte-identical re-generation. Static delivery, original hashes and prototype exports are reproducibly checked.
