## Baseline and exclusions

FY2025 and Q3 2026 have different reporting periods. The latter includes nine-month profit and September closing deposits. FY2025 application-retirement and Copilot licence disclosures are reported measures with their own scope. Licences do not measure usage or value realised. Strategy disclosures identify platform renewal, a sub-45% cost/income ambition and projected £2bn gross savings. Keep dates, definitions, scope and forward-looking status with each figure. Gross savings do not establish net programme benefit or the contribution of a specific supplier.

Existing records cover reported AI capability and a Google collaboration, PRA policy and the March 2027 date, Curve's earlier conditional status and later reported completion, and a brand milestone announced for 2027. Keep earlier snapshots and allow the newer relationship state only from its effective evidence. Acquisition completion does not establish adoption, integration or financial benefit. FCA H1 2026 returns provide matched activity volumes for Lloyds Bank plc, NatWest and HSBC UK. Denominators, aligned servicing perimeter and outcome measures are still needed for service ranking. The 2024 Treasury pilot design can describe an announced mechanism at that time. It provides insufficient evidence for a live rollout, Lloyds participation or an AI-native competitor.

The retained working projection carries its own authority and provenance. Preserve the Lloyds §2 inactive path for newly processed restricted material, and defer §5 direct PDF links. Existing public claims, private Financial runs, draft outputs and canonically accepted records keep their own access and lifecycle. Existing planning themes, vendor announcements, media leads and forecasts can focus investigation. Projected targets, unverified allocations, inferred supplier edges, unmatched benchmarks and proposed opportunities are unsuitable as verified evidence.
