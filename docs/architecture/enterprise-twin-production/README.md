## Architecture and commercial critique

A decision-relative contract makes research actionable. It forces disclosure of which operating dependency, accounting perimeter or unresolved contradiction could change an executive decision. Stable references allow Facts, analytical views and evidence inspection to share the same intelligence.

The principal architecture risk is letting the family matrix become a parallel database. Treat it as a checklist over current owners, with links and versioned references into the existing knowledge and portfolio. A second risk is equating publicly readable reports with authorised automated collection or with approved private runs. Match the exact operation and do not make legal exceptions into a blanket research gate.

The Lloyds material can frame a strong strategy discussion, but a plausible diagram offers weak evidence for allocating investment or pursuing a sale. Independent corroboration and alternatives matter most where outcomes, suppliers and buyer intent are inferred. Peer complaint volumes can make service scale tangible, while using them to rank banks would mislead. Valid exhaustion can justify a narrow release and an explicit Unknown. It does not establish an unsupported outcome.
