# Enterprise factual information schedule

**Proposed.** An information obligation is a question against existing owners. Field names below are required meanings in existing objects or review material. They do not specify new storage objects.

## Common envelope

For every applicable family retain existing stable Enterprise/Industry, legal-entity, segment, product, geography and relationship IDs as applicable. Record the assertion and assertion type, predicate and endpoint IDs, scope and inclusion/exclusion, admissible Source ID/version and exact locator, source role, acquisition/reuse scope, verification, publication and observation dates, effective start/end and reporting date/duration, uncertainty, applicability, as-of date, and predecessor/successor relationship. Numerical facts retain original value, unit, scale, currency, accounting or regulatory definition, denominator, precision and reported/restated/derived basis. Derived views also retain input revisions, method, assumptions and exclusions.

**M** means a material Tier 1 question must be answered or explicitly recorded as Unknown with impact. **C** applies where a material exposure, activity or decision triggers it. Record the test and reason when it does not apply. A lack of evidence is Unknown, not inapplicability. For each family capture the current material state, comparable predecessors spanning significant changes, and dated applicable commitments or scenario ranges. Historical depth is determined by the decision, cycle and availability. A prior restatement or acquisition boundary remains distinct. Where there is no forward claim, record the horizon as inapplicable to that assertion.

Prefer original filings, regulatory records, attributable owner disclosures and directly scoped sources. For material inference or disputed topics seek independent evidence, and record when two sources repeat the same original. One source can establish what it says within its scope. Where fields cannot resolve using existing objects, record a research obligation or owner decision rather than introducing another identity, report store or canonical assertion.

Each row names the trigger for review. On trigger, recheck coverage, observed date, comparability and applicability. Keep prior valid history, exact restatement links and unresolved competing claims. Withdraw or invalidate dependent projections as soon as a basis becomes unusable. For every Unknown record object/scope, missing field, attempted sources, consequence, review owner and trigger. For every Contradiction retain competing assertions, periods, evidence, cause if known, decision impact and resolution treatment. Exclude an unresolved material contradiction from an unqualified factual or implicit causal conclusion.

Model names refer to the [catalogue](analytical-models.md). Visual destinations refer to the [experience schedule](presentation-and-quality.md).
