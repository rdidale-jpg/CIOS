# Enterprise Intelligence Standard v2

**Version 2.0 · Proposed for owner acceptance.** This is the product and research coverage standard within the Enterprise Twin Production Contract. The [31 factual families](factual-contract.md), [20 analytical models](analytical-models.md), [industry schedules](industry-schedules.md), [authority boundaries](authority-and-factual-use.md) and [Experience Contract](../../product/flora-experience/FLORA-Experience-Contract-v1.0.md) retain their roles. The domains below describe what a user must be able to understand, often by combining several existing families and models. An audit result creates no new factual family, schema, release authority or permission.

Flora helps strategic sales professionals understand the enterprise and industry, understand how the business operates, anticipate change, investigate credible demand, then build competitive preference and convert qualified opportunities into contracts. Start with the business outcome and its economics. Technology explains part of the operating mechanism or a response. Preserve Home · Industries · Enterprises · Opportunities · Pipeline. Landscape, Inside and Forces are three readings of the same enterprise intelligence. Use clear commercial language, original visual compositions, accessible navigation and progressive disclosure of evidence and uncertainty.

## Domain requirements

Each row gives the minimum answer, the relationship that makes it useful and the evidence needed. Apply at Group and decision-relevant scopes. Industry extensions refine evidence and denominators without replacing these requirements.

| ID and domain | Minimum substantive coverage | Required connections | Evidence and example extension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L01 Enterprise identity | History, ownership, geographic footprint, scale and market presence | Legal enterprise → subsidiaries, markets and aliases with effective dates | Official registry and dated report, with jurisdiction. Government uses statutory remit, sponsor and territorial jurisdiction. |
| L02 Purpose, vision and mission | Published purpose, values, commitments and ambitions with explicit gaps | Statement → issuer, period and compatible objective | Dated governing or corporate publication. An unstated separate vision or mission remains unstated. |
| L03 Business model | Matched income, costs, profit, drivers and material value flows | Value flow → product, customer, reporting segment and measures | Reconciled accounts and commercial mechanism. Use deposits/margin for banking, yield/load factor for aviation, recurring revenue for technology services and funding/service outcomes for government. |
| L04 Business portfolio | Material divisions, brands, products, services and customers | Proposition → served market, owning legal entity and reporting segment where evidenced | Segment reports plus product/legal disclosure. Show code-share versus airline operator and public delivery agency versus policy owner separately. |
| L05 Financial performance | Historical results, drivers, restatements and key trends | Measure → statement, period, entity, revision and comparative basis | Official matched reporting and original calculation. Add capital metrics, aircraft leases, service margin or budget/outturn as appropriate. |
| L06 Business strategy | Strategic pillars, choices, objectives and investment allocations | Objective → priority, investment, business and executive when published | Original dated strategy and later updates, with scope and status. |
| L07 Strategy execution | Baselines, commitments, milestones, matched actuals, variance and response | Objective version → baseline → dated target → comparable actual → management response | Original and subsequent disclosures with definition and perimeter checked. Preserve every target vintage. |
| L08 Business priorities | Current and emerging Group and divisional priorities, trade-offs and materiality | Priority → business outcome, affected segment and accountable role | Current results, strategy and relevant corroborating measures. Distinguish management intent and actual investment. |
| L09 Growth and investment | Acquisitions, disposals, markets, propositions, investment and delivery status | Initiative → counterparty, effective date, business and economics | Official filings, transaction notices and comparable measures. Planned, agreed and completed are separate milestones. |
| L10 Future ambition | Financial, commercial and non-financial targets with dates and assumptions | Target vintage → business, unit, baseline and dependencies | Dated management publication. Separate expectation, ambition, target, guidance and actual. |
| I01 Leadership and governance | Board/executive roles, responsibilities, decision remit and effective dates | Person → role → legal or management scope → governed objective | Official board, appointment or governance record. Announcement and start dates differ. |
| I02 Organisation structure | Management units, shared functions, delegated responsibility and reporting lines | Unit → parent/role, brand and reporting segment through independently evidenced edges | Dated annual or official organisation records. A segment or brand alone supplies no reporting line. |
| I03 Workforce | Numbers, role mix, geography, skills, costs, restructuring and trends | Workforce measure → unit, location, role, initiative and period | Consistent FTE versus headcount and agency scope. Add crew qualification, services skills or public staff grades as applicable. |
| I04 Business capabilities | Enterprise-specific capabilities with business purpose and performance | Capability → proposition, process and accountable unit | Official remit plus operating proof. Reference capability maps remain conceptual. |
| I05 End-to-end business processes | Actual steps, ownership, volumes, speed, cost, controls and outcomes | Process step → handoff, unit, system and performance measure | Observed enterprise-specific stage or approved process evidence. Separate estimated design flow from observed execution. |
| I06 Applications and business systems | Named applications, purpose, adoption, lifecycle and material coverage | Application → process, owner, supplier or dependency with scoped evidence | Official statements, public contracts or admissible evidence. A pilot does not establish Group rollout. |
| I07 Infrastructure | Cloud, compute, hosting, network, workplace and resilience footprint | Infrastructure → workloads, location, lifecycle and control boundary | Dated operational evidence with scope. Add airside services or public critical infrastructure where relevant. |
| I08 Data and information assets | Data domains, uses, stewardship, flows, controls and quality limits | Data asset → process, application, use and governance role | Named deployment/control and evidence of scope. Respect protected lineage and customer data. |
| I09 Estates and physical assets | Facilities, locations, footprint history, utilisation, cost and estate strategy | Site or asset → service, business unit, date and economics | Official estate or operational disclosures. Aviation adds fleet and bases; technology adds capacity; government adds service network. |
| I10 Suppliers and dependencies | Named counterparties, service, terms where disclosed, lifecycle and extent of reliance | Supplier ↔ buyer entity → evidenced agreement, workload or dependency | Signed award/disclosure or corroboration. Procurement listings and partnerships are separate from contracts. |
| I11 Operating performance | Costs, quality, speed, productivity and resilience by scope and horizon | Measure → process, owner, comparator, baseline and controls | Actual outcome with numerator, denominator and period. Add completion/service rates, utilisation, SLA or citizen outcome as relevant. |
| I12 Internal priorities and change | Process, workforce, estate, service and technology changes, investment, status and outcome | Initiative → objective, accountable unit, resource, dependency and milestone | Planned and delivered events, funded decisions and results carry separate dates. |
| F01 Macro environment | Material macro drivers and enterprise-specific sensitivity or limits | External driver → business exposure → possible outcome | Dated external data plus issuer exposure. Avoid unsupported sensitivity or forecasts. |
| F02 Industry dynamics | Sector structure, economics, value chains and material shifts | Industry mechanism → market → enterprise participation and dependence | Sector/regulator evidence and enterprise mapping. Use appropriate market boundaries. |
| F03 Competition | Direct peers, challengers and substitutes across material markets | Competitor → overlapping market, proposition, customer and geography | Evidenced market participation with relevant dates. Include non-traditional substitutes and in-house provision where relevant. |
| F04 Competitive position | Comparable share, growth, pricing, propositions, economics and performance | Peer measure → mapped comparable market, population, scope and period | Matched figures and denominators, or explicit comparison limit. Government may compare alternative provision or peer authorities. |
| F05 SWOT | Evidence-backed strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats | Observation → business, evidence and counter-evidence, materiality, trend, horizon, confidence and response | Factual input plus transparent analytical inference. A SWOT opportunity is not qualified demand. |
| F06 Critical business challenges | Outcome-led priorities across economics, customers, conduct, resilience and execution | Challenge → affected business outcome, measure, trajectory, timing, response and unknowns | Rank via evidenced observations with uncertainty. Supplier fit and technical relevance are independent. |
| F07 Risk and regulation | Material risk/exposure, regulatory obligations, timing and mitigants | Rule/risk → liable entity, exposed activity, deadline, evidence and response | Official regulatory publication and separate evidence of enterprise applicability or impact. |
| F08 Executive agendas | Role-specific decisions, responses, constraints and accountability | Role → priority → action → outcome/next decision | Disclosed responsibility and response, with analytical question labelled as such. |
| F09 Scenarios and triggers | Plausible alternative outcomes, observable triggers, timeframe, exposure and implications | Scenario → trigger/threshold → affected business, measure and response | Explicit assumptions, uncertainty and counter-evidence. No estimated impact without appropriate inputs. |
| F10 Commercial implications | Fact-based signal, research question, counter-case and qualification route | Evidence → challenge → response → signal → investigation → authorised qualification | Keep inferred relevance, confirmed buyer intent, procurement status and Pipeline authority separate. |

## Maturity and completeness

| Level | Objective gate, cumulative |
| --- | --- |
| 0 Missing | No substantive applicable evidence or retained research found for the requirement. Record search scope. |
| 1 Identified | A specific question, applicable scope and source candidate are recorded. Source discovery alone stops here. |
| 2 Researched | A substantive retained assertion with provenance, date and scope, or a documented bounded search, is present. Review, authority or support may still be open. |
| 3 Verified | The referenced existing assurance supports the exact assertion, entity, date, perimeter and revision. Material conflict and counter-evidence have explicit dispositions. Record Source eligibility independently. |
| 4 Connected | Verified intelligence is connected via traceable existing identities and typed, dated relationships or governed measure associations. A link or conceptual diagram alone does not qualify. Specify whether canonical or a governed existing read projection. |
| 5 Operational | The verified and connected answer is accessible in a normal Flora journey, serves a stated decision, offers evidence inspection and maintains history, corrections and withdrawal under an existing owner. Prototype and tests alone never establish this. |

For coverage, inventory atomic requirements by domain and relevant entity or reporting scope before scoring. Use a stable ID and explicit denominator. Mark true non-applicability with a reason. Keep inaccessible, unresearched, and researched with a documented disclosure limit in the applicable denominator. Report research, verification, model connection and user availability as independent numerator counts, with evidence and scope for each. A visible generic statement does not satisfy a factual requirement. Partial slices and confidence describe separate things. Never average ordinal levels or use a domain-touch percentage as completeness. Where the denominator is a small, explicitly scoped requirement sample, label it as such. Record date, provenance quality, currentness, counter-evidence and authorised audience separately.

## Financial integrity

Any display that implies a total, contribution or comparison must verify entity and perimeter, metric definition, periods or as-at dates, accounting basis, currency and scale, compatible precision, stock versus flow, source revision and signed direction. Show central items, eliminations and accounting adjustments explicitly. Reconciliation needs declared inputs, exact arithmetic at source precision, rounding rules and visible differences. A stock belongs at an as-at date. Ratios must carry denominators. Keep restatements and comparatives as distinct revisions with their original comparators. If bases differ, show the bridge or separate the panels and explain the boundary directly beside the figures. Never label a calculated residual as a reported category. Dated management targets and expectations remain distinct from actuals and from evidence of attainment. Reuse existing Financial and Source owners, provenance, audience and withdrawal.

## Commercial materiality and presentation

Proposed weights for calibration against independently reviewed business decisions are 35% financial/business impact, 25% urgency, 20% strategic significance, 10% breadth and 10% deteriorating trajectory. Rate each dimension against explicit evidence on an ordinal 0–4 scale, with 0 evidenced immaterial and 4 evidenced severe, and document thresholds per industry before scoring. Unknown inputs prevent a single published score. Confidence, supplier fit and opportunity attractiveness are separate. No score grants qualification or Pipeline authority.

Primary views answer the executive question immediately. Use existing original artwork, readable whole-region navigation and contextual return paths. Keep source scope, date and uncertainty close to the fact and deeper evidence inspectable. Test actual desktop, iPad portrait and landscape, and mobile. Judge live rendering and exported/printed rendering separately.
