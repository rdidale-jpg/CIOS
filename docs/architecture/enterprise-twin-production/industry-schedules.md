# Industry schedules

**Proposed.** Each schedule adds perimeter, metric meaning and evidence obligations to the [common families](factual-contract.md). Existing enterprise/industry IDs, Source handling, Financial semantics, typed relationships and analytical readiness apply to all schedules. Absence of data yields Unknown when the question applies. These examples commission no acquisition and claim no new issuer eligibility.

## UK Banking · Lloyds Banking Group

| Decision | Tier 1 specialisation | Validation and projection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand the group and markets | Distinguish consolidated Group, Lloyds Bank PLC, brands, subsidiaries, retail, commercial banking and insurance/pensions/investments. Map household/business/institutional customers, deposits, mortgages, other lending, payments and savings. Date legal, reporting and brand changes. | Filings and registers for identity, brand disclosures for offers. Do not assign a branch, supplier or expense from a reporting label. Canvas and service lanes by qualified perimeter. |
| Explain funding and profitability | Capture eligible deposits by date, funding mix, average earning assets, margin basis, operating cost, cost-to-income, impairment and capital. Include cash-flow where it matters. Flag statutory/underlying, IFRS/regulatory, stock/flow, hedging and segment allocation differences. | Financial owner reconciles comparable periods and definitions. Preserve original units and exact reported perimeter. Unverified cost attribution remains Unknown. No Group earning estimate from legal-bank service volumes. |
| Assess service and continuity | Map payments, credit and service journeys and important controls. Record complaint denominator, product mix, firm-upheld vs independent resolution, branch availability, channel transition and measured customer outcomes. | Use applicable regulator/issuer evidence. Draw only supported dependency edges. FCA volumes can show operational scale at the same legal-entity/reporting basis. No quality ranking from raw counts. Show Inside and benchmarking lanes. |
| Evaluate platform change | Record named platform/control only when sourced, scope of workloads, disclosed cloud/AI role, supplier-criticality evidence, stage of migration, transition costs, adoption and verified outcomes. | A partnership does not establish a contract or workshare. A control design does not establish effectiveness. Recheck dates, attribution, conditional targets and actual benefit measures. Use Ecosystem, Risk and Mechanisms. |
| Interpret pressure and disruption | Distinguish PRA/FCA proposals, final rules and effective deadlines from observed legal-entity applicability, readiness and mitigation. Follow wallet/payments alternatives and actual entrant launch proof. | Industry pressure never automatically qualifies enterprise exposure. Compare product/market overlap, adoption and policy status separately. Use Forces and timeline lanes. |
| Historical and forward | Align at least a prior comparable published period with each claimed trend, include material breaks across an acquisition/restatement, and retain effective dates and announced milestones. | Treat unannounced outcomes, unknown timelines and scenario horizons explicitly. Track materiality by decision and withdrawal dependency by input. |

The known case and evidence needs are in the [Lloyds reference](lloyds-reference.md). Nationwide and Equinix prove bounded public financial history capability through EEI‑3. They supply no Lloyds admission or cross-company comparator unless basis and scope match.

## Telecoms · BT Group

- Bind Group, Openreach, Consumer and Business reporting perimeters, wholesale vs retail operation, regulator scope and access network assets. Include premises passed versus connected, take-up denominator, speed/product, geographic coverage, fibre build and copper retirement. Keep announced footprint distinct from service actually available.
- Capture wholesale charges/agreements, subscribers, churn basis, ARPU definition, capital intensity and regulated service obligations. Separate gross build from net capex and expansion from maintenance when disclosed. Verify comparator definitions before rankings or directional analysis.
- Assess deployment dependency, operational resilience and competitive overbuild through regulator, filings and independently scoped network evidence. Use an infrastructure and wholesale landscape, build timeline, cost/capex view and competitor lanes. Authentic filing and admitted-use questions remain with Source.

## Technology · Arm Holdings

- Bind the issuer, segment basis, licensing terms where disclosed, royalties by authorised basis, architecture/product generations, partner type and end markets. Distinguish license contracts, design wins, shipments, architecture adoption and royalty recognition.
- Capture reported license versus royalty periods, recognition, concentration and measurement limitations. Track AI-related use at an evidenced product/market scope. Treat alternative architectures and ecosystem competition as distinct from customers acting in multiple roles.
- Compare like licensing models or explicitly show the mismatched basis. Use an IP-to-ecosystem value chain, adoption lanes, earnings drivers and substitution/scenario contexts. Avoid estimates of contract scope, exclusivity, chip volume or adoption from a partnership alone.

## Aviation · easyJet

- Record aircraft operator, ownership/lease basis, commercial fleet scope, network/airport season and route availability. Distinguish passengers, seats, load factor, yield, ancillary income and holidays with periods, units and definitions.
- Connect route/network economics to seat capacity, fleet utilisation, fuel/FX, resilience and disruption, distribution and customer service where evidenced. Fuel or reliability mechanisms expose levers without asserting causation or route profitability.
- Compare airlines only on compatible operating and currency/period bases. Separate company operating scope, regulatory obligations and competitive alternatives by route/season. Use fleet/network states, runway timeline and operational Inside maps. Source terms and financial admission stay separately owned.
