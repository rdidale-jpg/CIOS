## Proposed reusable categories

| Category | Permitted when the actual use falls within the adopted category | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary official or verifiable public material | Lawful access through the approved origin and method. Independent factual restatement and original analysis across domains. Retain minimal facts, locators and limitations. | No protected expression or table reuse, bulk collection, private workspace run, third-party output beyond scope, or unrestricted archive. Readability alone establishes none of those operations. |
| Explicit licensed public data or open material | Apply the actual licence to the exact release, attribution and allowed transformations. | Check database, third-party, trade-mark, personal-data and audience exclusions. Separate unlicensed content packaged with the release. |
| Independently published secondary evidence | Bounded factual leads or corroboration within an existing authorised method. Record original dependency and scope. | An article citing an issuer is not independent verification of the issuer’s statement. No expansion to unapproved origins or protected extraction. |
| Restricted, confidential, personal or technically gated content | Existing lawful and expressly authorised method only. Publish minimal permitted derivatives at allowed audience scope. | No paywall/access workaround, undisclosed private ingestion, retention, republication or authority expansion. Refer material privacy or legal issues to existing owners. |

### Deterministic review before release

1. Validate original publisher or regulator, issuer identity, document version, authorised origin/path, method and actual access conditions. Record publication, retrieval and observed period independently.
2. Match operation, scale, retention, audience and attribution against the adopted category and exact licence/terms. Explicitly distinguish read, extract independent facts, calculate, retain, link, display, export, copy and persist. A generic copyright notice or absence of explicit factual licensing alone does not create another approval step.
3. Check the specific applicable contractual restriction, protected expression, database extraction extent including cumulative use, privacy and access controls. Route a concrete unresolved issue to the Source owner, with existing legal escalation only where material. Bound the held operation and record why. Continue unrelated lawful work. No new rights registry or approval service.
4. Bind each selected fact to subject and perimeter, exact locator, period, accounting basis, unit, uncertainty and current lifecycle. Record original comparatives and supersession. Match calculation inputs by meaning and version. Reject incompatible or incomplete comparisons.
5. Verify permitted minimal retained form, audience, current status and withdrawal dependencies. Withdraw or recompute dependent outputs including opened views and exports as applicable. Reopen the category only when facts about the legal or operational context change. Compatible new issuers, reports and values receive routine verification.
