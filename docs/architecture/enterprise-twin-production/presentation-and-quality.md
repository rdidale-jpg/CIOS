Facts shows attributed, eligible facts and clearly labelled derived measures or management plans. FLORA Insight explains a selected fact, relationship or decision using an existing bounded model output. It retains distinction between evidence, interpretation, expected outcome and hypothetical scenario. A view must preserve selected Enterprise, segment and period, link to the relevant existing destination, and show a material caveat next to the assertion.

Keep audit metadata, permission codes and research workflow controls outside the executive canvas. Make Source, date, applicability, assumptions, conflicting evidence and lineage accessible through existing governed inspection. Preserve attribution and show decision-material uncertainties in context. A map of related topics does not imply a causal edge. On desktop and iPad use legible type, reasonable touch targets, visible focus, predictable selection, accessible chart labels and a usable sparse/withdrawn state. Use differentiated industry visuals over generic decorative dashboards.

## Gate checklist

| Gate | What passes | Evidence to record; hold condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Factual coverage and depth | All material questions for the named decision are answered to declared Tier 1 depth or explicitly dispositioned. | Family matrix by scope and time. Hold the decision if an uncovered material question would change it. |
| Evidence sufficiency and admissibility | Each asserted claim has scoped support, lawful operation and eligible provenance, including independence where consequence requires it. | Citation/version/locator and actual owner disposition. Hold unsupported material use and continue unrelated research. |
| Relationship integrity | Existing stable IDs resolve, relationship predicate and status match evidence, and no records conflate company, brand, programme or supplier. | Edge-by-edge checks and counterexample. Hold a causal or commercial inference where connection is unverified. |
| Historical fidelity | Observed, effective, reported and anticipated dates distinguish original, restated and superseded states. | Period/perimeter/unit alignment, predecessor trace and stale treatment. Reject mismatched trend or terminal date. |
| Model-input sufficiency | Each output satisfies the catalogue minimum and every dependent input is eligible for the current reader. | Reproduce selected inputs and missing reasons. Hold absent or incompatible decisive evidence. |
| Analytical validity and explainability | Calculation and reasoning can be replayed, tested against an alternative and invalidated when required. | Method/version, counterevidence, uncertainty and falsifier. Reject unsupported causal links, probabilities and scores. |
| Commercial decision usefulness | Executive can identify decision, action constraints and a question whose answer could change the choice. | Independent review of implication, avoidable confusion and limitations. Protect opportunity/qualification boundary. |
| Executive visual usability | User can navigate appropriate depth and find material information, uncertainties and eligible inspection. | Desktop and iPad orientation evidence, keyboard/touch, history and accessible labels. Reject severe clutter, inaccessible controls or hidden material uncertainty. |
| Cross-industry reuse | Same core fields/owners work in multiple schedules while preserving domain-specific definitions. | Replay a second issuer/sector using its scope. Hold generalisation claims based only on fixtures or a single issuer. |
| Security, permissions and withdrawal | Identity, ACLs, read-only boundary and existing Source withdrawal invalidate dependent public or private outputs independently. | Exact-head security, denied access, withdrawal, regression and rollback. No private discovery, data retention escalation or deployment by inference. |

**Model verdicts apply to a named scope, decision, reader and date.**

- **Ready** means material input and applicability gates pass, known contradictions have an approved treatment, reasoning is explainable and the output can serve the stated decision and use. Ready for an orientation decision can be narrower than Ready for investment.
- **Partial** means at least one bounded portion passes, but a missing, stale, conflicting or unverified axis limits use. Publish only that portion with the limitation and review trigger. Do not present the full model as ready.
- **Blocked** means a material minimum for that stated output is missing, incompatible, restricted or awaiting an authority decision. Suppress the affected conclusion. Record the blocker, owner and independently authorised work. An invalidated output remains blocked even if its cached view exists.
- **Not Applicable** means the scope or decision provides a supported exclusion. Record the evidence and reconsideration trigger. Lack of evidence is never sufficient to declare it.

Readiness does not replace existing approval. Release owners record exact revision, provisional/accepted status, scope, reviewer, held operations and rollback. No record count, aggregate score or checklist completion substitutes for judgement. Preserve unresolved contradictions and unavoidable evidence exhaustion in the release decision.

### Estate and commercial quality checks

- Factual coverage and relationship integrity require a verified installation and entity, scoped supplier role, verified term versus option, and exact role mandate. Keep Unknowns and conflicted terms visible.
- History and evidence require traceable version, amendment, succession and notice records. Test a withdrawn source and an extension that supersedes an earlier date. Retain prior valid history and suppress affected current conclusions.
- Analytical validity and commercial usefulness require all ADR‑026 gates for Pressure, plus separate timing evidence for any urgency label. A projected date cannot populate an observed procurement event.
- Executive usability and security require optional contract/leadership drill-through and commercial events within existing depth and access. Test that private material, personal details and research controls stay outside ordinary presentation, including after a mode change.
