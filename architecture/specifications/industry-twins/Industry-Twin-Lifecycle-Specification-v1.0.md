## Purpose

This specification owns Industry Twin maintenance, refresh cadence and lifecycle semantics.

## Lifecycle

Industry Twins are maintained through scoped collection, Knowledge Asset review, cadence-based refresh, contradiction review, market participant comparison, opportunity signal review and Cross-Twin Intelligence synthesis. Industry monitoring and automated refresh are out of scope for Phase 1.

## WP1-002 review note — lifecycle fidelity and promotion

Refresh and supersession preserve prior states, effective dates, changed conclusions/recommendations and visible Unknowns/Contradictions. Promotion is distinct from package validity and requires all IT-001 High-Fidelity Completeness Contract gates plus independent decision-relative review. A legacy or syntactically valid release is not promoted by inference. This note retains this specification's declared authority status and introduces no runtime behaviour.
