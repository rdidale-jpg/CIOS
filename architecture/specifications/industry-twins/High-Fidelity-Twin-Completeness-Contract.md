# High-Fidelity Twin Completeness Contract

**Mission:** WP1-002  
**Status:** Review — proposed controlled schedule to IT-001  
**Owner:** IT-001, with delegated canonical owners below  
**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Change boundary:** Architecture and governance only; no runtime or RET content change.

## 1. Governing rule and scope

This contract operationalises IT-001's High-Fidelity Twin Principle for release assessment. A Twin is governed intelligence, **not a summary**. Detail must be inside the release as structured facts and intelligence objects; a URL, source document, dashboard, generated narrative, or Flora's ability to fetch from the web is not a substitute. Lawfully non-redistributable sources are represented by permitted metadata, citation, provenance, extracted facts, compliant paraphrase, observations, reasoning and access constraints.

Completeness is decision-relative but never merely subjective: scope, target decisions, material objects, applicable dimensions, tiers, measures, deficiencies and review decisions must be explicit. Irrelevant detail is not required. Missing or contradictory evidence is represented honestly, never inferred away.

## 2. Nine distinct states

| State | Meaning | It is not |
|---|---|---|
| Twin fidelity | Reality-representative breadth, depth, history, uncertainty and connections | polish or volume |
| Intelligence density | Quality and amount of relevant, evidenced, structured, connected intelligence per governed object | raw count |
| Evidence sufficiency | Evidence is authoritative, diverse, current enough and adequate for the claims | a list of links |
| Decision maturity | Declared decisions can be supported and challenged at their consequence level | general richness |
| Package validity | Envelope, manifest, checksums and required syntax conform | content completeness |
| Flora addressability | Packaged objects and lineage can be located and traversed | default visibility |
| Promotion readiness | All applicable gates and independent review pass | any one score |
| Research completion | Tier and dimension obligations are met or explicitly deficient under valid exhaustion | delivery of a report |
| Evidence exhaustion | A reproducible, bounded search demonstrates why a remaining gap persists | “no further evidence found” |

Each is reported separately as `pass`, `conditional`, `fail`, or `not_applicable` with rationale and deficiencies. No aggregate score is permitted. A rich Twin can be decision-immature; a decision-ready Twin can expose contradictions; a valid package can be shallow.

## 3. Intelligence Density

**Intelligence Density** is the amount and quality of commercially relevant, evidenced, structured and connected intelligence associated with a governed object. Assessment considers breadth and within-dimension depth; distinct factual assertions; evidence and observation coverage; meaningful typed relationships; historical span; independent source organisations and families; authority, freshness and primary coverage; corroboration; visible contradictions and Unknowns; inspectable reasoning; commercial relevance; and reuse beyond the immediate question.

Counts are diagnostic, not proof. Assertions with the same semantic claim/source are deduplicated; copied, circular or syndication-dependent sources count as one evidence cluster; unsupported prose counts zero; weak sources cannot satisfy primary-source obligations; relationships count only when typed, resolvable and meaningful; irrelevant facts do not count; materially false or stale records reduce rather than increase assurance. Sampling must test claim diversity and evidence linkage. **Two thousand weak or duplicated facts do not establish completeness; one high-level narrative does not establish fidelity; links without extracted facts do not establish density; generated prose without evidence does not establish depth; excessive irrelevant content does not establish quality.**

## 4. Object tiers

* **Tier 1 — high-fidelity:** material to an executive, commercial, procurement, market, supplier or investment decision. Requires substantial applicable multi-dimensional intelligence, evidence, history, relationships, observations and uncertainty.
* **Tier 2 — commercially useful:** enough detail for comparison, context and selected reasoning, with declared limitations; not full decision-grade analysis.
* **Tier 3 — reference:** governed identity and relationship anchor, limited detail and explicit incompleteness; cannot support a material conclusion.

Tier rationale records materiality, target-decision relevance, market and opportunity significance, ecosystem/supplier importance, evidence availability, research cost and expected commercial value. Cost or unavailable evidence may explain a deficiency or exhaustion record, but cannot justify demoting a materially decision-relevant object. Tier changes are versioned and reviewed.

## 5. Content requirements

### 5.1 Industry and subsector

Applicable coverage includes definition/boundaries, subsectors, value chains and operating models; economic structure, revenue/margin pools where evidenced, demand/cost drivers; size, growth, maturity, concentration/consolidation/disruption; geography; regulation/policy; technology and customer/supply shifts; entry barriers/dependencies; risks/scenarios; historical development; material news; attributable analyst views; Unknowns, Contradictions and evidence lineage. Summary statements must trace to this detail.

### 5.2 Enterprise dossiers

A Tier 1 Enterprise covers, where applicable:

1. **Corporate:** legal/trading identity, ownership, group/business-unit structure, geographies/sites/listing, acquisitions, divestments, JVs, partnerships and history.
2. **Leadership/governance:** board, executive and relevant unit leaders, changes, responsibilities/tenure, evidenced decision rights, priorities/statements and evidenced succession risk.
3. **Strategy:** priorities, plans, targets, transformations, capital allocation, growth/efficiency/risk programmes, entry/exit and portfolio change, including change over time and interpretive evidence.
4. **Financial intelligence:** multi-period revenue, profit, cash flow, relevant balance sheet, capex, R&D/investment, debt/returns, segment/geographic performance, operating metrics, guidance/commentary/expectations, variance and risk, with primary references and commercial interpretation. Annual reports are one source, not the model.
5. **Products/services/offers:** catalogues/families/features, proposition, delivery/deployment/licensing/contract/support/distribution, target segments/industries, pricing signals, lifecycle/roadmap, differentiation/weakness, adoption and customers.
6. **Technology/digital estate:** platforms/systems/cloud/data/AI/automation/channels, architecture/integration/API signals, partners/dependencies, modernisation/cyber/technical-debt signals, investment and news.
7. **Operating model:** functions/value creation, delivery/service/distribution/customer operations, supply chain/shared services, sourcing/workforce, change, dependencies, productivity, constraints and performance issues.
8. **Customers/markets:** segments, markets/routes/channels, material named customers when lawful, retention/churn and experience signals, demand, position/brand and concentration.
9. **Suppliers/ecosystem:** strategic technology/service/delivery partners, outsourcers/consultancies/integrators, contracts/alliances/changes, concentration and dependency risk.
10. **Procurement/buying:** centres, owners/influencers, routes/frameworks/cycles/renewals/incumbents, budget/approval signals, sourcing/access constraints and timing, including Unknowns.
11. **Transformation:** active/completed/planned programmes, owners/outcomes/budgets, partners/dependencies, status/milestones/delays/benefits/risks/contradictions/news and commercial implications.
12. **Opportunities:** buyer/problem/pressure/value/scope/urgency/current response/incumbent/competition/procurement/access/fit/implementation/value, evidence, reasoning, risks and uncertainty.
13. **Events and analyst intelligence:** governed histories meeting §§5.5–5.6.
14. **Evidence/uncertainty:** evidence metadata, observations/implications, gaps, Unknowns, Contradictions, affected decisions/recommendations.

Tier 2 covers the decision-relevant subset across several dimensions with evidence and current state; Tier 3 has identity, classification, provenance, key relationships and explicit missing depth. EIF-001/EI-001 retain Enterprise semantics.

### 5.3 Market Participants and suppliers

A participant is never complete as a name/node/category. Tier 1 suppliers, vendors, services firms, integrators, outsourcers, consultancies, distributors, regulators, trade bodies, channels, infrastructure/platform and specialist providers cover applicable: corporate identity/ownership/leadership/structure/geography/strategy/finance/change; capability catalogue, scale, certifications, IP/assets/constraints; detailed products/services/managed services/platforms/modules; commercial and competitive positioning; technology/cloud/data/AI/security/interoperability; delivery/partner ecosystem and capacity; customers and sector footprint; launches, acquisitions, leadership, results, partnerships, wins/losses and regulatory/analyst history; and reasoned relevance, strengths, risks, Unknowns and Contradictions. This must enable supported comparisons of transformation fit, sector proof, buyer alignment, ecosystem, analyst challenge, recent suitability changes, gaps and incumbent access.

### 5.4 Capabilities and offers

Capabilities and offers are first-class objects where existing architecture permits, never implied by provider identity. Each records stable identity, description/category/provider; industry, functional and technical scope; deployment, implementation, delivery and integration; target customer/buyer; evidence and reference customers; maturity/constraints/dependencies; lawful pricing/licensing signals; roadmap, analyst/news links and competitors; supported opportunities, risks, Unknowns and Contradictions. Product and service records link rather than duplicate these objects.

### 5.5 News as temporal intelligence

A governed News Event records event/publication dates, source/authority, affected objects, classification, factual statement, materiality, strategic/financial/operational/capability/opportunity implications, linked observations/contradictions, confidence and expiry/reassessment. Tier 1 objects require material-event coverage across the declared research period. Links or an unstructured feed fail. Full text is not required or permitted beyond licence; package governed facts, lawful excerpts/paraphrase and lineage.

### 5.6 Analyst intelligence

An Analyst Observation is a distinct attributable evidence class: research organisation and analyst when known, publication/date, market/category and affected enterprise/vendor/capability, comparative position, strengths/cautions, forecast/adoption view, implications, evidence relationships, confidence, contradictions, licence/access constraints. “Analysts believe” is unsupported. Where content cannot be packaged, retain only permitted metadata, governed paraphrase, citation, interpretation and constraints. Absence may be an explicit Unknown/exhaustion outcome where no lawful applicable material exists.

### 5.7 Temporal fidelity

Applicable Tier 1 objects retain current and prior states, effective/observed/published/retrieved dates, change events and supersession lineage. Coverage includes leadership/strategy, financial trends, launches/retirements, M&A, partnerships/contracts, transformations, regulation, analyst-view changes, news, freshness, superseded conclusions and changed recommendations. Every assertion is typed as `current_fact`, `historical_fact`, `forecast`, `management_target`, `analyst_expectation`, `inference`, or `superseded_observation`; forecasts and inferences cannot masquerade as facts.

### 5.8 Relationship fidelity

EI-002 owns identifiers and graph integrity. Applicable typed, directed and temporally qualified relationships include Industry→Subsector/Enterprise; Enterprise→Subsidiary/Executive/Product/Capability/Supplier/Partner/Customer Segment/Buying Centre/Transformation/Contract/Opportunity/Evidence; Participant→Capability/Offer/Partner/Reference Customer; Capability→Opportunity; News/Analyst Observation→Affected Object; Evidence→Observation; Observation→Signal/Insight/Theme; Reasoning Object→Hypothesis→Commercial Thesis→Recommendation; and Unknown/Contradiction→affected object/decision.

All endpoints resolve, identifiers remain stable, duplicate identities are reconciled or flagged, direction/type/provenance/effective dates are valid, and material expected relationship classes are covered or deficient. No relationship is fabricated to improve coverage.

## 6. Evidence, reasoning and uncertainty

Material factual assertions link to evidence with source identity/type/authority, publication/source/retrieval/effective dates, provenance, access/licence, freshness and confidence. Decision-material claims require primary evidence where reasonably available and independent corroboration proportional to consequence; conflicting evidence remains linked. Evidence lineage follows ADR-010/EI-012 boundaries.

EI-004 reasoning is inspectable from Evidence → Observation → applicable Signal/Insight/Theme → Hypothesis → Commercial Thesis → Recommendation, retaining existing vocabulary ambiguity rather than silently redefining it. Each transformation states inputs, rule/rationale, author/agent, time, confidence, assumptions, counterevidence and affected decision. Recommendations without supporting factual detail and traversable lineage block promotion. Unknowns and Contradictions are first-class, linked, materiality-rated and never hidden or “resolved” by prose.

Evidence exhaustion does not erase a deficiency: it may make a non-critical gap conditional/non-blocking, but never cures evidence required for a material recommendation. Every dimension reports purpose, objects, evidence, depth, time, diversity, deficiencies/warnings, Unknown/Contradiction treatment, exhaustion, promotion impact and `architectural` or `RET_calibration` status.

## 8. Package and release

The `.zip` is self-describing and contains the logical inventories in the Package Content Inventory Contract: release/version/scope/decisions/research period/effective date; object IDs/types/tiers and rationale; facts, sources/evidence, lawful document references, news, analyst observations, capabilities/offers, relationships/reasoning/history, Unknowns/Contradictions/deficiencies/exhaustion; freshness/supersession/validation/completeness/promotion state; provenance/checksums/compatibility. Physical paths remain flexible. Structured facts and intelligence must be packaged, not only summaries or documents.

## 9. Flora projection and addressability

Flora is a projection/exploration environment and must not reconstruct a missing Twin from the web. Packaged traversal must address Industry → Subsector → Enterprise → corporate structure → leadership → strategy → financials → products/services/capabilities → technology → customers/suppliers/partners → buying centres/procurement → transformation → news/analyst intelligence → opportunity → recommendation → reasoning → evidence → Unknowns/Contradictions → historical state. Progressive disclosure is permitted. `not_displayed_by_default` is acceptable; `not_addressable` and `absent` are distinct deficiencies and unacceptable for required complete content.

## 10. Research completion and evidence exhaustion

The Researcher's mission is not report production; it is constructing and improving governed Twin objects. Work must increase fidelity, density, evidence/history/relationships, usefulness/explainability, contradiction visibility, confidence or decision maturity. An explicit decision-relative source-family plan considers annual reports/accounts/filings/results/investor presentations/calls; official product/service/technical sites; procurement portals/contracts; government/regulators/trade bodies; releases/reputable news; lawfully usable analyst/industry research; partner/case-study/customer evidence; interviews/public strategies/policy/standards; and trusted databases.

Research stops only when applicable tier/dimension obligations are met **or** each remaining gap has a governed exhaustion record containing: object; dimension; question; source families and individual sources inspected; search and covered time periods; terms/strategy; evidence found/not found; access/licence restrictions; likely absence cause; remaining deficiency/materiality; conclusion and recommendation effect (including whether it could change one); human escalation; and next review date. “No further evidence found” is invalid. Checkpoints reassess tiering, source plan, deficiencies, contradictions and marginal research value; exhaustion remains reviewable and expires.

## 11. Promotion gates

Promotion requires all of the following, independently recorded:

1. applicable Tier 1 objects meet fidelity requirements, or a non-waivable block fails promotion;
2. material claims are evidenced and dimensions populated or explicitly deficient;
3. scope-appropriate news and analyst coverage is sufficient or validly exhausted without decision-critical impact;
4. material participant capability/offer detail exists;
5. reasoning lineage and recommendations drill down to packaged facts/evidence;
6. identity/graph integrity and temporal coherence pass;
7. Unknowns and Contradictions are visible with decision effects;
8. exhaustion records are complete and independently challenged;
9. Flora can address the packaged detail without external reconstruction;
10. package/release integrity passes; and
11. an independent reviewer confirms support for each declared decision.

Waivers name owner, reason, expiry and affected decisions, but cannot waive unsupported material recommendations, fabricated/hidden uncertainty, broken lineage, graph corruption or absent Tier 1 substance. `package-valid-but-content-shallow` is always promotion-blocking.

## 12. Authority, compatibility and open issues

Canonical delegation remains: IT-001 composition/release; EI-001/EIF-001 Enterprise depth; EI-002 graph/identity; EI-004 reasoning; EI-006/OT-001 opportunity; EI-012 uncertainty; ADR-009 progressive decision assurance; ADR-010 acquisition; ADR-016/Knowledge Pack exchange; lifecycle refresh/supersession; Production Protocol/Researcher completion; FEIR-001/presentation addressability. This schedule does not promote Review/Draft owners.

Unresolved authority issues from WP1-001 remain explicit: IT-001 is Review; lifecycle, pack and presentation specifications are Draft Normative; Market Participant ownership/status and the canonical Evidence object owner require reconciliation; financial ownership spans documents of unequal authority; EI-004 lineage vocabulary includes Signal/Insight/Theme variants; OT-001 is Review; `industry-twin-v1` is not one unambiguous protected machine contract. The smallest reconciliation here is cross-reference and delegation, not authority clean-up.

Compatibility is additive: v1 assets remain importable and keep identifiers/lineage, but are assessed as `legacy_unassessed` until evaluated. New completeness metadata may be ignored by old consumers. No retroactive completeness, destructive migration, runtime behaviour or RET mutation is authorised.
