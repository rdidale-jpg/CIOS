Mission ID: WP1-003
Title: RET High-Fidelity Completeness Assessment and Evidence Gap Plan
Mission type: Assessment and planning only

Objective
Assess the actual governed intelligence content of RET against WP1-002's High-Fidelity Twin Completeness Contract. Do not assess only package structure, importability, navigation, file presence or record count. Do not alter RET, runtime, schemas or canonical architecture.

Governing inputs
- architecture/specifications/industry-twins/IT-001-Industry-Twin-Specification.md §§9–12
- architecture/specifications/industry-twins/High-Fidelity-Twin-Completeness-Contract.md

- WP1-001 baseline and its preserved authority ambiguities

Method
1. Freeze/checksum and identify RET scope, version, target decisions, research period and effective date.
2. Inventory all governed objects and propose Tier 1/2/3 using materiality, never current depth. Identify missing material objects.
3. Inspect packaged structured facts, evidence, observations, history, news, analyst intelligence, capabilities/offers, relationships, reasoning, Unknowns, Contradictions, deficiencies and exhaustion records.
4. Assess all 22 dimensions independently as pass/conditional/fail/NA with evidence. Never create an aggregate score.
5. Deduplicate semantic facts and syndicated/circular sources. Quality-sample claims and relationships so volume cannot game results.
6. Trace every material insight and recommendation to packaged facts and evidence. Test Flora/package addressability offline; distinguish not displayed, not addressable and absent.
7. Identify missing financial periods, weak news periods, absent lawful analyst perspectives, unsearched source families, missing historical changes, shallow objects and relationship gaps.
8. Apply the machine-readable deficiency taxonomy and state commercial impact, affected decisions, blocking status and remediation.
9. Audit every evidence-exhaustion assertion. “No further evidence found” is invalid.
10. Test the proposed RET calibration values for demand, usefulness, achievability, discrimination, reviewer repeatability and resistance to record-count gaming. Recommend changes per measure, not a composite.
11. Obtain an independent review of tiering, material-claim evidence samples, lineage, exhaustion and Reference Twin blockers.

Required outputs
- executive conclusion on whether RET genuinely contains Twin-level detail;
- complete object/tier inventory and rationale;
- 22-dimension assessment;
- per-object Intelligence Density Matrix results and semantic quality samples;
- source-family, primary-source, freshness and temporal coverage maps;
- Enterprise, Market Participant, supplier, capability and offer gap analysis;
- financial-period, news-period and analyst-perspective gaps;
- reasoning/recommendation drill-down and graph-integrity results;
- Unknown/Contradiction and evidence-exhaustion audit;
- Flora offline-addressability findings;
- machine-readable deficiency register with commercial materiality and Reference Twin blockers;
- calibration results and proposed adjustments;
- prioritised evidence-gap plan with object, question, source families, owner, effort, value, dependency and next review date;
- explicit confirmation that RET/runtime/architecture were not changed.

Constraints
A valid package may fail. Links, documents, summaries, generated prose and entity counts are not sufficient intelligence. Do not infer absent facts, hide uncertainty, unlawfully package analyst/full-text content, demote material objects to excuse shallowness, or treat exhaustion as remediation. Preserve WP1-002 authority/status ambiguities.
