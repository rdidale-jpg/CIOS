# RET High-Fidelity Assessment Checklist

**Mission source:** WP1-002  
**Status:** Assessment instrument and **calibration proposal**, not accepted doctrine  
**Boundary:** Use in WP1-003 to inspect RET content. Do not infer missing facts, edit RET, or substitute package validation.

## 1. Assessment method

1. Freeze and checksum the RET release; record version, declared scope/decisions/research period and compatibility.
2. Inventory every governed object and classify proposed T1/T2/T3 with materiality rationale. Identify material objects absent from inventory separately.
3. Map packaged facts, evidence, sources, events, analyst observations, history, relationships, uncertainty and reasoning to each object and the 22 independent dimensions.
4. Deduplicate semantic facts and correlated/syndicated sources before counting. Sample every material conclusion plus a stratified sample of other facts to verify linkage, relevance, provenance and freshness.
5. Trace every insight/recommendation backwards to packaged facts/evidence and forwards to decisions. Attempt without web access; record `not displayed`, `not addressable`, and `absent` distinctly.
6. Apply deficiency codes and assess commercial/decision impact. Validate every claimed evidence-exhaustion record against its mandatory fields.
7. Report each dimension separately (`pass|conditional|fail|NA`); do not compute a headline percentage.
8. Independently review tiering and promotion gates. A package-valid-but-shallow finding blocks Reference Twin status.

## 2. Required checklist

### Scope, tiering and real substance

- [ ] Does RET contain structured detail rather than principally summaries, documents or links?
- [ ] Are target decisions, industry boundary, research period, effective date and materiality criteria explicit?
- [ ] Which Enterprises are T1/T2/T3, and does each rationale reflect decision/material/ecosystem significance rather than current research depth?
- [ ] Which Market Participants/suppliers are material enough for full dossiers, including incumbents and likely recommendations?
- [ ] Are any material objects omitted or demoted to disguise shallow research?
- [ ] Which objects are present but shallow against the Density Matrix?

### Industry and Enterprise

- [ ] Are industry definition, subsectors, value chain, economics, drivers, structure, geography, regulation, technology, customer/supply dynamics, risks/scenarios and history evidenced?
- [ ] For each T1 Enterprise, assess all 14 dossier dimensions and identify missing facts, observations and evidence—not only empty files/fields.
- [ ] Which comparable financial periods, segments/geographies, cash/balance-sheet/capex/R&D/debt/guidance/operating metrics are missing or not primary-sourced?
- [ ] Are annual reports complemented by filings/results/presentations/calls and commercially relevant interpretation?
- [ ] Which procurement routes, buying centres, approval signals, cycles/renewals, incumbents and access constraints remain unknown?
- [ ] Which transformations lack owners, state/history, milestones, partners, dependencies, benefits/risks or implications?

### Participants, capabilities and offers

- [ ] Does every material participant have corporate, capability/offer, commercial, technology/AI, delivery ecosystem, competition, customer proof, news/analyst and reasoning coverage?
- [ ] Which supplier capabilities/offers are missing, name-only, weakly evidenced, stale or insufficiently detailed to test transformation and buyer fit?
- [ ] Can RET answer which suppliers have sector proof, required ecosystem, missing capabilities, recent suitability changes and incumbent/access effects?
- [ ] Are provider claims distinguished from customer, contract and independent analyst evidence?

### News, analyst and time

- [ ] Is news structured by event/publication date, classification, affected objects, factual statement, materiality, implications and observation/contradiction effect?
- [ ] Which months/quarters/years or material event classes in the declared period are weak for each T1 object?
- [ ] Are analyst observations attributable and lawfully packaged with position, strengths/cautions, forecast/adoption view, constraints and contradictory perspectives?
- [ ] Which relevant analyst organisations/categories/perspectives are absent, and is lawful availability documented?
- [ ] Which leadership, strategy, financial, product, M&A, partnership/contract, transformation, regulation, analyst-view, conclusion or recommendation changes are missing?
- [ ] Are current fact, history, forecast, target, expectation, inference and superseded observation distinguishable?

### Evidence, explanation and reasoning

- [ ] What proportion of material claims has adequate evidence, primary evidence where available, and independent corroboration proportional to consequence?
- [ ] Which evidence families in the Researcher source plan have not been searched, for which questions and periods?
- [ ] Which insights are summary assertions without sufficient underlying governed facts and observations?
- [ ] Can every recommendation be traversed through thesis/hypothesis/observation to packaged facts/evidence, including assumptions, counterevidence and contradictions?
- [ ] Are source authority, freshness, provenance, licence/access and dates recorded?
- [ ] Are Unknowns, evidence gaps and Contradictions linked to affected objects/decisions with materiality and review actions, rather than hidden in prose?

### Graph, package, Flora and promotion

- [ ] Which expected material relationship classes are absent? Which edges dangle, collide, duplicate identities or lack provenance/effective dates?
- [ ] Does the package contain all logical inventories in the Package Content Inventory Contract, with checksums and supersession/compatibility state?
- [ ] Can Flora address the complete required drill-down path from Industry through evidence/uncertainty/history without web reconstruction?
- [ ] Which content is merely not displayed, which is not addressable, and which is absent?
- [ ] Does every research stop claim contain a reproducible exhaustion record and recommendation-effect assessment?
- [ ] Which gaps are commercially material, which declared decisions are affected, and which deficiency codes apply?
- [ ] Do any non-waivable promotion gates fail? Which gaps block Reference Twin status?
- [ ] Has an independent reviewer challenged tiering, evidence samples, exhaustion and decision support?

## 3. Proposed RET calibration values — not doctrine

These are deliberately demanding starting hypotheses for WP1-003. RET assessment must report both observed values and quality findings. Counts never override semantic sampling, applicable-domain coverage or a blocking deficiency.

| Object / measure | T1 proposal | T2 proposal | T3 proposal | Anti-gaming/interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Industry applicable domains | ≥18 of Contract §5.1 domain groups and 100% material domains | ≥10 | identity/boundary + ≥3 | one evidenced domain assessment, not one populated field |
| T1 Enterprise dossier dimensions | ≥12/14, with all decision-material dimensions and corporate/strategy/financial/offer/uncertainty mandatory when applicable | ≥7/14 | identity, classification, provenance, key links | `NA` reviewed; prose does not populate a dimension |
| T1 Participant dimensions | ≥7/8 major groups in §5.3, all material groups | ≥4/8 | identity/type/provider role | capability, offer and ecosystem are separate depth tests |
| Distinct evidenced factual assertions | Enterprise ≥120; Participant ≥70; Industry ≥100; Capability/Offer ≥12 each | about 40% of corresponding T1 | no count minimum beyond governed identity | deduplicate semantic claims; unsupported/irrelevant count zero |
| Material-claim evidence linkage | 100% | 100% for claims used; ≥90% other material claims | no material conclusion permitted | unresolved unsupported material claim blocks regardless of rate |
| Primary sources | Enterprise ≥5 documents across ≥3 primary families; Participant ≥3 across ≥2; Industry ≥4 authoritative bodies/datasets | ≥2 relevant primary sources | identity source | unavailable source requires exhaustion; annual report alone fails diversity |
| Independent source organisations | Enterprise/Participant ≥6; Industry ≥8 | ≥3 | ≥1 | syndicated/circular sources count once |
| Source families | Enterprise ≥5; Participant ≥4; Industry ≥6 | ≥3 | ≥1 | must reflect decision-relative plan, not arbitrary variety |
| Financial time span | ≥3 comparable annual periods plus latest interim/current guidance when available | ≥2 periods if relevant | none | missing applicable period is deficient, not interpolated |
| Material news coverage | ≥24 months; Enterprise/Participant ≥8 events and Industry ≥12, or explicit zero-event search/exhaustion | ≥12 months and ≥3 events | current identity-affecting event only | counts vary by eventfulness; gap analysis by period/class controls |
| Analyst observations | ≥3 attributable records from ≥2 organisations/categories where lawful/applicable | ≥1 | none | lawful unavailability recorded; paraphrases cannot multiply one report |
| Governed observations | Enterprise ≥20; Participant ≥12; Industry ≥20; Capability/Offer ≥3 | ≥40% T1 | none | atomic, evidenced and materially distinct |
| Typed relationships | Enterprise ≥20; Participant ≥12; Capability/Offer ≥4; Industry ≥20 | ≥40% T1 | ≥1 key edge | expected-class coverage and endpoint integrity trump totals |
| Temporal depth | material changes over ≥3 years, plus older defining events where relevant | ≥18 months | current state | effective dates and prior states required; repeated current snapshots do not count |
| Freshness | ≥90% current material claims within declared object threshold; 100% decision-critical claims reviewed | ≥80% | identity reviewed at release | thresholds must be declared by volatility; freshness cannot validate false claims |
| Cross-source corroboration | ≥2 independent sources for ≥80% high-consequence non-primary/self-interested claims | ≥60% | none | primary facts may stand when authoritative; contradictions stay visible |
| Reasoning lineage | 100% recommendations and commercial theses trace to evidence | all included recommendations | no recommendation permitted | each edge must add rationale, not only IDs |
| Unknowns/Contradictions | no numeric minimum; 100% discovered material items represented | same | explicit incompleteness | zero is plausible only after independent challenge; never manufacture records |
| Evidence exhaustion | 100% relied-upon incomplete material dimensions have complete records | same | optional for known reference gaps | exhaustion does not satisfy a decision-critical evidence requirement |

### Calibration evaluation

WP1-003 must test: (a) demand—does the bar expose shallow content and improve commercial questions; (b) achievability—effort/source access needed per object; (c) discrimination—do rich and thin objects separate; (d) gaming resistance—do deduplication, source clustering and semantic samples defeat inflated counts; and (e) stability—would reasonable independent reviewers reach the same deficiencies. Recommend revised values dimension-by-dimension, never a composite score.

## 4. Required WP1-003 outputs

Produce object/tier inventory; 22-dimension results; evidence/source-family and time-period coverage maps; fact/observation/relationship semantic samples; missing participant/capability/offer list; news/analyst/financial gaps; lineage failures; Unknown/Contradiction audit; deficiency register with commercial impact; exhaustion audit; Flora offline addressability results; calibration findings; Reference Twin blockers; prioritised evidence-gap plan with owners/cost/value/review dates. Do not change RET.
