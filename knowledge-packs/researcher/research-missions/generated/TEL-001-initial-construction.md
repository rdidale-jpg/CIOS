## Composed research contract
### evidence-closure
Close a gap only with attributable evidence linked to the affected claim. Record sources searched and stop only at COMPLETE or EVIDENCE EXHAUSTED.
### analyst-estimates
Use only manifest-permitted estimate types. Label method, inputs, assumptions, range, confidence and analyst authorship; never present an estimate as observed fact.
### opportunity-timing
State timing as an evidenced window, dependencies and earliest decision trigger; never infer a date from general market momentum.
### opportunity-value
Separate evidenced value, bounded estimate and scenario. Record currency, period, basis, range and uncertainty.
### horizons
Classify Horizon 1 as current/committed, Horizon 2 as emerging/scaling, and Horizon 3 as exploratory/structural; cite evidence and triggers for every classification.
### monitoring-triggers
For unresolved or time-dependent claims specify the observable event, source, review cadence and state transition it would trigger.
### unknown-handling
Represent an Unknown explicitly with question, searches performed, reason unresolved, decision impact and next trigger. Do not fill gaps with plausible prose.
### contradiction-handling
Preserve each conflicting sourced claim and lineage. Do not silently select or average; record the resolution owner and required evidence.
### pre-delivery-validation
Validate profile IDs and versions, structured outputs, evidence lineage, Unknowns, Contradictions, estimates, horizon rules and mandatory outputs before delivery. Narrative is not a substitute for governed records.
